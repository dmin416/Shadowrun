## Falling

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Fall **> 3 meters** |
| DV | Physical = **meters fallen**, AP **−4** |
| Resist | Body + Armor |
| Soft landing | GM may reduce DV (sand, branches, etc.) |

### Fall speed

| Combat Turn of fall | Distance that Turn |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | 50 m |
| 2nd | 150 m |
| 3rd+ | 200 m (terminal ~200 m/Turn) |

## Fatigue (general)

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Type | **Stun** damage |
| Resist | Body + Willpower (**no** Armor) |
| Heal ban | Cannot heal while the causing condition continues |

DV usually starts at **1S** and increases each interval (+1S cumulative pattern as with consecutive sprints).

## Fatigue from Running

| Activity | When fatigue hits |
| --- | --- |
| Sprint consecutive Action Phases / Combat Turns | Cumulative **1S**, then **2S**, **3S**… each consecutive Sprint |
| Running (not sprint) | Every **3 minutes** |

## Fatigue from Environments

Hot/cold/humid/dry/polluted/irradiated, etc. DV starts 1S and escalates each interval. Unconscious in the hazard → damage **keeps coming**.

| Severity | Interval |
| --- | --- |
| Mild | 6 hours |
| Moderate | 3 hours |
| Harsh | 60 minutes |
| Extreme | 1 minute (10 Combat Turns) |
| Deadly | 6 seconds (2 Combat Turns) |

---

## Hunger, Thirst, Sleep

After **24 hours** without food, (non-alcoholic) drink, or sleep, risk fatigue. Starts 1S; escalates each interval. Stops when you eat / drink / sleep respectively.

| Need | Interval after the 24h gate |
| --- | --- |
| Hunger | 6 days |
| Thirst | 2 days |
| Sleep | 3 hours |

## Environmental severity (*Run & Gun*)

Core Fatigue intervals (Mild 6 h / Moderate 3 h / Harsh 60 min / Extreme 1 min / Deadly 6 s) still apply unless RnG specifies alternate timing for that hazard. Unconscious in hazard: damage continues. No healing while exposure continues.

### Heat bands

| Severity | Temp range | Typical survival (avg metahuman, unprotected) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mild | 32-38 C (90-100 F) | Fatigue after ~18 h; unconscious ~36 h; death ~60 h |
| Moderate | 38-43 C (100-110 F) | Conscious ~18 h; death ~30 h |
| Harsh | 43-55 C (110-130 F) | Conscious ~6 h; death ~10 h |
| Extreme | 55-71 C (130-160 F) | Conscious ~6 min; death ~10 min |
| Deadly | >71 C (160 F) | Conscious ~40 s; death ~1 min |

**Dehydration (heat):** after initial delay, Fatigue Tests on interval; half liter water restarts timers.

| Severity | Delay before thirst Fatigue | Interval after delay | Unconscious interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mild | 8 h | 6 h | 12 h |
| Moderate | 4 h | 3 h | 8 h |
| Harsh | 2 h | 1.5 h | 6 h |

Modifiers: humidity high/low, sunburn, clothing, armor (see RnG Environmental Test Modifiers table). **Radiant heat:** exposed skin resists escalating DV starting 1S each Combat Turn near lava, open flame, etc.

### Cold bands

| Severity | Temp range | Typical survival |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mild | 10 to -1 C (50 to 30 F) | Fatigue ~18 h; unconscious ~36 h; death ~60 h (jacket + long pants doubles intervals) |
| Moderate | -2 to -13 C (30 to 10 F) | Conscious ~18 h; death ~30 h (jacket doubles intervals) |
| Harsh | -14 to -39 C (10 to -40 F) | Conscious ~6 h; death ~10 h |
| Extreme | -40 to -72 C (-40 to -100 F) | Conscious ~6 min; death ~10 min |
| Deadly | < -73 C (-100 F) | Conscious ~40 s; death ~1 min |

**Frostbite** (Moderate+ cold): separate damage track; cap 4 boxes per monitor; overflow converts Stun↔Physical 2:1 or 1:2; -2 dice on extremity actions after first box; -2 Agi for Movement at first Physical box. Gloves +1 resist (+insulated extra).

| Severity | Frostbite DV | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moderate | 1S | 30 min |
| Harsh | 2S | 10 min |
| Extreme | 1P | 1 min |
| Deadly | 2P | 2 Combat Turns |

**Cold substances (examples):** inverted aerosol 2P AP+2; metal below 0 C 4P AP+2; liquids -50 to -100 C 12P AP-2; below -200 C 28P AP-6.

### Pollution bands

Fatigue uses GM-set toxicity; narrative timing:

| Severity | Effect (avg metahuman) |
| --- | --- |
| Mild | OK ~18 h; incapacitate ~36 h; death ~60 h continuous |
| Moderate | Incapacitate ~18 h; death ~30 h |
| Harsh | Wheezing hours; down ~5 h; death ~10 h |
| Extreme | Pain ~3 min; unconscious ~6 min; death ~10 min |
| Deadly | Unconscious ~30 s; death ~1 min |

Long-term exposure → **Bighted** negative quality (RnG p. 169).

### Radiation bands

Uses Fatigue and/or **Nausea** (Core p. 409). Harsh+ leaves **contamination aura** (10 m) for 24 h (severity carried on victim; persists on corpse).

| Severity | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Mild | ~24 h: Nausea Power 2, +1 Power / 12 h |
| Moderate | ~18 h: Nausea Power 3, +1 / 6 h; unconscious ~72 h without care |
| Harsh | Debilitate ~6 h; death ~10 h; carries Mild aura 24 h |
| Extreme | Survive ~10 min; carries Moderate 24 h + Mild 24 h |
| Deadly | Unconscious ~40 s; death ~1 min; carries Harsh 24 h + Mild 24 h |

No healing while still in polluted/irradiated zone (including self-generated radiation field). Magical healing allowed but pulls healer into field.

## Underwater cold (*Run & Gun*)

Water pulls heat ~25x faster than air. Below 10 C while soaked: same Fatigue rules; drying clothes may need Intuition + Survival (1).

| Severity | Water temp | Interval | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mild | 22-17 C | 1 h | ~10 h to hypothermia; pass out ~6 h |
| Moderate | 16-11 C | 30 min | ~6 h survival; pass out ~4 h |
| Harsh | 10-6 C | 6 min | ~40 min shivering; pass out ~1 h |
| Extreme | 5 to -2 C | 1 min | Titanic-class: ~10 min max; many fail ~6 min |

**Underwater combat:** -2 all Attack Actions; ranged -1 DV per meter water (non-aquatic guns cannot fire submerged unless modified). Melee STR halved for DV (grapple/clinch full STR). Explosions: see RnG Underwater Explosives table.

**Diving decompression:** Intuition + Diving (Depth/10) shallow; failure tables for burst capillaries, DCS, narcosis, embolism, kraken at depth 300 m+.

## Space and variable gravity (*Run & Gun*)

**Vacuum / unprotected space:** treat as **Deadly** immediately (Core intervals); no graded Fatigue bands.

**Matrix lag:** orbital hacking requires local access; distance adds Noise beyond normal elimination.

### Space combat modifiers (summary)

| Gravity | Physical-action dice | Melee DV | Ranged DV | Defense | Recoil |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zero-G | -2 | STR halved (swing); grapple/strapped normal | Half DV (quarter if one anchored; none if both) | -2 unless anchored | x2 |
| Low-G | -1 | STR -25% rounded up | -1 DV | +1 | x2 unless anchored |
| Heavy-G | -1 | STR half for strikes | -1 attack dice | -1 | halved |

**Movement (Walk/Run/Sprint):** Zero-G climb/jump only (+2 climb); Low-G Walk=Agi, Run=Agi x2, Sprint +3 m/hit; Heavy-G Walk=Str, Run=Str x2, Sprint +0.5 m/hit.

**Falling DV:** Zero-G none (impact at bad angle = Stun = Movement before hit); Low-G from 4 m, DV = half meters; Heavy-G from 2 m, DV = 2x meters. Terminal velocity rates differ by gravity (RnG p. 168).

**Range multipliers:** see RnG Range in Space / Throwing Weapons Range in Space tables (Zero-G extends range; artificial gravity shortens).

Full spell/spirit underwater and space notes: RnG Staying Alive §Magic of the Sea / space section.
