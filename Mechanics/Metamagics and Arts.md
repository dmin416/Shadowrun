## Initiation framework

When initiating into an **art**, learn **one** available ritual, enchantment, or metamagic free (prerequisites apply).

| Category | Learn cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rituals (school) | 5 Karma, normal time | Initiates of that school only; others may assist if leader knows it |
| Enhancements (adept) | 2 Karma, 1 day | Must have base power; Way-locked teaching |
| Enchantments (school) | 5 Karma | User must also have initiated into school |
| Metamagics (school) | **New initiate grade each** | Full initiation Karma/time per metamagic |

### Initiation costs and discounts

| Type | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Base | 10 + (Grade x 3) Karma |
| Group initiation | -10% (need Group Bond) |
| Initiatory ordeal | -10% |
| Schooling | -10% (10,000¥, 5 weeks) |
| Combined max | -30% |

Only one ordeal and/or one group per initiation; choose before the rite.

## High Arts

| Art | Typical first learn | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Geomancy** | Aspect Mana Line | Aspect Mana Line, Recharge Reagents, Manifest Sha, Ley Sight |
| **Necromancy** | Grave Binding | Grave Binding, Death Mark, Blood Sight; Mortis Optigram, Hand of Glory |
| **Psychometry** | Sympathetic Linking | Symbolic Link, Mystic Restraints; Sympathetic Linking, Psychometry |
| **Divination** | Augury and Sortilege | Danger Sense |

**Sympathetic / Symbolic linking:** Sympathetic in Material Link ritual: +2 Force to Seal oppose; Symbolic: +4.

**Psychometry:** Assensing + Intuition + grade [Astral]; duration GM/1D6 minutes. Modifiers by age, violence, Smudging, etc.

**Danger Sense (mage):** As adept Danger Sense; levels = initiate grade; does not stack (use higher).

## Defensive Arts (Apotropaic)

| Path | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Shielding | SR5 p. 326 (first) |
| Absorption | Prereq Shielding |
| Reflection | Prereq Shielding; Reflect / Deflect / Greater Reflection |

**Absorption:** Interrupt -10 Init. Magic + Counterspelling [Force of spell]. Each hit: -1 Force of spell + 1 mana charge. Keep charges 1+grade CT, max Magic charges. Next spell: -1 Drain/charge (min 2). Expire unused: Drain = remaining charges.

**Reflect Spell:** Interrupt -7 Init. Counterspelling+Magic [Astral] vs Spellcasting. More hits: spell to caster at half Force; resist Drain as Reckless (+3) at half Force.

**Deflect Spell:** Interrupt -5 Init, -1 Edge. Indirect only; each net hit +1 scatter die.

**Greater Reflection:** Interrupt -10 Init, -1 Edge. As Reflect at full Force and/or full area on caster.

**Quickening art:** Quickening (SR5 p. 326); Anchoring (prereq Quickening); Trap Ward.

**Anchoring:** Any duration spell; any prep trigger (incl. BGC / Detection). Pay Karma = Force. Max active anchors = grade. On trigger: release with recorded hits.

### Toxic Magic

**Tradition shift:** Abandons prior tradition for Toxic Tradition: choose any **five** toxic spirits to summon + any Mental Attribute (+ Willpower) for Drain.

**Twisted Arts (toxic magicians):** Spells Pollutant Stream/Wave, Radiation Beam/Burst; metamagics **Corruption**, **Taint**; ritual **Leeching**.

**Corruption:** Banishing Test with +initiate grade; if Summoning then succeeds after services reduced to 0, spirit becomes toxic version.

**Toxic adepts:** Toxic Strike, Plague Cloud (*SG* adept powers).

**Sites:** BGC typically 1-6, always toxic Domains; lodges often on polluted sites.

#### Toxic mentor spirits (selected)

| Mentor | All | Magician | Adept | Disadvantage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Doom | +2 Demolitions or one Combat skill | +2 Combat spells/preps/rituals | Free Killing Hands | Wil+Cha(3) to avoid/stop a fight once begun |
| Disease | +2 resist pathogens/toxins | +2 summon/bind plague spirits | Free Plague Cloud | Cha+Wil(3) to not flee unless outnumbering foes |
| Mutation | +2 one Physical skill | +2 Health spells/preps/rituals | Free Attribute Boost 2 (one Physical) | -1 all actions if inferior to opponent until gap closed |
| Pollution | +2 Chemistry (pollutants; may default) | +2 summon/bind toxic air, earth, or water (pick one) | Free Toxic Strike | In cleaner areas: Wil+Cha(3) daily or spend >=8h despoiling |

### Blood Magic

- Requires **Sacrifice** (cannot self-teach; same-tradition teacher or spirit).
- Aura reads strongly negative to most assensing.

#### Metamagic: Sacrifice

1. Inflict Physical damage with a **melee weapon that draws blood**.
2. Melee attack; restrained/unable to defend: unopposed **+4 dice**.
3. **Blood Magic Points (BMP):**
   - Non-sapient or **willing** victim (incl. self): **1 BMP per 3** Physical boxes
   - Sapient critter or **unwilling** metahuman: **1 BMP per 1** box
   - Spirits never valid (even possessing)
4. Unused BMP lost after the following Combat Turn.
5. Spend BMP (any mix):
   - +1 Force of spell per point (may exceed 2x Magic)
   - +1 effective Magic for Drain Stun vs Physical threshold per point
   - -1 Drain per point (min 0)
   - Store in athame
   - Summon blood spirit (BMP generated = max Force)

#### Related metamagics (*Forbidden Arcana* also)

| Metamagic | Prereq | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cannibalize | (adept) | BMP as Sacrifice; 3 BMP -> +1 Physical Attribute (Simple); duration Magic x grade CT; Karma = points raised -> days |
| Power Bleed | Cannibalize | 3 BMP -> siphon one critter/adept power from victim |
| Predator Feast | Cannibalize | Regeneration Magic x Init CT; 3 Karma -> days |
| Soul Tether | Sacrifice | Sustain spell with BMP from kill; -1 Force/hour |
| Spiritual Sacrifice | Sacrifice | Sacrifice possessed hosts / ally flesh / loa-shedim hosts |

#### Athame (blood focus)

Bond requires Sacrifice. Stores BMP from **Blood Rite**; Free Action spend as Sacrifice when casting. Capacity = Force. Decay: after 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, **half** unused BMP lost each interval.

#### Blood rituals

Leader with Sacrifice may spend BMP **1:1 as reagent drams** (Give the Offering). **Blood Rite** stores BMP into bonded athames.

**Blood Spirit Invocation:** Sacrifice + Invocation + **metahuman victim killed** for great-form ascension. Optional unique power **Hemorrhage** (Complex, [Magic] m, Magic+Wil [Force] vs Str+Wil per target; net hits = unresisted Physical + Nausea).

Blood spirit stat block: *Street Grimoire* p. 90.

### Insect spirits (key procedures)

**Summoning / Inhabitation**

- Need metahuman agent initially; without vessel, summoned insect fades in hours (Evanescence).
- Prepare vessel for Inhabitation (*SG* p. 195); cocoon by Queen/Mother, shaman, or ally.
- Placing spirit in host stops Evanescence.
- Shaman may add **Binding** to either Inhabitation pool; Queen/Mother may add **Force** to spirit or vessel pool.

**Insect shamans**

- Contact insect mentor of one type; abandon prior tradition; summon only that insect's spirits.
- Mentor gives **no** numeric bonuses/penalties.
- Build hive; hive insects work obsessively toward Queen.

**Insect Spirit Tradition**

| Role | Spirit |
| --- | --- |
| Combat | Soldier |
| Detection | Scout |
| Health | Caretaker |
| Illusion | Nymph |
| Manipulation | Worker |
| Drain | Willpower + Intuition |

**Queen / Mother summoning**

1. Know **Invocation**; lodge Force >= desired Force.
2. Create spirit formula as for ally (*SG* p. 200).
3. Transform honored **nymph** via normal Summoning; becomes Queen/Mother.
4. On success: automatically **free spirit**; hive focus; gains **Astral Gateway** (no longer needs shaman to summon).
5. May spirit-pact; shaman summons only **one** Queen/Mother ever.
6. Disrupted: returns home; same process to return.
7. **Destroyed:** shaman Magic -> **0** (cannot raise); true-forms destroyed; flesh/hybrid forms lose cohesion.

Spirit type stats (Nymph, Scout, Caretaker, Soldier, Worker, Queen/Mother): *Street Grimoire* p. 96-98. Breed subtypes: [Magic Supplemental](Magic%20Supplemental.md) (*Dark Terrors*).

### Shedim (key procedures)

- Possess **dead or abandoned** vessels; hate life; Evanescence weakness.
- **Master shedim:** host memories; aspected magicians (Sorcery); **Astral Gateway** to summon more.

**Combat (*Dark Terrors* builds on *SG*)**

- Astral form: spectral jellyfish; Energy Drain shows tentacles.
- Assensing: host looks gray ghost; peering invites Astral Combat; Aura Masking can mask shell.
- Physical Attributes use inhabited body's metatype adjustments.
- All damage (incl. mana spells) hits **body first**; fill Physical CM + Overflow, then Regeneration check; spirit forced to astral.
- Then destroy on astral (Astral Combat, weapon foci, mana spells) or it flees.
- **Banishing:** start with services = Force; no summoner bonus. After ejection, Banisher keeps link and may Banish again next Action even if fled.

**Master ritual powers (*Dark Terrors*):** Anchored Resident, Shell Game (hop Force x 100 m), Superior Banishing Resistance, Superior Regeneration, Wounded Fury. Variant types (Blade-Summoned, Hoppers, Unbreakables, Horror Shows): [Magic Supplemental](Magic%20Supplemental.md).

Base **Shedim** / **Master Shedim** stat blocks: *Street Grimoire* p. 93-94.
