# Healing and Injuries

## Tracks reminder

- **Stun** and **Physical** monitors fill independently.
- Wound modifiers: −1 per 3 boxes on *each* track (stack).
- Physical full → overflow → death if untreated.

## First Aid

- **First Aid + Logic** (Paramedic specialization helps).
- Must start within a short window after injury (Core timing).
- Heals boxes based on hits; limited uses per patient per set of injuries.
- Medkit rating often adds dice / acts as tool limit.

## Medicine & clinical care

- Longer-term Physical healing uses **Medicine** skill and facilities.
- Hospitalized lifestyle / clinic quality changes healing rate and cost.
- Surgery for implants or critical trauma — extended care, Essence risks for new ware.

## Magical healing

- **Heal** spell and similar magic convert hits to boxes healed.
- Drain applies; Force and net hits matter.
- Cannot usually stack infinite Heal on the same damage without rules limits.
- Magical healing and First Aid interaction: follow Core stacking limits (typically one effective magical heal pass per damage set, etc.).

## Natural recovery

- Stun heals relatively quickly with rest.
- Physical heals slowly per day based on Body / care level / lifestyle.
- Poor lifestyle and unfinished fights slow recovery.

## Stabilization

Dying characters need **First Aid / Medicine / magical Stabilize** to stop overflow progression. Edge and GM fiat may allow “Not Dead Yet” moments.

## Toxins, diseases, pathogens

- Rated with Power, Effect, Speed.
- Resistance often Body + toxin resistance ware / spells.
- Antidotes and treatment change stages.

## Permanent consequences

Optional: scars, lasting negative qualities, lost limbs (cyber replacements), psychological trauma. Use when it serves the story.
