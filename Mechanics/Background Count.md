# Background Count

Agent reference (SR5). Full **background count** rules from *Street Grimoire*; astral phenomena that modify magic where you stand.

**Verified from:** `Source Texts Condensed/Street Grimoire Condensed.md` (Ch. 05 Magic in the World)  
**See also:** [Magic](Magic.md) · [Critter and Spirit Powers](Critter%20and%20Spirit%20Powers.md) · *Aetherology* (regional phenomena brief in [Magic](Magic.md#aetherology-phenomena))

**Scope:** Scale, dice/Limit effects, acclimation/alignment, domain examples, mana ebbs/voids/foveae/warps/lines (play rules)  
**Out of scope:** Full SG spell/ritual catalogs; toxic/Insect-specific domains (see *Street Grimoire* Dark Magic)

## Scale

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **-24 to +24** | Background Count (BGC) rating |
| **0** | Normal mana |
| Negative | Mana **ebbs** and **voids** (low mana) |
| Positive | Aspected **domains** and **warps** (charged mana) |

Use the **absolute value** of the rating for magnitude of effects unless a rule says otherwise.

---

## Dice and magic effects

Anyone using magic in an area with nonzero BGC:

1. **-Rating** dice pool to all **magic-linked tests** (Spellcasting, Summoning, skill tests using active adept powers such as Killing Hands or Improved Sense).
2. **Domain exception:** If the tradition, person, skill group, or skill is **aligned** to the domain aspect, that use may be exempt from the penalty (GM: aspect must match).
3. **Dual-natured / purely astral beings:** **-Rating** to all actions (domain may exempt spirit/creature types).
4. **Active magic already running:** Sustained spells, quickened/anchored spells, rituals, and pre-existing foci: Force/effectiveness reduced by Rating. At **0 or less:** spells fizzle; wards and rituals collapse; foci deactivate.
5. **Foci cannot activate** while under the background count.
6. **Anchored / quickened recovery:** After leaving the count (or as count allows), revive at **+1 Force per hour** up to preexisting Force.
7. **Preparations triggered in BGC:** **Potency immediately reduced by the Rating**.
8. **Adepts:** Simple Action to turn a passive power on/off when BGC penalties would exceed power bonuses.
9. **Assensing, Astral Perception, Astral Combat:** **-Rating** to associated tests.
10. **Astral tracking:** Through an area where BGC **> target's Force or Magic**, lose the astral link at that point.

## Dangerous power (|Rating| >= 12)

Anyone magically or astrally active (dual natured, astrally perceiving, casting, active adept power, etc.) takes **(Background Count - 12) Stun Damage** each Combat Turn. Resist with a **Willpower** Test.

## Acclimated

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Effect | Ignore BGC penalties for magic use; **no** domain Limit bonus |
| Time | Rough guideline: **1 week exposure per Rating point** (BGC often changes first) |
| Home Ground (*SR5* p. 74) | Ignore customary local BGC; count only **half** of temporary-event BGC |

Example: Barrens customary 1 ignored; riot raises BGC to 4 → temporary portion halved by 2 → effective temporary contribution 2.

---

## Alignment

Mesh with the domain aspect: BGC Rating applies as a **positive modifier to the Limit** of aligned skill(s).

Example: Wiccan conjures Force 4 spirit in Hag Bolette ring (Rating 3, Wiccan-aligned): Conjuring limit **7** (4 + 3).

Switching tradition/practice to match a domain is possible but a long process. Some domains have no alignable aspect: acclimate only.

## Astral phenomena (BGC-related)

### Mana ebbs

Reduced mana; Rating typically **-1 to -12**. Astral/dual forms uncomfortable but can endure (unlike voids).

Sample: Crater Lake, Tír Tairngire: ebb **-7**.

### Mana voids

Mana loses cohesion; Rating typically **-13 to -20**. Size ~500 m² to ~4 km diameter. Outer space is a void.

Sample: Cattenom Void (SOX): ~6 km dia; Rating **-20**; reagents instantly drained; foci left **Force hours** permanently destroyed.

### Foveae (moving voids)

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Nature | Invisible astral vortex; BG typically **-7 to -12**; height ~ Rating km |
| Reagents after pass | Treat Rating as **weeks** for area to recover (*SR5* p. 317) |
| Preparations inside | Potency **-1 every half hour** (fixated: every **6 hours**) |
| Duration | <= 24 hours; Force **-1 every 2 hours**; below Force 4: stops, becomes temporary ebb (+1 Force / 2 hours) |

### Mana warps

Chaotic mana; **cannot** be aspected, aligned, or acclimated. Can spawn mana storms. Positive **>=16** or cited band **-16 to -20**. Astral exposure damages as high BGC; casting triggers **mana surge**.

Sample: Blackstone Prison (Sioux): warp ~1 km dia, Rating 16.

### Mana lines

Concentrations flowing across the astral. Ratings **1-15**. Aspecting via ritual magic (*Street Grimoire* Geomancy). Spell-like line effects: dice pool **Rating x 2**, Limit **Rating**.

## Cleansing and related (pointer)

**Cleansing metamagic** (*Street Grimoire*): temporarily reduce positive BGC. Rituals: Smudging, Circle of Cleansing, Light of Dharma. See [Metamagics and Arts](Metamagics%20and%20Arts.md).

**Mana Static** spell: +1 BGC/hit (permanent recedes 1/hour). **Mana Flow / Mana Ebb** rituals (*Shadow Spells*): shift local count; see [Magic](Magic.md#shadow-spells-rituals).
