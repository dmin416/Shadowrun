# Magic Basics

Awakened characters manipulate mana through **Sorcery**, **Conjuring**, and **Enchanting**.

## Spellcasting

1. Choose spell & **Force**.
2. Roll **Spellcasting + Magic** ([limit] Force).
3. Resist **Drain** with tradition attributes (e.g., Willpower + Logic Hermetic; Willpower + Charisma Shamanic).
4. Apply effects; net hits often improve spell.

**Overcasting** (Force > Magic) risks Physical Drain.

## Spell categories

- Combat, Detection, Health, Illusion, Manipulation
- Direct vs Indirect combat spells use different defense / resistance patterns

## Conjuring

- **Summon** spirit of a type your tradition allows; Force vs opposing spirit Force.
- Services owed = net hits (typically).
- **Bind** for longer control (expensive, risky).
- Spirits resist with Force; summoner takes Drain.

## Astral

- **Astral Perception** - dual-natured while perceiving; see auras.
- **Astral Projection** - leave body; time limits; astral combat possible.
- Wards, barriers, and backgrounds affect magic.

## Adepts

Spend Power Points on physical/mental powers (Improved Reflexes, Critical Strike, etc.). No spell Drain, but Magic still drops with Essence loss.

## Foci & reagents

- Foci add dice / sustain spells; must be bonded with Karma.
- Reagents power rituals, lodge creation, and some edge cases.

## Background count

Mana pollution / domains penalize Magic tests. Toxic and alchera weirdness escalate this (*Street Grimoire*, etc.).
