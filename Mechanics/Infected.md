- Buy **Advanced Optional** only after **at least 2 Optional Powers** (exceptions: chiron, jabberwock, lamia, nibiinaabe need no Optionals first).
- **1 Advanced Optional per 2 in-game months** (in addition to *Run Faster* lists).

## Amrita (Dread Feast)

- 250 mL own blood + exotic reagents on alcohol/fruit; sacrifice 1 Essence
- Extended Alchemy + Magic [Astral] (18, 12 hours); glitch: -1 Essence delivered per glitch; critical: fail, Essence lost
- Addiction Rating 8, Threshold 3 (Psych + Physical)
- Vector Ingestion; Speed 1 CT; Duration 7 days
- Effect: +1 Body, +1 Charisma, +1 Phys and Social Limit, High Pain Tolerance 3, Euphoria (8 - Body hours, min 1)
- Addict pawn: Regeneration + Essence Loss; dose every ~3 months typical; 1D6 Essence per dose; carry up to 2x natural Essence; bond sense (Essence in meters); creator death + broken addiction stabilizes Essence at current or natural-1, whichever less

**Renfield** (SR5 reprint): Ingest/Inject; +1 Agi/Int/Str; +1 Phys Limit; Euphoria; +1D6 Init; Addiction 8 / Thr 3; Duration 7 days.

## Dark Magic Tradition

- Drain: Charisma + Willpower
- Combat Fire / Health Water / Detection Earth / Illusion Air / Manipulation Man
- Mentor often Adversary; ego / blasphemy focus; burnout risk
- Common among vampiric covens
