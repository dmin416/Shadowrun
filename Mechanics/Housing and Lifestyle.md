# Housing and Lifestyle

**Lifestyle** abstracts rent, food, clothes, utilities, and day-to-day Matrix access.

## Lifestyle levels (SR5)

| Lifestyle | Vibe |
| --- | --- |
| **Street** | Squats, charity, crime for meals |
| **Squatter** | Abandoned buildings, unreliable power |
| **Low** | Cubicle hotels, coffin motels, shared dumps |
| **Middle** | Actual apartment, some comfort |
| **High** | Nice neighborhood, security, space |
| **Luxury** | Penthouse / mansion living |

Monthly nuyen costs escalate sharply. Prepaid months can be bought at creation with Resources.

## What Lifestyle gives you

- Assumed clothing and basic gear appropriate to the level
- A place to sleep and recover (healing rates improve with care)
- Social camouflage — High Lifestyle characters look “legit”
- Bills, neighbors, and paper trails as plot hooks

## Safehouses

Teams often pool cash for a shared **safehouse** (warehouse, basement, offshore container). Treat as a Lifestyle variant with security ratings, secrecy, and upkeep.

## Boltholes & multiples

Runners may keep a cheap public Lifestyle plus a secret crash space. Pay for what you maintain.

## Losing Lifestyle

Missed payments → eviction, street drop, or debt to scary people. SINner lifestyles may be garnished by corps/gov.

## Gear vs Lifestyle

Lifestyle does **not** replace tactical gear, weapons, cyberdecks, or vehicles. It covers living, not loadouts.
