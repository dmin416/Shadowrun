## Primary vs extras

Pick **one primary** Lifestyle. Extra flops / safehouses: pay **full** cost on each maintained lifestyle. Run Faster expands bolt-hole / safehouse packages when that book is in play ([Encyclopedia Lifestyles](../Encyclopedia/Lifestyles%20and%20Safehouses.md)).

## Lifestyle Options

Not for Streets or Hospitalized. GM may disallow if implausible.

| Option | Effect | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Special Work Area | Workshop/garage/studio; relevant skill Limits **+2** there | +1,000¥ / month |
| Extra Secure | HTR / security response rolls one level better | +20% of lifestyle |
| Obscure / Difficult to Find | Others' Sneaking near residence **-2** (owner excluded) | +10% of lifestyle |
| Cramped | Logic-linked skill Limits **-2** (min 1) while there | -10% of lifestyle |
| Dangerous Area | HTR/security response one level worse | -20% of lifestyle |

## Paying the bills

Prepay months OK; refunds unlikely.

Miss a payment → roll **1D6**. If result **>** consecutive months missed → landlord slides (still owe). If result **≤** months missed → lose the unit. Non-primary: unit gone. **Primary:** downgrade lifestyle **one tier** (repos/eviction); GM may leave debt to bank or loan shark.

---

## Buying a Lifestyle (permanent)

Pay **100 × monthly** once (investments/trust). Example: Luxury permanent = 10,000,000¥ at 100k/month.

Can still lose it to plot (hacked investments, blown property). Selling Middle+: with time for legit deal roll **2D6 × 10%** of purchase price (SINless or dump-fast: **1D6 × 10%**).

---

## Team Lifestyles

Joint home: **+10% per additional person**. Low or better: one **tenant of record** holds the debt if payments fail.

## Starting Nuyen (chargen)

≤5,000¥ gear leftover may carry over. Rest from lifestyle dice:

| Lifestyle | Starting Nuyen |
| --- | --- |
| Street | 1D6 × 20¥ |
| Squatter | 2D6 × 40¥ |
| Low | 3D6 × 60¥ |
| Middle | 4D6 × 100¥ |
| High | 5D6 × 500¥ |
| Luxury | 6D6 × 1,000¥ |

Buy months of lifestyle with Resources at chargen ([Resources and Gear](Character%20Creation/Resources%20and%20Gear.md)).
