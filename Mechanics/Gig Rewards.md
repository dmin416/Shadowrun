# Gig Rewards

Runs pay in **nuyen**, **Karma**, and sometimes **reputation / gear / favors**.

## Nuyen

- Negotiated with **Mr. Johnson** using Negotiation (and leverage).
- Typical pay scales with risk, opposition, and sprawl (GM sets baseline).
- Extras: hazard pay, silence bonuses, live-capture bonuses, expenses covered or not.
- Cut for fixers, fences, and team shares - decide before the run.

### What money is for

- Replace ammo, destroyed drones, burnt fake SINs
- Lifestyle payments
- New gear (Availability still applies in play, with contact help)
- Bribes and favors

## Karma

Awarded for:

- Completing objectives
- Good roleplay / teamwork
- Surviving hard opposition
- Personal goals / complications

### Spending Karma (advancement)

| Advance | Cost idea |
| --- | --- |
| Raise skill | Higher as rating climbs |
| Raise attribute | Expensive; respect racial max |
| New specialization | Flat cost |
| New quality / buy off negative | Listed costs |
| Initiate / submerse | Grade-based |
| Spells / complex forms / powers | Flat or Force-related |

Exact costs are on Core Karma tables.

## Reputation

Track as needed:

| Stat | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Street Cred** | Reputation among runners / shadows |
| **Notoriety** | Bad reputation, fear, or unreliability |
| **Public Awareness** | How much the public / media knows your face |

High Cred helps negotiations and contacts; Notoriety and Awareness bring heat.

## Non-cash rewards

- Gear gifts (ware, foci, vehicles)
- Corp script / rippers / unusual currency
- Information, blackmail material
- Safehouse access, SIN upgrades
- Spirit services, sprite registration help

## GM pacing tip

Pay enough that runners can maintain Lifestyle and replace losses, but not so much that Resources priority becomes meaningless after two gigs.
