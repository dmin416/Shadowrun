# Initiative

## Rolling

**Initiative Score** = (Reaction + Intuition + modifiers) + Initiative Dice results.

Base Initiative Dice = **1D6**. Extra dice from:

- Wired Reflexes / Reaction Enhancers (stacking rules apply)
- Adept Increase Reflexes
- Drugs / spells (Increase Reflexes, etc.)
- Matrix / VR cold-sim / hot-sim bonuses for deckers
- Vehicle speeds when applicable

## Order

- Act from **highest Initiative Score to lowest**.
- Ties: compare Edge, then Reaction, then Intuition (Core tiebreakers), or roll off.

## Initiative Passes

After everyone with a positive score has acted once:

1. Subtract **10** from each Initiative Score.
2. Anyone still above 0 acts again in the new order.
3. Repeat until all scores are 0 or less.

High Initiative Dice characters often get **multiple passes** — the core advantage of wired samurai and hot-sim deckers.

## Changing Initiative mid-fight

- Delay / change of action options exist.
- Seizing Initiative with Edge jumps you to the top for that pass.
- Surprised characters may get no actions on early passes — see [Surprise](Surprise.md).

## Astral / Matrix / Rigging

Different forms change Initiative attributes and dice. Use the Initiative for the form you’re currently operating in.
