# Called Shots and Special Combat Options

## Called shots

Trade dice penalties (or DV shifts) for specific effects:

| Intent | Typical outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **Bypass armor** | Hit an unarmored / weakly armored location |
| **Vitals** | Extra DV / nasty wound |
| **Disable limb** | Arm / leg impairment |
| **Weapon** | Knock or damage enemy gear |
| **Blast out of hand** | Disarm |
| **Shake up** | Force status / distraction |

Exact modifiers live on Core / *Run & Gun* called shot tables. Always declare before the roll.

## Take Aim

Simple Action: improve Accuracy / dice for a subsequent shot (stacking limits apply). Breaks if you take damage or change targets carelessly.

## Full Defense

Interrupt: boost defense; you sacrifice offense until your next action phase.

## Suppression & covering fire

Full Auto and some BF options force defenders to keep their heads down — movement and actions restricted while suppressed.

## Knockdown & grappling

Net hits / DV vs Body thresholds can knock prone. Clinch, subduing, and hold actions use Unarmed / special rules.

## Fire, toxins, drugs in combat

Onset times matter — a narcoject may not drop someone before they shoot back. Track stages.

## Vehicles & passengers

Attacking tires, windows, drivers — treat as called shots / vehicle damage. See [Vehicles](../Vehicles.md).
