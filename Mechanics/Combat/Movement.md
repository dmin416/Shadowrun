# Movement

## Per Combat Turn

Characters get a **Free Movement** allotment based on Agility / metatype walking rate (Core Movement Rate table).

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Walk** | Use walking rate; usually no penalty |
| **Run** | Use running rate; often −2 to attacks / some tests |
| **Sprint** | Complex Action; Athletics + Strength test for extra meters |

Trolls, dwarfs, and critters use their own rates. Agility augmentations can improve movement.

## Vertical & special movement

- Climbing, swimming, free fall — Athletics tests + rates
- Jumping — Strength / Athletics
- Difficult terrain halves or quarters movement at GM discretion

## Prone

Dropping prone is usually Free; standing often Simple. Prone affects ranged defense and melee differently (harder to shoot, vulnerable up close).

## Vehicles

Vehicle movement uses Pilot skill, Speed, Handling — see [Vehicles](../Vehicles.md). On foot movement rarely applies inside a moving vehicle unless climbing on it like a maniac.
