# Surprise

When one side is unaware of the other at the start of hostilities:

## Surprise test

Typically **Reaction + Intuition** (± Ambush modifiers, perception prep, etc.) opposed or against a threshold set by the situation.

## Effects

- Surprised characters often **cannot act** on the first Initiative Pass (or suffer equivalent restriction).
- They may also face defense penalties until they recover.
- Ambushers act normally and frequently get a devastating opening volley.

## Avoiding surprise

- Good Perception / sensors / spirits / drones on overwatch
- Expecting trouble (ready weapons, declared overwatch)
- Astral / Matrix overwatch catching the setup

## After the opener

Once surprised characters have had a chance to react (per Core), combat proceeds with normal Initiative. Don’t forget Glitches on sneaky approaches — critical glitches announce the team early.
