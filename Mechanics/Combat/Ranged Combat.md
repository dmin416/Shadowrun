# Ranged Combat

## Attack test

**Dice pool:** Weapon skill + Agility ± modifiers  
**Limit:** Weapon **Accuracy** (modified by Take Aim, laser sight, smartlink, etc.)

Opposed by defender’s **Reaction + Intuition** (± modifiers), or Interrupt defense.

**Net hits** add to Damage Value.

## Fire modes (summary)

| Mode | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **SS** Single Shot | Simple Action; no recoil accumulation that shot |
| **SA** Semi-Auto | Simple Action; recoil applies across shots |
| **BF** Burst Fire | Simple Action; multiple bullets, recoil, defense mods |
| **FA** Full Auto | Complex Action; heavy recoil, suppression options |

Exact bullet counts and defense penalties are on weapon profiles / Core tables.

## Recoil

Uncompensated recoil penalizes subsequent shots. Recoil compensation from strength, gyromounts, shock pads, underbarrels, etc., reduces the penalty.

## Range & environmental mods

Apply dice penalties for:

- Range brackets (short / medium / long / extreme)
- Partial / good / full cover (usually help defender)
- Visibility, wind, running, wounds, dual wielding

## Defending

- Standard defense: Reaction + Intuition
- Full Defense: add Willpower (Interrupt)
- Cover adds dice to defense
- “Cannot defend” situations (unaware, restricted) are deadly

## Damage

**Modified DV** = weapon DV + net hits  
Then resist with Body + Armor − AP — see [Damage Armor and Wounds](Damage%20Armor%20and%20Wounds.md).

## Grenades & explosives

Scatter, blast ratings, and timing use separate tables. Cook / throw / launch carefully indoors.
