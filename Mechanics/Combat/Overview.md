# Combat — Overview

Combat in SR5 is tracked in **Combat Turns** (about 3 seconds) divided into **Initiative Passes**. Characters act in Initiative order each pass.

## Flow

1. **Surprise** (if applicable) — see [Surprise](Surprise.md)
2. **Roll Initiative** — see [Initiative](Initiative.md)
3. **Initiative Pass** — highest to lowest:
   - Declare/resolve actions — see [Action Economy](Action%20Economy.md)
   - Attacks — [Ranged](Ranged%20Combat.md) / [Melee](Melee%20Combat.md)
   - Apply damage — [Damage, Armor, and Wounds](Damage%20Armor%20and%20Wounds.md)
4. Subtract 10 from Initiative Scores; anyone still positive gets another pass
5. New Combat Turn when all passes finish — roll Initiative again (unless table uses rolling once per fight)

## What you need on the sheet

- Initiative attribute & dice
- Attack dice pools (skill + attribute + mods)
- Weapon DV, AP, Accuracy, modes
- Armor rating & resistances
- Condition monitors & wound modifiers
- Edge remaining

## Design goals at the table

- Position and cover matter
- Wound penalties spiral — first aid mid-fight saves runs
- Action economy (Simple vs Complex) is the real “AP system”
- Matrix and magic participants often act on the same timeline
