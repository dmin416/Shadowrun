# Melee Combat

## Attack test

**Dice pool:** Melee skill + Agility (or Unarmed + Agility) ± modifiers  
**Limit:** Weapon Accuracy (Unarmed often uses Physical Limit)

Opposed by:

- **Reaction + Intuition** (dodge), or
- **Weapon skill + Agility** (parry / block with ready weapon / shield), or
- Unarmed block where allowed

## Reach

Weapons and trolls have **Reach**. Higher Reach gives attack or defense dice advantages per comparative Reach.

## Damage

- Weapon DV (+ net hits) or Unarmed DV based on Strength
- Some weapons use **(STR+x)P** or **S** damage
- AP applies as usual

## Charging & moving attacks

Moving into melee, charging, and attacking prone / unaware targets use situational modifiers. Intercept actions can stop someone running past.

## Multiple opponents

- Attacking several targets splits dice pools or uses specific multi-attack rules.
- Friends in melee, superior position, and cover still matter.

## Guns in melee

Firing at point-blank / engaged targets often incurs penalties; some smash / pistol-whip with club skill instead.
