# Damage, Armor, and Wounds

## Damage types

| Type | Track | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Physical (P)** | Physical monitor | Bullets, blades, many spells |
| **Stun (S)** | Stun monitor | Fists, gel rounds, tasers, drain sometimes |

If Stun overflow fills the Stun track, excess converts to Physical.

## Damage resistance

After a successful attack:

1. Start with **DV + net hits** (modified DV).
2. Defender rolls **Body + Armor + other** − attacker’s **AP** (Armor Penetration reduces armor dice).
3. Each hit reduces DV by 1.
4. Remaining DV is marked on the appropriate Condition Monitor.

If armor ≥ modified DV before resistance (special cases / certain rules), some attacks may convert Physical to Stun — follow Core “armor vs DV” notes for the attack type.

## Condition monitors

- **Physical boxes** = 8 + ⌈Body / 2⌉
- **Stun boxes** = 8 + ⌈Willpower / 2⌉

Every **3 boxes** filled on a track impose a **−1 wound modifier** to most tests.

## Knocked out / dying

- Stun track full → unconscious
- Physical track full → unconscious / dying; use **Overflow** (Body boxes) before death
- Stabilization and healing: [Healing and Injuries](../Healing%20and%20Injuries.md)

## Elemental & special damage

Fire, electricity, toxin, water, pollution, radiation — secondary effects (armor flags, status) apply after or with the DV. Check elemental rules when they come up.
