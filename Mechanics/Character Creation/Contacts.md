# Contacts

Contacts are your network: fixers, talismongers, cops on the take, gang buddies, corp secretaries, chop-shop mechanics.

## Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection** | How useful / well-placed they are (access, info, goods) |
| **Loyalty** | How far they’ll go for *you* (risk, favors, silence) |

Both usually range **1–6** at creation (higher possible later).

## Free contacts

Core grants contact points from **Charisma** (formula: typically Charisma × 3 points to split among Connection + Loyalty across contacts). Confirm exact formula in your book.

## Using contacts

- Favors, info, gear at better Availability / price, introductions, alibis.
- High Connection + low Loyalty → useful but transactional.
- High Loyalty + low Connection → will try hard but limited reach.
- Burning a contact or getting them hurt has story consequences.

## Advancement

Improve Connection/Loyalty with Karma and roleplay. Add new contacts through play more often than shopping with Karma alone.
