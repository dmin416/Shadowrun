# Priority System

Assign each column a unique priority letter (**A** highest → **E** lowest). You cannot put two categories on the same letter.

## Priority table (SR5 Core)

| Priority | Metatype | Attributes | Magic / Resonance | Skills | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Human (9), Elf (8), Dwarf / Ork (7), Troll (5) | 24 | Magician or Mystic Adept: Magic 6, 2 spells rating 5; Technomancer: Resonance 6, 2 complex forms rating 5; Adept: Magic 6 | 46 / 10 | 450,000¥ |
| **B** | Human (7), Elf (6), Dwarf / Ork (4), Troll (0) | 20 | Magician or Mystic Adept: Magic 4, 2 spells rating 4; Technomancer: Resonance 4, 2 CF rating 4; Adept: Magic 4; Aspected Magician: Magic 5 | 36 / 5 | 275,000¥ |
| **C** | Human (5), Elf (3), Dwarf / Ork (0) | 16 | Magician or Mystic Adept: Magic 3, 2 spells rating 3; Technomancer: Resonance 3, 2 CF rating 3; Adept: Magic 3; Aspected Magician: Magic 4 | 28 / 2 | 140,000¥ |
| **D** | Human (3), Elf (0) | 14 | Adept: Magic 2; Aspected Magician: Magic 3 | 22 / 0 | 50,000¥ |
| **E** | Human (1) | 12 | — | 18 / 0 | 6,000¥ |

Numbers in **Metatype** parentheses are **special attribute points** (Edge, and Magic/Resonance if applicable).

**Skills** column is **Skill points / Skill Group points**.

> Exact spell/complex-form freebies and aspected options follow Core wording; *Run Faster* adds alternate builds (sum-to-ten, life modules, etc.).

## How to choose

| Role | Often high | Often low |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Street samurai | Attributes, Resources | Magic |
| Mage / shaman | Magic, Skills or Attributes | Resources |
| Decker / technomancer | Skills, Resources or Resonance | Metatype flash |
| Face | Attributes (Cha), Skills | Resources or Magic |
| Rigger | Resources, Skills | Magic |

## Alternate creation

*Run Faster* and other books add **Sum to Ten**, **Karma build**, **Life Modules**, and expanded metatypes/qualities. Use Core priorities unless the table agrees otherwise.
