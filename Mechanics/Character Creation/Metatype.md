# Metatype

Metatype sets attribute minimums/maximums, racial qualities, and how many **special attribute points** you get from the Metatype priority.

## Standard metatypes (SR5 Core)

| Metatype | Notable traits |
| --- | --- |
| **Human** | Extra Edge flexibility; “default” social camouflage in many sprawls |
| **Elf** | Higher Agility / Charisma ceilings; Low-Light Vision |
| **Dwarf** | Higher Body / Strength / Willpower; Thermographic Vision; toxin resistance; shorter stride |
| **Ork** | Higher Body / Strength; Low-Light Vision; often social prejudice |
| **Troll** | Highest Body / Strength; Thermographic Vision; dermal armor; reach; size issues (gear, space, noticeability) |

Consult the Core racial attribute tables for exact min/max per Attribute.

## Special attribute points

Spend Metatype priority points on:

- **Edge** (all characters)
- **Magic** or **Resonance** (only if you took a Magic/Resonance priority that grants it)

You cannot raise special attributes above their racial maximum with these points alone without later karma / advancement rules.

## Story impact

Metatype is not only numbers:

- Security profiles and prejudice differ by sprawl and corp.
- Trolls and orks may need custom gear and housing considerations.
- Elves/dwarfs may access certain communities or face stereotyping.

## Expanded options

*Run Faster*, *Bullets & Bandages*-adjacent material, and other books add metavariants, Infected, shapeshifters, etc. Confirm Availability and table buy-in before using them.
