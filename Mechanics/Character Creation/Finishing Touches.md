## Additional Purchases & Restrictions (chargen)

| Item | Karma | Restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bound spirits | 1 per service (Force = Magic) | Number of bound spirits ≤ Charisma |
| Complex forms | 4 each | Total forms ≤ Resonance × 2 |
| Spells / rituals / preparations | 5 each | **Each** group ≤ Magic × 2 (spells, rituals, and preparations counted separately) |
| Bond foci | Per Magic bonding costs | Total Force bonded ≤ Magic × 2 |
| Contacts | 1 per Connection + 1 per Loyalty (min 2) | ≤ **7** Karma on any one contact; unlimited count |
| Registered sprites | 1 per task (Level = Resonance) | Number ≤ Charisma |
| Mystic adept Power Points | 5 per PP | PP ≤ Magic (not free like adepts) |

Free contact Karma (Cha×3 / ×6) is a **separate** pool for contacts only. Extra contacts may also use leftover Karma at the same rates.

## Karma costs (used at chargen Step Seven)

Same formulas as Character Advancement (no training time at creation).

| Improvement | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Attribute | new Rating × **5** |
| Active skill | new Rating × **2** (cumulative from 0; e.g. buy to 2 = 2+4 = 6) |
| Active skill group | new Rating × **5** |
| Knowledge / Language | new Rating × **1** |
| New specialization | **7** Karma (Step Seven; breaks skill group if applied to a group skill) |
| New spell / ritual / prep | 5 |
| New complex form | 4 |
| New Positive quality | Listed cost (chargen; in play = listed × 2) |

### Cumulative Active skill (quick)

| Rating reached | Total Karma from 0 |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 12 |
| 4 | 20 |
| 5 | 30 |
| 6 | 42 |

Knowledge/Language cumulative from 0: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, …

Attribute raise examples: 2→3 = 15; 4→5 = 25; 5→6 = 30.

Skill groups may be broken in Step Seven by buying a specialization for a skill in the group (not allowed in Step Five priority spend).

## Starting nuyen

1. Spend Resources on gear + lifestyle months (see Resources and Gear).
2. Unspent Resources into play: max **5,000¥** (rest lost).
3. Roll Starting Nuyen for the lifestyle you bought; add carryover.

| Lifestyle | Cost / month | Starting Nuyen roll |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Street | Free | 1D6 × 20¥ |
| Squatter | 500¥ | 2D6 × 40¥ |
| Low | 2,000¥ | 3D6 × 60¥ |
| Middle | 5,000¥ | 4D6 × 100¥ |
| High | 10,000¥ | 5D6 × 500¥ |
| Luxury | 100,000¥ | 6D6 × 1,000¥ |

**Starting nuyen** = (roll) + (carryover ≤ 5,000¥).

Dwarf +20% / troll +100% lifestyle cost (Metatype / Resources).
