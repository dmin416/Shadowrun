# Finishing Touches

After priorities, qualities, and shopping:

## 1. Spend remaining Karma

Common spends:

- Raise attributes or skills (creation cost tables)
- Extra spells / complex forms / adept powers (where allowed)
- Bind foci
- Extra contacts
- Positive qualities if Karma remains

## 2. Knowledge & languages

Spend free Knowledge/Language points from Logic + Intuition. Cover sprawl knowledge your runner should have.

## 3. Calculate derived stats

- Initiative & Initiative Dice
- Mental / Physical / Social Limits
- Physical Condition Monitor boxes = 8 + (Body ÷ 2, round up)
- Stun Condition Monitor boxes = 8 + (Willpower ÷ 2, round up)
- Overflow boxes = Body (for dying track)
- Essence, Magic/Resonance after ware
- Armor totals, weapon modes, Matrix persona attributes

## 4. Lifestyle & identity

- Buy starting Lifestyle (or share a safehouse)
- Fake SIN(s), licenses, cred accounts
- Where do they sleep? Who knows their face?

## 5. Starting reputation / heat

Most tables start at **Street Cred / Notoriety / Public Awareness** 0 unless qualities say otherwise.

## 6. Background & hooks

Write a short history: why they run, who they owe, what they won’t do. Give the GM two hooks.

## Final sheet check

- [ ] Priorities unique A–E
- [ ] Attributes within racial min/max; only one at max
- [ ] Skills within creation caps
- [ ] Availability legal for starting gear
- [ ] Essence / Magic math correct
- [ ] Lifestyle paid
- [ ] Contacts listed with Connection/Loyalty
