# Resources and Gear

Resources priority gives starting **nuyen**. Spend it on weapons, armor, cyberware, vehicles, lifestyles, fake SINs, and tools of the trade.

## Availability at creation

- Starting characters typically cannot buy gear with **Availability higher than 12**, or gear marked **R** (restricted) / **F** (forbidden) beyond what the GM allows.
- Exact Availability ratings are in the gear tables (*Street Gear* chapter and supplements).

## Common budget sinks

| Category | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Cyberware / bioware** | Essence cost; grade (standard, alphaware, etc.) changes Essence and price |
| **Weapons & armor** | Accuracy, DV, AP, capacity for mods |
| **Commlink / cyberdeck** | Matrix attributes; decks are expensive |
| **Vehicles / drones** | Riggers burn Resources fast |
| **Magic goods** | Foci, reagents, lodge, ritual materials |
| **Fake SINs & licenses** | Needed to walk around “legit” |
| **Lifestyle** | Prepaid period; see [Housing and Lifestyle](../Housing%20and%20Lifestyle.md) |

## Essence & ware

- Essence starts at **6**.
- Implanted ware reduces Essence (bioware often less Essence than cyber for similar effect).
- Lower Essence → lower Social limit; Awakened/Emerged lose Magic/Resonance.

## Leftover nuyen

Unspent nuyen carries into play as cash / accounts (GM may require how it’s held — certified credstick, hidden accounts, etc.).

## Street level vs high lifestyle

Priority **E** Resources (6,000¥) forces creative scrounging. Priority **A** (450,000¥) can buy serious chrome or a deck — still not infinite.
