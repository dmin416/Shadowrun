## Standard goods

No Availability rating → buy at listed Cost (store / online / vendor). Traceable on SIN/datatrail. Black-market purchase of legal goods possible (GM surcharge).

Chargen caps (Avail ≤12, Device Rating ≤6) **do not** apply after play starts.

---

## Black market (Availability Test)

```
Negotiation + Charisma [Social]  vs  item Availability
```

| Result | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Win | Gear at listed price; delivery = table time **÷ net hits** |
| Tie | Gear found; delivery = table time × **2** |
| Fail | Retry after **2×** table time |
| Glitch | Unwanted attention (sting, rivals, bad deal) — GM |
| Critical glitch | Extreme heat; **no** acquisition this attempt |

### Money talks

+1 die on Negotiation per **+25%** of listed price you're willing to pay. Cap **+12 dice** at **400%** price.

### Contacts

Contact rolls their Negotiation + Charisma; **Connection** adds to Social limit. May charge finder's fee if Loyalty/relationship is weak. Specialty contacts are better at their niche.

## Fencing

Standard legal goods with no Avail → essentially worthless on the fence.

### Step 1 — Find a buyer

Extended **Etiquette + Charisma [Social] (10)**; interval = Delivery Time for that item's cost. May Teamwork with item Availability (rarer bundled goods help).

Glitch / crit → unwanted attention (as buy glitch).

### Step 2 — Haggle

Opposed Negotiation + Charisma [Social].

Final offer = **25%** listed + **5%** per your net hits, or −5% per buyer's net hits. Walk away and restart with another buyer if desired.

Glitch on haggle → insulted buyer; deal may die or worse.

### Contact fence

Contact pays **5% × Loyalty** of listed value, no questions. Faster/safer; usually less ¥ than DIY.

## (Il)legality

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| (none) | Legal to buy/own/carry (crime with it still prosecuted) |
| **R** Restricted | Needs permit/license (real or fake tied to SIN). Misuse still illegal |
| **F** Forbidden | Never OK for private citizens; no permit helps |

Fake license dies if linked Fake SIN burns. Jurisdiction varies; adjust by setting.

## Concealing Gear

Concealability is a DP modifier on the **observer**.

### Passive notice

Perception + Intuition [Mental] + Concealability (± vis/distract). ≥1 hit → notices item on someone else.

### Active hide

Complex Action to stash → Opposed **Palming + Agility [Physical]** vs Perception + Intuition [Mental] (searcher may use Palming). Searcher pool modified by Concealability.

### Pat-down

Searcher may use Agility + Physical limit. Halve negative Concealability; double positive Concealability.

### Concealability Modifiers

| Mod\* | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| −6 | RFID, bug slap patch, microdrone, contacts |
| −4 | Hold-out, monowhip, clip, credstick, chips, sequencer/passkey, autopicker, lockpicks, commlink, glasses |
| −2 | Light pistol, knife, sap, minidrone, microgrenade, flash-pak, jammer, cyberdeck, RCC |
| 0 | Heavy pistol, MP folded stock, grenade, goggles, ammo belt/drum, club, baton collapsed |
| +2 | SMG, MP stock extended, medkit, small drone, baton extended, stun baton |
| +4 | Sword, sawed-off shotgun, bullpup AR |
| +6 | Katana, monosword, shotgun, AR, sport rifle, crossbow |
| +8 | Sniper, bow, grenade launcher, medium drone |
| +10 | MG, rocket/missile launcher, staff, claymore, metahuman body |

\*Applies to observer's pool.
