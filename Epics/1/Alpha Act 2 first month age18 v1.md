# Act 2 Expansion: The First Month in the Sprawl

Standalone companion to the main outline. Expands Act 2 into a day-by-day. Main outline's Act 2 section stays as-is.

Scope: Day 1 = last day on foot / first vehicle ride, through Day 30 = threshold into Act 3. Crew is Elma + Bee + Alpha only. No Dom, no Cass.

Format: up to 20 beats per day. Routine days shorter. Pivotal days longer.

Consistency notes:
- No SIN, no nuyen at start. Only barter goods (furs, carved items, dried food).
- SIN = fake SIN, sourced through a contact. Rating 1-6, cost/time scale with rating. Not instant.
- Bioluminescence, Mood Eyes, Mood Hair, Distinctive Style, Astral Hazing/Critter Spook are constant, not on/off. "Nature slip" = a moment these become undeniable, not a one-time trigger.
- Bee already has history with Elma. Reunion, not introduction.
- GridGuide ~80 km/h urban. Walking in rough terrain ~1.25-2.5 km/h. Day 1 = the whiplash between those two numbers.

## DAY 1

1. Final hours walking, tree line thinning, gravel road underfoot instead of dirt
2. First distant highway sound, Elma names it before he identifies it
3. Reaching the roadside, first clear view of pavement, guardrail, signage
4. Elma arranges transport (supply truck, drone-cab, or contact call)
5. First contact with a door handle, seatbelt, powered window
6. Vehicle pulls away, first time moving faster than a sprint without his own legs
7. Motion sickness/disorientation possible, body has never processed sustained artificial acceleration
8. Elma explains GridGuide as the vehicle self-corrects at an intersection
9. The math lands: a week on foot erased in an afternoon. Elma states the drive time aloud, he goes quiet doing the comparison
10. Powerlines, then a gas station, then a strip mall, built density stacking up
11. First traffic light
12. Technosense picks up the vehicle's systems as ambient noise
13. Elma checks on him periodically, doesn't hover
14. Highway on-ramp, real traffic, noise and speed of vehicles passing close
15. First sight of the sprawl skyline in the distance, hazy
16. Streetlights coming on as the drive runs into evening
17. Sprawl's outer edge, vehicle slows, buildings replace trees
18. City sound at low volume from inside the vehicle, traffic hum, distant sirens
19. Elma names the neighborhood/district
20. End of drive, arrival at the first night's location, exhaustion setting in

## DAY 2

Centerpiece of the month.

2. Location: Bee's workshop/garage, drone parts around

4. Bee's unguarded reaction, may not have known Elma survived
5. Embrace/greeting reflecting real history
6. Bee notices something's different about Elma before it's explained
7. Elma explains, at least partially, what happened over the missing year
8. Bee's reaction: worry, relief, some anger at being left in the dark
9. Bee registers Alpha, curiosity filtered through loyalty to Elma
10. Old shorthand between Elma and Bee, Alpha visibly outside it
11. Alpha notices the outsider feeling honestly, not played as jealousy
12. Bee asks Elma directly what Alpha is / where he's from
13. Elma gives Bee a version of the truth
14. Bee's read shifts to provisional acceptance, on Elma's word
15. First real three-way conversation, shape of the team starting to exist
16. Bee offers something concrete: a place to stay, a lead, equipment
17. Closes on a forward note, agreement to catch up properly (sets up Day 15)

Payoff beat, earned by Days 20-27 rather than arriving cold.

2. Elma lays out plainly what life without a solid SIN looks like long-term: restricted housing, no legal work, permanent vulnerability
3. Alpha connects this to why Elma operates the way she does, shadows as survival logic, not aesthetic
4. He grapples with what kind of life this actually is compared to the one he left
5. Resolve rather than despair, he chooses to keep going, understanding the cost now
6. Month's arc closes on this internal beat rather than an external plot event
