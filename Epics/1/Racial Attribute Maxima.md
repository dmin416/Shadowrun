## Adult dragons (Core NPC blocks)

| Type | BOD | AGI | REA | STR | WIL | LOG | INT | CHA | EDG | ESS | MAG |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eastern / Sirrush | 17 | 8 | 8 | **35** | 9 | **10** | 8 | 9 | 6 | 10 | 10 |
| Feathered Serpent | 15 | **9** | **10** | 30 | **10** | 9 | 9 | 8 | 6 | 10 | 10 |
| **Western** | **18** | 7 | 8 | **40** | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 6 | 10 | 10 |

Western is the physical peak among printed adult dragons (**STR 40**, **BOD 18**). Great Dragons sit further beyond these adult blocks; Core does not give them a full PC-style max table.
