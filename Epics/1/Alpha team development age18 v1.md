## Assumptions

Adult dragon baseline for reference: Body 17-18, Agility 7-9, Magic 10, Sorcery 14, Unarmed 12, Hardened Armor equal to Body. A young dragon starts well below this and grows toward it over three years of active running alongside the team.

Elma is adjusted to early-to-mid twenties, experienced runner but not a career veteran.

## CASSIANDRA (CASS) — Young Female Western Dragon, Rebel Medic, Maturing Rapidly

**Cassiandra**, **Cass** for short. A dragon rebel — walked away from what dragons are supposed to be, the same way Elma walked away from court and Alpha walked away from the circle. Team medic / doctor by calling and practice; the hands that set a bone are the same hands that could crush a car door. Her strength is unparalleled next to any non-dragon on the team. In human form it reads as “impossibly strong metahuman” if she holds back. When she doesn’t, the comparison stops being useful.

**How she joins:** **Really far out.** Long after Dom is already crew. The team is on (or entangled in) an **assassination mission** with Cass as the target. She survives the hit. In the middle of that violence she finds a match in Alpha -- something that finally feels peer-scale -- and becomes interested in him. She then **forces herself onto the group**: medic seat claimed; she stays because she chose them / him. Not a soft recruit. Not Alpha saving her into gratitude. Not on Dom's introduction job.

The adult western dragon stat block sits at Body 18, Agility 7, Reaction 8, Strength 40, Magic 10, Sorcery 14, Unarmed 12, Hardened Armor 18. Cass is scaled down from this, growing toward it across three years of active engagement with the world — which for a dragon is an extraordinarily compressed timeline. Dragons do not normally mature this fast. Running alongside this team and absorbing real conflict is accelerating something that should take decades.

**Starting (juvenile, rough equivalent ~300 Karma)**
Body 10, Agility 5, Reaction 6, Strength ~20, Magic 6
Sorcery 7 + Magic 6 → **13 dice**
Unarmed 7 + Agility 5 → **12 dice** plus natural weapon damage
First Aid / Medicine already real — she came to the team as a healer, not as someone learning from scratch
Hardened Armor 10 — most pistol rounds simply do not register
Elemental Attack: DV 12P, AP -6. Not subtle.
No human form yet stable. Still figuring out how to exist in a world built for things much smaller, and how to practice medicine without revealing what her hands can actually do.

**Year 1 (+accelerated growth, ~560 total)**
Body 10→13, Agility 5→6, Strength ~25, Magic 6→8, first initiation grade
Sorcery 9 + Magic 8 → **17 dice**
Unarmed 9 + Agility 6 → **15 dice**
Medicine / First Aid rising with field trauma — the team’s primary stabilizer under fire
Hardened Armor 13 — small arms fire is an annoyance
Elemental Attack: DV 16P, AP -8
Human form begins to stabilize. Passing as metahuman in social situations becomes possible with effort. Still deeply uncomfortable with the scale of it. Strength in human form already outclasses every augmented human and most orks she meets; she learns early how carefully she has to touch injured people.

**Year 2 (+accelerated growth, ~820 total)**
Body 13→15, Agility 6→7, Strength ~32, Magic 8→9, second initiation grade
Sorcery 11 + Magic 9 → **20 dice**
Unarmed 10 + Agility 7 → **17 dice**
Medicine deepens — trauma, toxins, battlefield triage at a level that makes street docs defer to her without knowing why
Hardened Armor 15 — assault rifle fire causes her to flinch, not bleed
Elemental Attack: DV 18P, AP -9
Human form comfortable enough to hold through a full run. Begins to understand why the others care about things like relationships and consequences. This is more unsettling to her than the combat. The rebel in her hardens: she is not going back to whatever life dragons expected of her.

**Year 3 (~1080 total, approaching adult threshold)**
Body 15→17, Agility 7, Reaction 8, Strength ~38, Magic 10, initiation grade 3
Sorcery 13 + Magic 10 → **23 dice**
Unarmed 11 + Agility 7 → **18 dice**
Hardened Armor 17 — she is now one point of Body short of full adult stat block
Elemental Attack: DV 20P, AP -10
Medic of record for the team — the person everyone looks for when someone goes down
She can hold human form indefinitely. She has started to understand why she cannot tell the others what she actually is yet and is not sure whether that bothers her more or less than the fact that she is beginning to care whether it bothers them.

By the end of Year 3 she is a dice pool or two short of a full adult dragon across the board. Strength sits just shy of the adult ceiling of 40 — still beyond anything non-dragons bring to a fight or a surgery table. What she has that the adult stat block does not capture is three years of learned judgment about when not to use any of it.
