## Act 1
- Memories back
- Unfinished business surfaces
- Going with her to the site where she was injured, to actually uncover what happened there -- far upriver from where he actually found her, well outside the range he'd ever explored himself, since she fought her way through trap lines and poachers both before finally going down a long way from where it all actually happened. Roughly 15-30 miles of rough, trail-less terrain, realistically 2-4 days on foot given the ground they'd have to cover.
- Finding only traces of past devastation there now -- whoever was responsible already moved on
- Learning her creed at the site itself, piecing together what she'd actually been fighting for
- Her creed is what inspires him to commit to traveling with her -- not a separate decision, the direct result of standing where it happened and understanding who she really is

**Travel note (Act 1 to Act 2):** The Olympic Peninsula to the edge of the Seattle sprawl is roughly 60-100 miles depending on route. On foot, 5-10+ days through mixed wilderness and rougher country. The moment they reach an actual road and a vehicle, that same distance collapses to 1.5-3 hours. This gap is worth playing up deliberately -- watching a week or more of walking distance disappear in a single afternoon is one of Alpha's first real, disorienting lessons about how different the wider world actually runs compared to everything he's ever known.

## Notes

**Do not fabricate turmoil.** No invented team fights, no mandatory ethical-dilemma beats, no "trust must be contested" arcs. Conflict comes from the job and the world when it actually shows up, not from padding interpersonal drama.

**On creeds -- Alpha vs. Elma:**

Alpha's Code of Honor is self-imposed. Nobody placed it on him and nothing outside himself enforces it beyond the fact that it's genuinely who he is. He isn't bound by it, he chose it, and he'd choose it again.

Elma's creed works differently by design, wuxia-style rather than bushido-style: a personal philosophy she holds because it's who she is, not a mechanical Code of Honor with a rulebook penalty attached. She can bend or break it in the moment if something more important is in front of her, the same way a wandering wuxia hero might abandon their own principles to save one person, and come back to the creed afterward rather than being punished by it.

**Elma's creed (draft):** *Strength exists to protect what can't protect itself.*

Both of them are free. Neither is a slave to what they believe. The difference is that Alpha's conviction runs so deep it functions like an unbreakable law of his own nature, while Elma's is a compass she carries and can set down if she has to, then pick back up.

**Team join pacing (locked):**
1. **Bee** -- early (Act 2 reunion). Elma's close teammate.
2. **Dom** -- far out. Met on a job after the three already have run history. Soul pact after tech enhancement reveal.
3. **Cass** -- really far out. Assassination target long after Dom is already crew. Matches on Alpha; forces her way in.
