# Character Sheet -- Alpha (v2)

**What he is:** Son of a living nexus and a goddess. Human-shaped. Not human-capped. The Sixth World will try to file him as a changeling mystic adept; that is a costume the world understands, not the truth of what walks around in it. He becomes whatever the story and his nature require. Numbers on this sheet are his **current expression at age 18** -- what eighteen years of lived life have made visible -- not ceilings, not chargen budgets, not racial maximums.

**The only balance frame that matters:** Nexus Absorption and the energy-flow / Nexus State rules below. Power-ups cost active flow, interruption, and recovery -- not arbitrary attribute caps, Essence gatekeeping, or "humans can't do that."

**How to read this sheet:** Every entry exists because something in the Timeline explains why he has it. Where a number appears, it reflects what he has actually lived into so far. Where something is missing or blank, nothing has happened yet to earn it. Story first. SR5 vocabulary is scaffolding for play, never a leash.

**BIG NOTE -- DEFERRED TO PLAY:** Reputation, Street Cred, Notoriety, Public Awareness, Why He Runs, his enemies, and his SIN status will all be figured out through actual play rather than decided in advance. This sheet does not need to answer those questions right now. Karma is tracked by story logic -- if it happened in the Timeline, it counts. Lifestyle will be determined once he's actually in play and has a place to live.

## Origin (see Alpha timeline age18 v1.md for full detail)
Found as a newborn by a druidic circle living off the grid in the Olympic Peninsula -- left at the base of an ancient cedar, already glowing, already *other*. He is the son of a living nexus and a goddess; the circle felt the scale of him the moment they came close and raised him anyway, with total honesty about what they knew and what they did not. Raised by **Bracken**, the circle's leader and his father-figure. **Wren**, the circle's rune-keeper, led the decade-long magical research into his nature alongside two other elders, and guided his first Initiation. Not a random SURGE accident. A blooming. Something deliberate wearing a human shape. Left the circle at 18 after roughly a year and a half spent caring for an injured shadowrunner named Elma, found badly hurt in the forest early in Year 17. Acting on the circle's own tenet of loving your neighbor and helping whoever crosses your path in need, he healed her physical injuries within weeks, but a head injury left her with amnesia that lingered far longer. Over the year and a half that followed, as she recovered under his care, what began as gratitude became real feeling between them. Close to his eighteenth birthday, a kiss triggered the sudden return of her memory, revealing not just her shadowrunning life but the elven noble family and court she'd deliberately walked away from long before any of this happened. He left with her not long after Bracken told him plainly that the circle had given him everything they had to offer.

**NEXUS ABSORPTION (WIP -- STORY FIRST. THIS IS THE BALANCE FRAME.)**

Alpha is the son of a living nexus and a goddess -- a living nexus in his own right, wearing a human shape. Ambient mana, background count, electrical fields, heat, radiation and sustained magical energy flows into him naturally. Instantaneous attacks in flight (combat spells mid-cast, bullets, explosions already detonating) are not free lunch; sustained flows and environments are. What he can become is not gated by metatype math -- it is gated by whether the energy is moving, what it costs him to hold, and what the story asks of him.

**Why spirits fear him:** Spirits of Man, Blood Spirits, and Shadow Spirits can sense that Alpha's nature makes him capable of absorbing their very existence. This is what drives his Spirit Bane qualities against all three.

**Origin of self-healing:** At Year 9, a fall from a tall tree broke his leg. Pure instinct triggered an inward pull of energy that knit the bone back together. This is the real origin of his Heal spell and his healing-adjacent adept capability.

**What it actually looks like:** Cracks of light bloom along his skin like veins catching fire from the inside, eyes go incandescent, hair lifts and moves, air visibly bends with heat-shimmer. At Nexus state he looks like something the world hasn't finished deciding whether to call a person.

**NEXUS STATES**

**Core rule: the energy has to be moving.** Nexus States exist only while energy is actively flowing through him -- inward as absorption, outward as expression, or both. The moment flow stops the state drops back toward Sealed.

| State | Level | Spell/Power Bonus | Environmental Effect |
|-------|-------|--------------------|------------------------|
| Sealed | 0 (voluntary) | None. Passes as normal. Costs 1 Stun per hour to maintain. | No tells. Critter Spook goes quiet. |
| Dormant | 1 | +2 dice | Minor flickers. Electronics stutter occasionally. |
| Awakening | 2 | +4 dice, spells unbound by Force | Lights fail nearby. Mana users feel him. Faint light traces along his skin. |
| Resonant | 3 | +6 dice, spells cost half drain | Electronics malfunction in range. Environment reacts. Light-cracks visibly spreading, eyes brightening. |
| Surging | 4 | +8 dice, no drain | Mana storms form around him. Awakened overwhelmed by proximity. Hair and clothing move like something alive, air shimmers with heat. |
| Nexus | 5 | Unlimited. No drain. No Force gate. No PP cost. | Reality distorts. Avatar state. Electronics in range serve him. He looks, unmistakably, like what he is -- divinity wearing a human shape. |

**Building a state:** Requires an active Magic + Willpower roll each turn to keep drawing energy inward, push it outward, or sustain both directions simultaneously.

**Maintaining a state:** The same active flow that built it has to continue. Going passive causes the state to immediately begin dropping.

**Losing a state:** Flow stops for any reason -- the state drops one full level per turn until it settles back to Sealed.

**NEXUS STATE INTERRUPTION**

| Duration held | Immediate consequence | Recovery |
|--------------|------------------------|----------|
| Less than 1 turn | 4 Stun, knocked out of state | Clears with a normal night's rest, often faster |
| 1-3 turns | 8 Stun, 4 Physical, disoriented | Full recovery within a day or two given rest |
| 4-10 turns | 12 Physical, Magic rating drops 1 temporarily | Magic returns within 24-48 hours of rest |
| Extended | Severe Physical damage, Magic rating drop lasts longer | Still fully recoverable; permanent loss is last-resort GM call |

**EFFECTS WHILE ACTIVELY IN A STATE**
- Dormant: restores 1 Stun box per turn while sustained
- Awakening: fuels a power without PP cost for that turn only
- Resonant: pushes a spell beyond normal Force for that turn only
- Surging: environmental discharge continues for as long as the state holds
- Nexus: anything, GM discretion, story moment territory

**NEXUS PROGRESSION (WIP -- GENEROUS, STORY FIRST)**

Each full Nexus cycle -- actively building to State 5 and consciously winding back to Sealed -- generates 3 bonus Karma.

Holding Nexus state through a significant narrative event triggers direct PP gain, metamagic, or Magic rating increase at GM discretion.

### Elma -- Character Profile: The Rebellious Royal Elf

Born into elven nobility in Tír na nÓg or Tír Tairngire, raised under the full weight of aristocratic perfection, flowing hair, cultivated grace, a carefully managed public image. She rejected most of it. Not all of it, which is exactly what makes her interesting.

**The Hair.** She kept the ponytail rather than cutting it short. Not a rejection of her heritage, a rewriting of it on her own terms. Practical, a little defiant, still beautiful.

**The Eyebrow Piercing.** A single ring or barbell, simple and sharp. In elven aristocratic circles this reads as a direct insult to a culture built on curated, untouched beauty. She marked her own face on purpose.

**The Look.** Royal and street layered together. An armored trenchcoat, real and maintained rather than decorative, worn over something that still hints at money and quality underneath. Nothing about her gear is polished for appearances. All of it has actually been used.

**Attitude.** Genuinely values friendship across every metatype. Holds a real, considered opinion that age and experience don't automatically grant wisdom. Better at street etiquette than court etiquette by deliberate choice, though the court upbringing is still in there, a second language kept buried until it's actually useful.

**The Tension.** That elven air of superiority still slips out sometimes. She knows it slips out. That gap between who she wants to be and how she sometimes actually behaves is where her most interesting moments live.

Other elves recognize her instantly as both nobility and trouble.

## Background
18 years old. Male. Son of a living nexus and a goddess -- a bloodline carrying a promise made before humanity had names for things. Not a SURGE accident. A blooming. Magic returning to the world woke what was always waiting. He embodies the nexus of nature, technology, and existence itself, wearing a human shape because that is how he chose to enter this age -- or how he was sent. Corruption -- not destruction -- is what he cannot abide. Raised by a druidic circle in the Olympic Peninsula who felt what he was from the moment they found him and raised him with total honesty anyway.

### Why They Run
Early in Year 17 he found a badly injured shadowrunner named Elma in the forest and helped her back to health. A head injury left her with amnesia, and over the roughly year and a half he spent caring for her, real feeling grew between them. Close to his eighteenth birthday, a kiss brought her memory rushing back, along with the unfinished business of her old life. He left with her to help her face it.

### Hooks / Enemies / Debts
- **Elma's unfinished business** -- the job that went bad, the team she was running with (including Bee), and whatever is still unresolved from her life before the amnesia.
- **Cass assassination contract** -- whoever put the hit on Cassiandra; the crew's entanglement in that job.
- **Elma's noble family and court** -- an elven noble house she deliberately walked away from long before any of this happened.
- Corporate logging operation responsible for the Year 16 pollution incident -- unresolved, possibly unaware Alpha exists.
- A corporate-funded paracritter poaching operation working the deep Olympic Peninsula, responsible for Elma's original injury -- unresolved.
- Spirits of Man, Blood Spirits, and Shadow Spirits all recognize him as a threat.
