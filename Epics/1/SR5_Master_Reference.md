## WEAPON VS DEFENSE — WORKED NUMBERS

### How damage resolves

1. Attacker rolls Weapon Skill + AGI [Accuracy] vs Defender REA + INT
2. Attacker net hits added to base DV → Modified DV
3. Modified Armor = Armor + AP (AP usually negative)
4. If Modified DV ≥ Modified Armor → Physical damage. If less → Stun.
5. Defender rolls BOD + Modified Armor. Each hit removes 1 from Modified DV.
6. Remaining DV = boxes on Condition Monitor.

**Assume 2 attacker net hits throughout the table below.**

### Dragon Hardened Armor

Hardened Armor = BOD. If Modified DV < Hardened value → zero damage, no roll.
If it clears: normal resistance + auto-hits equal to half Hardened value (after AP, round up).

| Dragon | Hardened | Assault rifle DV 11 AP −2 → Mod DV 11 | Result |
|--------|----------|----------------------------------------|--------|
| Feathered Serpent | 15 | 11 < 15 | Zero damage |
| Eastern | 17 | 11 < 17 | Zero damage |
| Western | 18 | 11 < 18 | Zero damage |

AV rocket DV 24 AP −4 vs Western: Mod DV 24 vs Hardened 18. 24 > 18 → penetrates. Resist BOD 18 + 9 auto-hits. Physical CM 17 boxes. Expect 3–6 boxes per rocket hit.

### Condition Monitor sizes by BOD and WIL

Physical CM = (BOD ÷ 2) + 8. Stun CM = (WIL ÷ 2) + 8.

| BOD | Physical CM | WIL | Stun CM |
|-----|-------------|-----|---------|
| 4 | 10 | 4 | 10 |
| 6 | 11 | 5 | 10 |
| 8 | 12 | 6 | 11 |
| 10 | 13 | 8 | 12 |
| 17 (Eastern) | 16 | 9 | 12 |
| 18 (Western) | 17 | 8 | 12 |

Stun overflow: 2 Stun = 1 Physical. Physical overflow past full track → death unless stabilized.
