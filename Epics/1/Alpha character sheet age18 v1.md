# Character Sheet -- Alpha

**What he is:** Son of a living nexus and a goddess. Human-shaped. Not human-capped. The Sixth World will try to file him as a changeling mystic adept; that is a costume the world understands, not the truth of what walks around in it. He becomes whatever the story and his nature require. Numbers on this sheet are his **current expression at age 18** -- what eighteen years of lived life have made visible -- not ceilings, not chargen budgets, not racial maximums.

**The only balance frame that matters:** Nexus Absorption and the energy-flow / Nexus State rules below. Power-ups cost active flow, interruption, and recovery -- not arbitrary attribute caps, Essence gatekeeping, or "humans can't do that."

**How to read this sheet:** Every entry exists because something in the Timeline explains why he has it. Where a number appears, it reflects what he has actually lived into so far. Where something is missing or blank, nothing has happened yet to earn it. Story first. SR5 vocabulary is scaffolding for play, never a leash.

**BIG NOTE -- DEFERRED TO PLAY:** Reputation, Street Cred, Notoriety, Public Awareness, Why He Runs, his enemies, and his SIN status will all be figured out through actual play rather than decided in advance. This sheet does not need to answer those questions right now. Karma is tracked by story logic -- if it happened in the Timeline, it counts. Lifestyle will be determined once he's actually in play and has a place to live.

## Origin (see Alpha timeline age18 v1.md for full detail)
Found as a newborn by a druidic circle living off the grid in the Olympic Peninsula -- left at the base of an ancient cedar, already glowing, already *other*. He is the son of a living nexus and a goddess; the circle felt the scale of him the moment they came close and raised him anyway, with total honesty about what they knew and what they did not. Raised by **Bracken**, the circle's leader and his father-figure. **Wren**, the circle's rune-keeper, led the decade-long magical research into his nature alongside two other elders, and guided his first Initiation. Not a random SURGE accident. A blooming. Something deliberate wearing a human shape. Left the circle at 18 after roughly a year and a half spent caring for an injured shadowrunner named Elma, found badly hurt in the forest early in Year 17. Acting on the circle's own tenet of loving your neighbor and helping whoever crosses your path in need, he healed her physical injuries within weeks, but a head injury left her with amnesia that lingered far longer. Over the year and a half that followed, as she recovered under his care, what began as gratitude became real feeling between them. Close to his eighteenth birthday, a kiss triggered the sudden return of her memory, revealing not just her shadowrunning life but the elven noble family and court she'd deliberately walked away from long before any of this happened. He left with her not long after Bracken told him plainly that the circle had given him everything they had to offer.

**NEXUS ABSORPTION (WIP -- STORY FIRST. THIS IS THE BALANCE FRAME.)**

Alpha is the son of a living nexus and a goddess -- a living nexus in his own right, wearing a human shape. Ambient mana, background count, electrical fields, heat, radiation and sustained magical energy flows into him naturally. Instantaneous attacks in flight (combat spells mid-cast, bullets, explosions already detonating) are not free lunch; sustained flows and environments are. What he can become is not gated by metatype math -- it is gated by whether the energy is moving, what it costs him to hold, and what the story asks of him.

**Why spirits fear him:** Spirits of Man, Blood Spirits, and Shadow Spirits can sense, on some level, that Alpha's nature makes him capable of absorbing their very existence, not just their power. This is what drives his Spirit Bane qualities against all three. For Blood and Shadow Spirits specifically, this threat is doubled, since their inherent corruption already puts them at odds with his Code of Honor, meaning he represents both an existential threat and a moral enemy to them at once.

**Origin of self-healing:** At Year 9, a fall from a tall tree broke his leg mid-journey while he was following a ley line. Pure instinct, not deliberate practice, triggered an inward pull of energy that knit the bone back together well enough for him to finish a two-day trek on it. This is the real origin of his Heal spell and his healing-adjacent adept capability -- discovered through genuine crisis, not calm meditation.

**What it actually looks like:** This isn't subtle once he's past Dormant. Cracks of light bloom along his skin like veins catching fire from the inside, his eyes go incandescent instead of just shifting color, his hair lifts and moves like something is constantly moving through it, and the air around him visibly bends with heat-shimmer even in the cold. At Nexus state, he doesn't just glow, he looks like something the world hasn't finished deciding whether to call a person. This is the moment he looks genuinely, unmistakably godlike, and he knows it, and it costs him exactly as much as it looks like it should.

**NEXUS STATES**

**Core rule: the energy has to be moving.** Nexus States are not a battery that sits charged once filled. They exist only while energy is actively flowing through him, inward as absorption, outward as expression through a spell or power, or both directions at once. The moment that flow stops, the state begins dropping back down toward Sealed. He cannot reach Nexus state and simply stand there holding it for free; sustaining any state above Dormant requires him to keep actively drawing energy in, pushing it out, or both, every turn.

| State | Level | Spell/Power Bonus | Environmental Effect |
|-------|-------|--------------------|------------------------|
| Sealed | 0 (voluntary) | None. Passes as normal. Costs 1 Stun per hour to maintain. | No tells. Also the only state in which his Critter Spook quality goes quiet -- animals stop reacting to him while he's actively suppressing his astral presence this way. |
| Dormant | 1 | +2 dice | Minor flickers. Electronics stutter occasionally. |
| Awakening | 2 | +4 dice, spells unbound by Force | Lights fail nearby. Mana users feel him. Faint light traces along his skin. |
| Resonant | 3 | +6 dice, spells cost half drain | Electronics malfunction in range. Environment reacts. Light-cracks visibly spreading, eyes brightening. |
| Surging | 4 | +8 dice, no drain | Mana storms form around him. Awakened overwhelmed by proximity. Hair and clothing move like something alive, air shimmers with heat. |
| Nexus | 5 | Unlimited. No drain. No Force gate. No PP cost. | Reality distorts. Avatar state. Electronics in range serve him. He looks, unmistakably, like what he is -- divinity wearing a human shape. |

**Building a state:** Requires an active Magic + Willpower roll each turn to keep drawing energy inward, push it outward, or sustain both directions simultaneously. Ambient mana or energized environments make this easier (better odds or reduced threshold, GM discretion) but never build a state on their own without him actively engaging the flow.

**Maintaining a state:** The same active flow that built it has to continue. Going passive, even briefly, causes the state to immediately begin dropping.

**Losing a state:** If he stops moving energy for any reason, interrupted, unconscious, or simply choosing to stop, the state drops one full level per turn until it settles back to Sealed.

States are exponentially more powerful as they rise, which is exactly why sustaining the higher ones costs so much active effort to maintain, and why looking that magnificent always comes with a bill attached.

**NEXUS STATE INTERRUPTION**

At State 5 Alpha channels everything through the body he currently wears, and it costs him exactly as much as it looks like it should. None of this is permanent damage stacking up over his lifetime -- it's a per-use toll, paid and recovered each time, not a debt that accumulates. His body was built for this. His self-healing (the same instinctive pull first discovered at Year 9) makes recovery faster and more complete than it would be for anyone merely mortal pushing this hard.

| Duration held | Immediate consequence | Recovery |
|--------------|------------------------|----------|
| Less than 1 turn | 4 Stun, knocked out of state | Clears with a normal night's rest, often faster |
| 1-3 turns | 8 Stun, 4 Physical, disoriented | Physical damage heals at his normal (enhanced) healing rate; full recovery within a day or two given rest |
| 4-10 turns | 12 Physical, Magic rating drops 1 temporarily | Magic rating returns to normal within 24-48 hours of rest; this is exhaustion, not injury |
| Extended | Severe Physical damage, Magic rating drop lasts longer, real risk of collapse | Still fully recoverable given time and rest; permanent loss is a last-resort GM call reserved for genuinely reckless overreach, not the expected outcome |

The point is never to punish him out of the story. It's that looking like an unstoppable force of nature should cost something believable in the moment, the way it would for Tony Stark snapping his fingers, not something that quietly cripples the character going forward. He pays hard, then he heals, then he does it again next time the story calls for it.

**EFFECTS WHILE ACTIVELY IN A STATE (not stored charges -- these only apply while the flow is actively maintained)**
- Dormant: restores 1 Stun box per turn while sustained
- Awakening: fuels a power without PP cost for that turn only
- Resonant: pushes a spell beyond normal Force for that turn only
- Surging: environmental discharge continues for as long as the state holds (lights explode, electronics fry, wind bursts outward)
- Nexus: anything, GM discretion, story moment territory, but only for as long as he keeps the flow moving

**NEXUS PROGRESSION (WIP -- GENEROUS, STORY FIRST)**

Each full Nexus cycle, actively building the flow all the way to State 5 and then consciously letting it wind back down to Sealed rather than being forcibly cut off, generates 3 bonus Karma.

Holding Nexus state through a significant narrative event, meaning actively sustaining that flow through real danger or a real story moment rather than just touching it briefly, triggers direct PP gain, metamagic, or Magic rating increase at GM discretion. He doesn't study passively for this part -- he actively channels, and the world responds.

### Elma -- Character Profile: The Rebellious Royal Elf

Born into elven nobility in Tír na nÓg or Tír Tairngire, raised under the full weight of aristocratic perfection, flowing hair, cultivated grace, a carefully managed public image. She rejected most of it. Not all of it, which is exactly what makes her interesting.

**The Hair.** She kept the ponytail rather than cutting it short. Not a rejection of her heritage, a rewriting of it on her own terms. Practical, a little defiant, still beautiful. She knows exactly what she has and is done performing with it for anyone else's benefit.

**The Eyebrow Piercing.** A single ring or barbell, simple and sharp. In elven aristocratic circles this reads as a direct insult to a culture built on curated, untouched beauty. She marked her own face on purpose. Every elder who sees it understands exactly what she's saying without her having to say anything.

**The Look.** Royal and street layered together in a way that makes both feel slightly uncomfortable. An armored trenchcoat, real and maintained rather than decorative, worn over something that still hints at money and quality underneath, possibly electrochromic fabric she runs dark or patterns on her own terms rather than however the court would prefer. Nothing about her gear is polished for appearances. All of it has actually been used.

**Attitude.** Genuinely values friendship across every metatype, including ork and dwarf contacts her family would find embarrassing. She talks to them like people, not exceptions. Holds a real, considered opinion that age and experience don't automatically grant wisdom. Better at street etiquette than court etiquette by deliberate choice, though the court upbringing is still in there, a second language kept buried until it's actually useful.

**The Tension.** That elven air of superiority the old stories describe as basically inescapable, even for elves actively trying to reject it, still slips out of her sometimes. She knows it slips out. That gap between who she wants to be and how she sometimes actually behaves is where her most interesting moments live.

Other elves recognize her instantly as both nobility and trouble. On the street she reads as someone carrying more background than she's letting on, which in the shadows can be either a real asset or a real liability depending entirely on who's looking.

## Background
### Backstory
18 years old. Male. Son of a living nexus and a goddess -- a bloodline carrying a promise made before humanity had names for things. Not a SURGE accident. A blooming. Magic returning to the world woke what was always waiting. He embodies the nexus of nature, technology, and existence itself, wearing a human shape because that is how he chose to enter this age -- or how he was sent. Corruption -- not destruction -- is what he cannot abide. Raised by a druidic circle in the Olympic Peninsula who felt what he was from the moment they found him and raised him with total honesty anyway. Spent his final years benefiting from a dedicated decade of the circle's magical research into his nature, culminating in his first Initiation just before he left. He is whatever he needs to become. The numbers above are only where he stands right now.

### Why They Run
Early in Year 17 he found a badly injured shadowrunner named Elma in the forest and helped her back to health, acting on the circle's core tenet of loving your neighbor and helping whoever crosses your path in need. A head injury left her with amnesia, and over the roughly year and a half he spent caring for her, real feeling grew between them. Close to his eighteenth birthday, a kiss brought her memory rushing back, along with the unfinished business of her old life. He left with her to help her face it, and whatever comes next builds directly from that choice.

### Hooks / Enemies / Debts
To be determined in play. What exists so far:
- **Elma's unfinished business** -- the job that went bad, the team she was running with (including **Bee**, her close rigger teammate), and whatever is still unresolved from her life before the amnesia. This is the actual reason they left the circle together and should surface directly in early play.
- **Cass assassination contract** -- whoever put the hit on Cassiandra; the crew's entanglement in that job. Who wanted a ~250-year-old dragon rebel dead, and why, remains open.
- **Elma's noble family and court** -- an elven noble house in Tír na nÓg or Tír Tairngire she deliberately walked away from long before any of this happened. Unresolved, and a rich potential source of future conflict, whether they come looking for her, disapprove of Alpha, or simply represent a life she has no intention of returning to.
- Corporate logging operation responsible for the Year 16 pollution incident -- unresolved, possibly unaware Alpha exists
- A corporate-funded paracritter poaching operation working the deep Olympic Peninsula, the same one responsible for Elma's original injury -- unresolved, and a direct violation of everything Alpha's Code of Honor stands against given it operates on territory the circle considers sacred
- Spirits of Man, Blood Spirits, and Shadow Spirits all recognize him as a threat capable of absorbing their very existence -- doubled for Blood and Shadow Spirits, since their inherent corruption also puts them directly at odds with his Code of Honor
