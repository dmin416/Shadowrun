# Shadowrun 5e Mega Bundle

Humble Bundle PDF editions from Catalyst Game Labs. Breakdown of everything in the bundle:

---

## Core

**Shadowrun, Fifth Edition Core Rulebook**
The foundation of everything. The complete core rulebook for SR5, covering all the base rules for the cyberpunk-fantasy Sixth World setting. Every other book below builds on this one.

---

## Player options

**Run Faster** (Player Options)
Expands character creation with more metatypes like hobgoblins, giants, centaurs, and sasquatch; adds new qualities; introduces rules for the Infected; includes expanded contact and lifestyle rules; and offers alternate character creation methods beyond the core priority system.

**Chrome Flesh** (Augmentation)
The SR5 augmentation catalog: cyberware, bioware, genetech, nanotech, and quick chemical boosts. Also covers breakdown/repair, where aug tech is headed, and compiled tables of SR5 augmentations.

**Complete Trog** (Ork and Troll Sourcebook)
The definitive guide for ork and troll characters, covering what life as a trog looks like across the globe, corporate life for orks and trolls, culture, heroes, enemies, gear, and qualities. Compatible with both SR5 and Shadowrun: Anarchy.

---

## Rules expansions

**Street Grimoire** (Magic Rules)
Covers spells, rituals, alchemical preparations, adept powers, metamagics, magical traditions, and magic societies. Essentially the expanded magic rulebook for SR5.

**Forbidden Arcana** (Advanced Magic)
Advanced magic rules expanding beyond what Street Grimoire covers.

**Data Trails** (Matrix Rules)
The extended Matrix supplement, covering hacking, decking, and the digital world in depth.

**Kill Code** (Advanced Matrix)
Advanced Matrix rules that push beyond Data Trails for deeper hacking and decking options.

**Rigger 5.0** (Vehicles and Drones)
The extended vehicle and drone rules supplement, for characters who specialize in piloting and remote-operating machines.

**Howling Shadows** (Critter Bestiary)
The SR5 critter bestiary, a catalog of monsters and creatures that inhabit the Sixth World.

**Run & Gun** (Combat Rules)
Extended combat rules for SR5. Think of it as the first tier of combat expansion.

**Street Lethal** (Advanced Combat)
A further combat options expansion on top of Run & Gun, pushing into more advanced territory.

---

## Campaign / metaplot

CFD five (full arc): Stolen Souls, Lockdown, Dark Terrors, Bloody Business, Market Panic. This bundle includes three of five (missing Lockdown and Bloody Business).

**Stolen Souls** (Plot Sourcebook / Campaign)
The opening book of the CFD (Cognitive Fragmentation Disorder) metaplot, in which new personalities awaken inside victims and fight for control. Includes extraction rules and related tools, plus a detailed look at Manhattan.

**Dark Terrors** (Plot Sourcebook / Campaign)
A later CFD-era horror/plot book. Catalogs Sixth World threats such as bug spirits, shedim, and dark metaplanar and Matrix horrors, with stats and campaign hooks.

**Market Panic** (Campaign Book)
One of the five CFD campaign books. Focuses on the Big Ten megacorps under strain (scandals, disasters, power shifts) and the corporate/economic fallout of that era, with plot hooks for runs for or against them.

---

## Adventure

**Serrated Edge** (Adventure)
The first adventure in the Denver Trilogy, a series of linked missions set in the free city of Denver.

---

## Setting

**Seattle Sprawl** (Setting Boxed Set)
A digital boxed set covering Seattle, the primary setting of Shadowrun. A strong entry point for getting oriented in the game world.
