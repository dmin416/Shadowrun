failure, the Man-Catcher was one of many proj-

with with the latter the compound only has a fifty 
percent chance of working (roll 1D6 with no re-
rolls for Edge; it works on a 4 or better). Weapon 
requires Exotic Ranged Weapon (Man-Catcher)
