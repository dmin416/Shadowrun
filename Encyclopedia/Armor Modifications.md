The weave, linings, pockets, and toys bolted into what you wear. Base armor pieces live in [Armor and Clothing](Armor%20and%20Clothing.md). Capacity for most worn armor equals its **Armor rating**.

---

## Rules

- Mods spend **Capacity** on a single armor piece (or helmet). You cannot exceed that piece’s Capacity.
- Rated mods are usually **Rating 1-6** unless noted.
- Elemental / chem protections **do not stack with themselves** across multiple pieces for the same effect - use the best applicable Rating (GM call if two different vectors matter).
- **Shields** may take Chemical Protection, Fire Resistance, and Nonconductivity (Capacity = shield’s Armor bonus). When armor + shield both have the same protection, only **one** applies (wearer’s choice).
- Full Body Armor **purchase options** (Chemical Seal +6 Avail / +6,000¥; Environmental Adaptation +3 Avail / +1,000¥) are built into the suit at buy time - see [Armor and Clothing](Armor%20and%20Clothing.md). The **Capacity** Chemical Seal below is the installable airtight kit (same idea, different SKU).
- You can also mount ordinary gear into armor Capacity (holsters, biomonitor, medkit, sensors, etc.). Common Capacity costs are listed under **Installed gear**.

## Run & Gun armor features

Printed features on fashion / specialty armor - not Capacity mods you bolt on later. They matter for stacking, social, and movement.

| Feature | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Custom Fit | Cut for one body. Social Limit bonuses from the piece apply only to that wearer. Physical Attribute changes (advancement or augmentation, not magic) require a refit: Armorer shop + Armorer + Logic [Mental] (10, 1 hour) Extended Test, or ship to a contact for 1 week and **25%** of armor cost. |
| Custom Fit (Stack) | Custom Fit rules, plus the piece can stack with a specific set from the same maker for the same person. Use either the piece’s base Armor or the stack **`+N`** bonus with that set (coat customization pattern). |
| Concealability | Built to look like clothes / hide its nature. Keep realism - chaps still don’t eat an assault cannon. |
| Gear Access | Drawing or retrieving items staged on the armor costs **one action step less** (Complex → Simple → Free). Free stays Free (GM may allow an extra Free). |
| Illuminating | Decorative / safety lighting built into the piece. |
| Newest Model | Current-season cut. If bought **Lightly Worn**, also lose **20%** Armor (round up) on top of Lightly Worn’s other penalties. Armor does **not** rot into “old model” over time by itself. |
| Padded | Extra vitals coverage. Called Shot to Vitals modifier is **2 worse** (normally −6 instead of −4). |
| Restrictive | Halve Movement. Fatigue tests on **every** Running check, including the first. |
| Lightly Worn (purchase option) | Needs Armand (or equivalent) Loyalty ≥ 2. **−25%** price, but you get Armor rating **only** - no Features. Restore Features with Armorer + Logic [Mental] (10, 1 hour) Extended Test per Feature at **10%** of original armor cost each. |

## Scope notes

- Helmet / shield Capacity for vision, audio, and the three elemental chem mods follows the same pool rules as worn armor.
- Custom ballistic masks, Nightshade IR contacts, and Rapid Transit detailing packages are armor **options** tied to specific lines - see [Armor and Clothing](Armor%20and%20Clothing.md).
- Pantheon / Kill Code hardpoints and Grey Mana Integration are setting-specific; not cataloged in full here.
