## Rules

- Ammo is bought by **type**, **weapon class** (light pistol, heavy pistol, SMG, assault rifle, etc.), and **cased vs caseless**.
- A gun fires **cased or caseless**, not both (unless its write-up says otherwise).
- Guns of the **same class** share ammo (e.g. all heavy pistols).
- **DV / AP modifiers** stack with the weapon’s base DV and AP unless noted (Stick-n-Shock is the big exception).
- Example: Ares Predator V (8P, AP −1) + Explosive (+1 DV, −1 AP) → **9P, AP −2**.
- Costs below are **per 10 rounds** unless noted.
- Usually **one ammo type per clip**; Tracer is an exception (every third round in FA).

### Capsule rounds

- Hit (including grazing) ruptures capsule → chemical effect.
- Empty rounds sold; fill with Logic + Armorer [Mental] (12, 1 minute) Extended Test **per round**.
- **1 dose** of chemical fills **5** capsule rounds (chem bought separately).
- Always use **Light Pistol** range brackets.

### Tracker rounds

- Damaging hit → tag stays in body until removed.
- Fully resisted → tag in armor.
- Grazing hit → roll Edge; hits embed in armor, zero hits → tag the pavement.

### Hand loads

- Choose **either** +1 DV **or** extra −1 AP (not both).
- Craft **10 rounds**: Armorer Extended Test; needs Hand Loading tool kit.
- Material cost = standard cost of that ammo type **+ 10%**.
