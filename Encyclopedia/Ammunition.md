What you load. Grenades, most rockets beyond the launcher feed table, Maker munitions, and torpedo grenades live in [Grenades and Explosives](Grenades%20and%20Explosives.md) — rockets that are bought as ammo for launchers are summarized here.

---

## Rules

- Buy ammo by **type**, **weapon class** (light pistol, heavy pistol, SMG, assault rifle, etc.), and **cased vs caseless**.
- A gun fires **cased or caseless**, not both (unless its write-up says otherwise).
- Guns of the **same class** share ammo (e.g. all heavy pistols).
- **DV / AP modifiers** stack with the weapon’s base DV and AP unless noted. Stick-n-Shock is the big exception (AP **replaces**).
- Example: Ares Predator V (8P, AP −1) + Explosive (+1 DV, −1 AP) → **9P, AP −2**.
- Costs are **per 10 rounds** unless noted.
- Usually **one ammo type per clip**. Tracer is the classic exception (every third round in FA).
- Some Street Lethal loads are **weapon-locked** (Supermach, Trackstopper foam, Man-Catcher compound, Spinstorm ferrous, gas-gun canisters).

### Capsule rounds

- Hit (including grazing) ruptures the capsule → chemical effect.
- Buy empty; fill with Logic + Armorer [Mental] (12, 1 minute) Extended Test **per round**.
- **1 dose** of chemical fills **5** capsule rounds (chem bought separately).
- Always use **Light Pistol** range brackets regardless of the gun’s normal class.

### Tracker rounds

- Damaging hit → tag stays in the body until removed.
- Fully resisted → tag stuck in armor.
- Grazing hit → roll Edge; hits embed in armor, zero hits → tag the pavement.

### Hand loads

- Choose **either** +1 DV **or** extra −1 AP (not both).
- Craft **10 rounds**: Armorer Extended Test; needs Hand Loading tool kit.
- Material cost = standard cost of that ammo type **+ 10%**.

| Type | Threshold | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular firearms ammo | 4 | 30 minutes |
| Special firearms ammo | 6 | 30 minutes |
| Regular heavy weapon ammo | 8 | 1 hour |
| Special heavy weapon ammo | 10 | 1 hour |

## Scope notes

- **Krime Katalog**, *Sail Away Sweet Sister*, and other niche books add branded specialty rounds (Krime penetrator buckshot, etc.) — not cataloged here.
- **Spare clips / speed loaders** are accessories, not ammo types.
- Hand-load rules are a process applied to another ammo type, not a separate SKU on the street.
