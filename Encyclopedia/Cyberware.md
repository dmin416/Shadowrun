## Rules

### Capacity vs Essence

- Items with Capacity in **`[brackets]`** can be installed in a **cyberlimb** (or cybereye/ear system) for Capacity instead of Essence.
- Items **without** brackets cost Essence and usually cannot go in a limb (bodyware exception: only bracketed bodyware fits in limbs).
- Eye/ear accessories with `[*]` (Image Link, Sound Link) are free Capacity when installed in a cybereye/cyberear of Rating 1+.

### Grades

Same ladder as bioware (Core p. 451; CF Omega / Gamma). Accessories must match the grade of the parent implant.

| Grade | Essence × | Cost × | Avail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Used / Omega | 1.25 / 1 | 0.75 | −4 |
| Standard | 1 | 1 | - |
| Alphaware | 0.8 | 1.2 | +2 |
| Betaware | 0.7 | 1.5 | +4 |
| Deltaware | 0.5 | 2.5 | +8 |
| Gammaware | 0.4 | 5 | +12 |

Adapsin (genetech) further trims Essence multipliers - see [Nanotech and Geneware](Nanotech%20and%20Geneware.md).

### Wireless & DNI

Most cyber has wireless + DNI. Turning wireless **off** drops wireless bonuses (and some Matrix exposure). Datajacks / control rigs still do direct cable work.

### Common incompatibilities

- Bone lacing ↔ bone density / other bone mods.
- Dermal plating ↔ orthoskin / other skin Armor.
- Muscle replacement ↔ muscle aug/toner / other muscle STR/AGI.
- Wired reflexes ↔ other Reaction/Initiative (except wireless Reaction Enhancers stacking rule).
- Skillwires ↔ reflex recorder bioware.
- Cybereyes ↔ natural-eye-only bioware (Cat’s Eyes, Troll Eyes).

## Scope notes

- Full cyberlimb attribute math, damage tracks, and called-shot limb rules: Core Street Gear + Combat.
- Nanoware / nanocybernetics → [Nanotech and Geneware](Nanotech%20and%20Geneware.md).
- Programs, decks, and RCCs as gear (not implants) → their own encyclopedia pages; implanted deck/link still buy the base device.
