## Core lifestyles

Must pick one **primary**. May also maintain additional full-cost lifestyles (alternate identity / safehouse residences).

| Lifestyle | Src | Cost | Full notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Luxury | Core | 100,000¥+/month | Best of everything: ritzy digs, high-tech toys, best food/drink. Household staff, maid service, or sophisticated drones. - After buying gear: at most **5,000¥** unspent Resources carry into play; remaining starting cash = lifestyle roll + leftover.
- Core example: Middle × 12 months = 60,000¥; Low safe house × 3 months = 6,000¥ (secondary prepaid).

### Paying bills (Core)

- Pay each lifestyle’s cost each month. Prepay any number of months; landlords highly unlikely to refund.
- Missed payment: roll **1 die**. If result **greater than** consecutive months of payments missed → landlord lets it slide for now (back payments still owed). If result **≤** months missed → out: secondary lifestyle lost; if primary, **downgrade one lifestyle level** (repossession/seizure/sale of goods + eviction). GM may leave debt to bank, loan company, loan shark, or syndicate.
- Intended as roleplaying opportunity.

### Permanent lifestyle (Core)

- Buy permanent: pay **100 ×** monthly upkeep once (Luxury example: 10,000,000¥). Represents investments/trust funds covering payments.
- Can still lose permanent lifestyle via enemy action or bad luck (hacker shreds investments, enemies destroy holdings, etc.) per story.
- Sell permanent **Middle or better**: with a couple months to broker legitimate deal, roll **2D6**; result × **10%** = percent of purchase price received. Without SINner quality (Core p.84): roll **1D6** instead. Also roll **1D6** if dumping home/possessions fast or through an agent while on the run.

### Team lifestyles (Core)

- Joint lifestyle: **+10%** cost per additional person.
- Purchasing **Low or higher**: one member is **tenant of record** (liable if team misses payments).

### Core lifestyle options

Allowed on any lifestyle **except Street or Hospitalized**. GM may disallow if implausible for the described residence or if listed bonus/penalty could never matter.

| Option | Src | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Special Work Area | Core | +1,000¥/month | Large specialized workspace (workshop, garage, office, studio, etc.) set up “just the way you like it.” Skill checks relevant to that setting: Limit **+2** in the space. |
| Extra Secure | Core | +20% of lifestyle | Security above average for the area (wall, vigilant contractors, altruistic gang, nearby powerful protector, etc.). All High Threat Response (Core p.356) and other security response rolls made **one level better** than the area would normally be. |
| Obscure / Difficult to Find | Core | +10% of lifestyle | Hard to find / obscure part of city; people need directions/map and still get lost. Any Sneaking skill checks in the vicinity of the residence (owner excluded): **−2**. |

## RF optional expanded system

Optional (RF Game Information). Adds depth to lifestyles.

### Categories

Four categories: **Comforts & Necessities (C&N)**, **Security (S)**, **Neighborhood (N)**, **Entertainment (E)**.

### Point economy

- Each lifestyle: preset C&N/S/N with **[limits]** + **Pts**.
- Cannot lower categories unless switching to a lower lifestyle (or a negative option that reduces level/limit as written).
- Raise C&N, S, or N with points; cannot exceed that category’s limit.
- Entertainment has **no level**; spend points on specific Entertainment entries.
- Not all points need spending.
- Each point spent on C&N, S, or N: monthly cost **+10% of base lifestyle** (Street exception below).
- Entertainment: listed monthly ¥ and Pts; if purchased lifestyle’s base expense **≥** item Min LS, ¥ cost is covered by lifestyle (points still required). Min LS **None** = always pay ¥.
- Lifestyle options: get more points and/or reduce categories; some positives raise limits (noted per option). Negatives cannot reduce a category below **0**. Cap from options: up to **2×** starting Pts.
- Some lifestyles include built-in options.
- Entertainment advantages only while **actively using** that domicile (not while living elsewhere / other city on a job).
- Each Entertainment generally once per domicile unless entry says otherwise.

### Maintaining add-ons (RF)

- If full lifestyle + add-ons unaffordable: drop add-ons (stop their monthly payments).
- Assets paid in advance: unpaid at month start → unavailable for entire month.
- Drop point-bought asset: points **not** returned; may resume payments later to regain it.
- Switch assets: buy a new lifestyle configuration with needed assets.

### Bolt hole vs safehouse (definitions)

- **Bolt hole:** Prearranged, cash-only paid space used when heat is on; usually set up in advance and left sitting; not a vacation home (cargo crate, rations, chemical toilet typical). Few people may share but confinement strains. **Lifestyle tier** below.
- **Safehouse:** Temporary out-of-way home (trid version); often controlled by house master/madame maintaining a façade; step up from bolt hole. Corps/govs: WITSEC or runners babysitting assets. **Positive option** below (+500¥/month, tracking penalty, house rules).

## RF category rules

### Comforts & Necessities

- Most session starts: resist Fatigue damage (Core p.172) for rest quality at lifestyle domicile. Skip if session continues prior adventure and PCs have not rested at that domicile.
- Base Fatigue DV **6S**; each C&N level **−2 DV**.
- Environmental allergies (pollution, UV, plastic, etc.): if lifestyle C&N **≤ 3**, add Fatigue DV **+1 per allergy level above Mild** for **common** allergies, or **+1 per level above Moderate** for **uncommon** allergies.
- Food allergies to common foodstuffs (soy, mycoprotein, etc.): if C&N **≤ 3**, Fatigue DV **+1 per level above Mild**. For uncommon food allergies: **+1** DV if Severe, **+2** if Extreme.
- RF example: Street C&N 0 + moderate pollution (+1) + severe soy (+2) → resist **9S**.

### Security

- Deters unwanted guests/crime other than the runner’s. Higher lifestyle → physical location can store/protect gear.
- Example burglary while away: opposed **(Security level × 2)** vs burglars’ Sneaking + Agility. If Security wins: officers noticed, alarm triggered, or other measure stops them without PC action. See also Core ~p.355.
- If item Availability **> 2 × Security level**, someone/something may notice and want it; greater discrepancy → more likely targeted. GM roleplays.
- Alternate abstract drain: dice pool = (minimum Security of lifestyle required for the goods − actual Security purchased). Each hit → pay **1%** of monthly lifestyle cost to replace stolen goods, repair damage, or pay insurance. Nothing exceedingly valuable vanishes this way (use Security Test for that).

### Neighborhood

- Abstract acceptable behavior, safety, atmosphere. Lone Star-style zone ratings AAA-Z. Combined with Security, GM/player picture the area.
- Example: D Zone taken over by gangers/Mafia.
- Fit of runner activities: guns/drugs in zones below **C** may not raise eyebrows; **A** or above, someone probably reports. GM may raise lifestyle cost for bribes to look the other way.

### Entertainment

- Perks that entertain, ease life, or provide people/places. Each has Pts + monthly ¥ + Min LS.
- Types: **Assets** (physical characteristics of domicile or nearby facilities); **Services**; **Outings** (neighborhood opportunities).
- Advantages require actively using the domicile.
