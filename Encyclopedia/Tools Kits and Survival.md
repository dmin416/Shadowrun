## Common rules

- **Skill-specific tools (Core):** Kit, Shop, and Facility are each bought for **one** skill (e.g. Armorer kit, Disguise kit, Automotive Mechanic shop, Nautical Mechanic facility, Demolitions kit, Hardware kit, Industrial Mechanic kit). They are not universal.
- **Kit vs Shop vs Facility (Core):** Kit = portable; basic gear for standard **repairs** for one skill. Shop = transportable in a van; more advanced tools for **building and repairing**; stocked with standard spare parts. Facility = immobile building with bulky/heavy machines for very advanced **constructions and modifications**; also stocked with standard spare parts. GM may still require unusual parts bought separately. Core points to Building & Repairing (p.145) for when a tier is needed, but that section does not print a kit/shop/facility necessity table; GM has final say.
- **Building & Repairing (Core):** Extended Test; GM sets threshold/interval (Task Difficulty / Extended Test tables p.48). Designing new items needs appropriate Knowledge skills for conceptualization/schematics. Apply Build/Repair pool modifiers (Shared procedures).
- **Flashlight variants (Core):** Survival flashlight text says low-light and infrared versions exist (reduce visibility modifiers for low-light / thermographic respectively) and that a flashlight can mount on a weapon top or underbarrel mount. Core prints **no separate Avail/Cost rows** for LL/IR survival flashlights. Do not invent prices. Some RF PACKs list Flashlight (low-light) as a component without a standalone Core price. RnG recessed weapon flashlights (Standard / Low-light / Infrared) are Weapon Accessories SKUs with their own costs.
- **Armor Capacity installs (RnG):** Climbing gear [5], Flashlight [1], Survival kit [6], Tool kit [6], Gas mask [2], Respirator [2], Micro flare launcher [1], GPS [1], Wire Clippers [1], Internal Air Tank [1 per hour of air]. Cap cost only; buy the Street Gear item separately when it exists. GPS, Wire Clippers, and armor Internal Air Tank have **no Core Avail/Cost buy rows** (do not invent prices). Cyberware Internal Air Tank is a separate implant (Cyberware).
- **Assisted climbing (Core):** Climbing gear and gecko tape gloves (when dry) enable assisted climbing. Full movement, modifiers, rappelling, and safety-catch rules are under Shared procedures -> Climbing and rappelling.
- **PACK-priced only (no Core buy row):** Roll of duct tape 5¥ (Mechanic Shop PACK); Backpack 20¥ (Intro/Basic/Advanced Runner PACKs). No Avail printed; no further mechanical rules printed.

### Climbing and rappelling (Core)

Assisted climbing uses specialized gear (ropes/harnesses such as Climbing gear, or gecko tape gloves when dry). Easier/safer than unassisted, but needs more preparation. Unassisted climbing needs no gear.

Climbing is a Complex Action. Distance moved = hits on Gymnastics + Strength [Physical], per the Climbing Table. Surface difficulty applies as dice pool modifiers.

| Situation | Movement Distance (round up) |
| --- | --- |
| Assisted climbing down (rappelling) | 20 m + 1 m per hit |
| Assisted climbing upward | 1 m per hit |
| Assisted climbing horizontally | 1 m per 2 hits |
| Assisted climbing upside down (ceiling/overhang) | 1 m per 3 hits |
| Unassisted climbing upward | 1 m per 2 hits |
| Unassisted climbing down | 1 m per hit |

| Situation | Dice Pool Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Assisted climbing | +2 |
| Surface easily climbable (e.g. chain-link fence) | +1 |
| Surface broken (tree, loose stone wall) | +0 |
| Surface flat (brick wall, old building side) | -3 |
| Surface sheer (metal wall, seamless stone) | -5 |
| Surface slippery or wet | -2 |
| Surface greased or gel-treated | -4 |

**Rappelling:** Free-Fall + Body [Physical] (2). Simple Action; fall 20 m per Combat Turn; +1 m that turn per net hit beyond threshold. May brake/slow/land safely with proper gear and training. Taking another Simple Action in the same phase: -2 to that action and the Free-Fall Test. Stopping: another Free-Fall (2); fail = keep descending and may take Falling Damage (p.172) on impact.

**Failures:** Failed climb/rappel test halts progress; Reaction + Strength to hold on. Fail that = fall (~20 m per Combat Turn). Next Action Phase may try to stop: Reaction + Strength (-2). Other climbers may try Reaction + Strength to grab you (GM).

**Climbing-gear safety catch:** If using climbing gear and you fall, the character who prepared the safety lines rolls Free-Fall + Logic [Mental] vs threshold = half the falling character's Body (round down). Falling character may spend Edge on this test. Success = gear catches (dangling). Fail = Falling Damage (p.172).

### Disguise kit (Core)

A Disguise Kit is a Kit bought for the Disguise skill. Build the disguise with Building & Repairing (Extended Test). Add **half** the Extended Test threshold as a dice pool bonus to the Disguise Test, maximum bonus +4.

### Grapple gun use (Core)

Shoot with Exotic Ranged Weapon; Light Crossbow ranges. Internal winch pulls the grapple back (or lifts small loads). Micro rope supports ≤100 kg; standard and stealth ropes support ≤400 kg. Microwire without rappelling gloves: 8P damage, AP -8 to hands.

### Myomeric rope (Core)

Remote-controlled fiber; max controlled length 30 m; moves 2 m per Combat Turn (snake around obstacles, tie to ledges, etc.). Also used as the controllable segment on Endoscope / Mage Sight Goggles (Sensors and Optics).
