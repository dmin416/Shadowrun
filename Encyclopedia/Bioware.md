Living upgrades - vat-grown organs, woven muscle, cultured neural tissue. Subtler and usually cheaper on Essence than chrome, pricier in nuyen, and **no wireless**. Genetech / transgenic treatments live in [Nanotech and Geneware](Nanotech%20and%20Geneware.md).

---

## Rules

### Basic vs cultured

- **Basic** - off-the-shelf / Type-O style. Faster to source.
- **Cultured** - grown for *your* genome. Higher cost and lead time; better integration (limb replacements match your attributes immediately).

### Grades

Bioware uses the same grade ladder as cyberware (Core p. 451; CF adds Omega / Gamma). Apply Essence and cost multipliers to the listed Standard values; Avail shifts stack with the item’s own Avail.

| Grade | Essence × | Cost × | Avail | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Used / Omega | 1.25 / 1 | 0.75 | −4 | Used = Core secondhand; Omega = CF cheap knockoff class |
| Standard | 1 | 1 | - | Listed stats assume this |
| Alphaware | 0.8 | 1.2 | +2 | |
| Betaware | 0.7 | 1.5 | +4 | |
| Deltaware | 0.5 | 2.5 | +8 | Clinic / mil-spec pipeline |
| Gammaware | 0.4 | 5 | +12 | Chrome Flesh bleeding edge |

### Incompatibilities (common)

- Muscle Aug / Toner ↔ other Strength / Agility boosters (incl. muscle replacement cyber).
- Orthoskin ↔ other skin Armor (dermal plating, etc.).
- Bone Density ↔ other bone augments (bone lacing).
- Synaptic Booster / Boosted Reflexes ↔ other Reaction or Initiative enhancements.
- Reflex Recorder ↔ skillwires.
- Cat’s Eyes / Troll Eyes ↔ cyber eyes and most other eye mods.
- Attribute bonuses from bioware still count toward the **+4 augmented** attribute cap.

## Scope notes

- **Genetech** (transgenics, phenotype scrub, genetic restoration, exotic metagenics, environmental microadaptation, complimentary genetics) → [Nanotech and Geneware](Nanotech%20and%20Geneware.md).
- Installation time, clinic quality, and essence hole / leftover rules follow Core augmentation surgery and CF clinic chapters.
- Bioware has **no Device Rating and no wireless bonus** - scanners still exist; subtle ≠ invisible.
