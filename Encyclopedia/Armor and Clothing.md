Modern ballistic fabrics (spiderweave, ceramictitanium composites, liquid packs, ruthenium coats) make armor light enough to look like clothes — or heavy enough to look like walking tanks. This entry covers **what you wear**. Capacity upgrades live in [Armor Modifications](Armor%20Modifications.md).

## Rules

### Damage resistance

Armor is added to **Body** on Damage Resistance tests. The attack’s **AP** modifies Armor for that test. If AP drops Armor to 0 or below, you roll **Body only** (you do not subtract from Body).

If an attack dealing **Physical** damage has modified DV **less than** AP-modified Armor, the damage becomes **Stun** instead (trauma plates / padding doing their job).

### Stacking & encumbrance

- Only the **highest** worn armor rating counts as primary Armor.
- Items listed with **`+N`** (helmets, shields, PPP kits, etc.) are **accessories** — they add their bonus to Armor for resistance tests.
- Accessory bonus total is capped by **Strength**. For every **2 full points** the bonus exceeds Strength, suffer **−1 Agility and Reaction**.
- Coats listed **A/+B** (e.g. 10/+3): use **A** alone as primary armor, or wear over other armor for **+B** (coat customization).

### Capacity

Worn armor’s Capacity for mods is usually equal to its Armor rating (exceptions noted). Specialized protections (fire, insulation, nonconductivity, etc.) share that Capacity pool.

### Shields

Shields are armor accessories (`+`). They impose **−2** on physical tests while in use and can be used as melee weapons (Clubs / appropriate skill). They are hard to conceal and scream “security” or “riot.”

### Hardened armor

**Hardened Mil-Spec Battle Armor** uses Hardened Armor rules (same family as vehicle / critter hardened armor):

1. Compare modified DV to Hardened Armor (after AP). If DV does **not** beat that rating, the attack does **no damage** (no resistance roll).
2. If it does beat it, resist as normal with Body + Armor, and add automatic hits equal to **half** Hardened Armor (after AP, round up).

Mil-spec suits are also **Restrictive**, usually **Custom Fit**, and often grant a visible Intimidation Social Limit bump.
