### Repair rules

- A vehicle or drone is totaled when its Condition Monitor fills.
- Spare parts cost 5% of base cost per box repaired. Short parts: interval 1 day, -4 dice per missing box-worth.
- Scavenge: inflict chosen boxes on a similar target, no resistance; Mechanic + Logic [damage inflicted], 1 box parts per 2 hits.
- Need at least a toolkit. A shop/facility may be needed by size. Repair: Extended Mechanic + Logic [Mental], threshold = boxes, interval 4 hours, or 1 day while short on parts.

| Condition | Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| Distracting / Poor / Bad / Terrible | -1 / -2 / -3 / -4 |
| Superior conditions | +1 |
| Unavailable tools or parts | -4 |
| Superior tools or parts | +1 or more |

### Swarms and operation

RCC running **Swarm**, 600¥, may slave Device Rating x3 drones and split them among swarms. A swarm acts as one drone: Pilot is highest member Pilot or RCC Device Rating, uses highest autosofts/Sensor and lowest Handling/Speed/Acceleration, and adds drones minus 1 to dice pools and limits. Use one weapon profile for the volley, scatter explosives separately. Target drones individually and update bonuses immediately as drones are lost.

Normal operating time is about 6 hours, longer idle and shorter at sustained top speed. Track narratively. Takeoff/landing: Long 2,000/1,800 m; Short 200/300 m; VTOL none/none.

### Drone modification rules

Mod Points equal Body. `Body X(Y)` is Body X with Y MP free after factory gear. The first +1 attribute or +3 Armor is free; further increases cost MP equal to increase minus 1. Body cannot rise. Attributes cap at 2x starting value, treating 0 as 0.5. One downgrade gives +1 MP: reduce an attribute by 1 or Armor by 3. A Body reduction of 1 grants +2 MP, net +1, and may not reduce Body below half its starting value.

## 14 - Maximum Pursuit

**Story/hooks:** Reylen's milk-run is ambushed by rotodrones; this chapter offers advanced chase rules. See [14 - Maximum Pursuit](14%20-%20Maximum%20Pursuit.md).

### Chase framework

End a chase when a vehicle escapes beyond Extreme, Handling reaches 0, Acceleration and Speed reach 0, or its Condition Monitor fills. Every Combat Turn take Control Vehicle as Complex or become Out of Control: occupants take -2 dice next CT; autopilot/GridGuide takes over at CT end, otherwise it crashes eventually. Vehicle actions are Vehicle Skill + Reaction [Handling] tests, usually Complex, with Terrain plus stated modifiers. Speed can rise up to Acceleration or decrease by 1 each CT.

### Stunts and mixed classes

Choose Stunt rating. Alone: Complex Vehicle Skill + Reaction [Handling] (rating + Terrain). Added: rating increases action threshold. Failure immediately requires Vehicle Test at rating penalty or crashes. Benefits: rating as range-change hits, soak dice, enemy Speed/Handling reduction, attack avoidance hits, Handling for CT, or dice to a witnessed Social test. Custom crazy stunt costs Edge. Across CT, no more than +4 dice or limit above 2x.

Aircraft use Speed x3 for modifiers. Scale terrain by size difference. Target size: Micro -3, Mini -2, Small -1, Body <=8 0, Body 9-14 +1, 15-20 +2, >20 +3, adding +2 Body per 10 seats. Moving vehicle imposes -Speed dice against pedestrian/stationary attacks. Vehicle movement multipliers: Water x0.8, Ground x1, Rotor x3, Jet x4.
