## 03 - Astral Sea

**Story/hooks:** Magister frames Dr. Gordon's logs, Halley's Comet "dreamwalking," navigational artifacts, and the Al Azif as the path into mapping the astral sea beyond the Gaiasphere.

**Mechanics (travel notes):**
- Astral sea is the threshold crossed from the astral plane; bridges metaplanes (and theoretically other biospheres) without traversing the void between spheres.
- Entering requires mental preparation and perspective: less prep than facing the Dweller, greater risk.
- Formulas in Al Azif (translated) are said to construct navigational tools for the journey.

**Items (named, no stats):** Al Azif; navigational artifacts (sextant, compass, map); Dendra Schematics; Piri Reis map; orichalcum pages of the Gaf manuscript.

## 04 - Metaplanes

**Story/hooks:** Magister's (and Gordon's) tour of metaplanes, hyper/bubble/fractured terminology, physical-world phenomena sites, and deep-metaplane horrors usable as destinations, patrons, or MacGuffins.

### Terminology (GM tools)

| Term | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Fractured metaplane | Skewed by intersection with another plane/physical realm; easy (sometimes accidental) crossing; may be hard to find or act as an astral shallow in both realms |
| Hyper-metaplane | Persistent "island" within a larger metaplane (Avalon, Guinee, etc.); skilled navigators can reach beyond tradition-default destinations |
| Bubble metaplane | Short-lived plane conjured for a traveler (e.g. Dweller quests); no known navigation to/from; lifespan tied to the visit |

### Astral sea travel (flavor mechanics)

- Thresholds apply leaving the physical/astral to the sea; metaplanes transition more like coastlines.
- Without navigational tools, travelers can be lost indefinitely.
- Astral form on the sea approximates physical appearance and capabilities (no free mental-pace travel); spirit mounts help speed.

## 06 - Rules

**Story/hooks:** Mechanical appendix for the phenomena and new spirits described in earlier chapters.

### Astral Rift

- Barrier thinned into a bridge to a metaplane; Awakened and mundanes can astrally project and cross.
- Uninitiated magicians may travel without the Dweller (SR5 p. 317).
- Unstable rifts: if closed, Awakened must find another way back; Awakened may remain on the metaplane without ceasing to exist (SR5 p. 314).
- **Mundanes** are bound to the rift to return. If the body leaves the rift area or the rift closes, they fade in a number of **hours equal to Essence**.

### Astral Shallows

- Thin barrier: mundanes can perceive astral like a window.
- Awakened who can astrally perceive: switch astral/normal vision as a **Free Action** (normally Simple).
- Can look at astral forms but not touch unless dual natured (perception/projection).
- Usually temporary (hours/days); geomancers can make permanent "arcane windows."

### Background Count

- Scale **-24 to +24** (0 = normal). Negative = ebbs/voids; positive = aspected domains/warps.
- Use **absolute value** for magic effects unless noted. See *Street Grimoire* p. 30.

### Mana Storm

- See *Street Grimoire* p. 36.

### The Mist (Brittany)

- Positive background count aspected to Fae spirits; **Force 3-10** (varies by location).
- **Noise** += Mist Force; Mist Force as modifier to ranged attacks and Perception.
- Confusion-like effect: Spellcasting Test **Force x 2 [Force]**.
- Movement power vs living: Mist Magic = Force; vs vehicles **Force x 2** (SR5 p. 399; Mist Willpower 0).
- Composure Tests: **+2 threshold**; every hour in Mist, Composure Test (SR5 p. 152).
- Spirits want to flee; **-1** to all active tests per spirit that must act in Mist; magician may reassert control (SR5 p. 301); on fail, no services until out of Mist or spend an extra service.
- Force **6+**: can open astral rifts (including deep rifts).

### Daoineann Draoidheil

- Permanent mana+physical storms aspected to Druidic tradition.
- Force **8-12**, equal positive background count.
- Mana storm qualities; do not dissipate; stay put.
- **All** spells scatter (not only indirect combat; SR5 p. 283); preparations scatter when triggered; GM picks closest target if needed.

### Foveae

- Moving mana voids; Force **7-12**, equal negative background count.
- Astral tornado ~Force km tall.
- After pass: background count = weeks for area to recover reagents (SR5 p. 317).
- Preparations: potency **-1 every half hour** (fixated: every 6 hours instead of daily).
- Last ≤24 hours; Force **-1 every 2 hours**; below Force 4 stop and become temporary mana ebb (recover +1 Force / 2 hours).

### Maya Cloud

- Special mana storm; Force / positive BC **14-16**; dome ~5 million km², up to 100 km thick.
- Aspected to magic unique to specific artifacts.
- Force as Perception DP modifier through cloud; Force as Noise to signals/devices.
- Perpetual ice, lightning, snow outside; hazard to all but sub-orbital flight.

### The Veil

- Permanent storm ring; inverted rain; Force **12-14**, positive BC attuned to certain Awakened elves.
- ~600 km diameter, 20 m thick.
- Sustains **Chaotic World** at Force = Veil Force.
- Confuses travelers without electronic/auto navigation; exit not where intended.

### Void

- Permanent no-mana points; Force **13-20**, equal negative BC.
- Size ~500 m² to ~4 km diameter (Cattenom: 6 km, Force 20).
- Perimeter fluctuates up to ~10 m.
- Reagents instantly drained; foci left **Force hours** (active or not) permanently destroyed.

### Free spirit movement (default)

Unless noted: Walk Agility x 2, Run Agility x 4; Running skill +2 m/hit sprint. Inhabiting a vessel uses vessel mobility.

### Spirit Powers

#### Shift

- **Type:** M | **Action:** Complex | **Range:** Self | **Duration:** Special
- Change to a specific animal or metahuman form and back. Attributes carry over; internal deltaware keeps; non-deltaware rejected: **(Essence cost of lost implants x 10, round down)P**, Essence not recovered. Cosmetics (tattoos, piercings) lost without injury.
- Gains non-paranormal racial abilities (Armor, Natural Weapons, Enhanced Senses). Keeps paranormal powers. No -2 sustain. Clothing/gear destroyed by change.

#### Skill

- Spirit may take an active skill instead of a power (named with the power). Not Matrix; not Conjuring, Enchanting, or Resonance groups.

#### Transfer Energy (Essence)

- **Type:** P | **Action:** Complex | **Range:** Touch | **Duration:** Permanent
- Pass drained Energy (Essence, Magic, or Force) to another via Spirit Pact.
- If recipient cannot drain that energy themselves: can only boost the matching attribute; cannot use Force if they lack it; temporary boost lasts **24 hours**.
- If they cannot drain it themselves: addiction risk (SR5 p. 414); Addiction Rating = energy value transferred.

#### Vanishing

- **Type:** M | **Action:** Free | **Range:** Self | **Duration:** Special
- Disappear from physical and astral at will; no known stop (barriers fail). Cameras can record disappearance.
- Reappear as Complex Action; may materialize directly or appear in astral. Speculated destination: fey-native metaplanes.

#### Korrigan Pact

- Magical link like conjurer-spirit bond: Korrigan can find the mage physically and astrally anywhere; bypass wards via the pact.
- They do not leave until the deal is complete (possibly beyond death).
