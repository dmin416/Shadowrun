## 06 - Opposition Report: CorpSec

### Qualities (positive)

**Corporate Loyalist (7 Karma):** +1 social/Composure with your corp; +2 to resist being turned against your corp.

**Location Attunement (5 / 7 / 9 Karma):** Pick a regularly visited location. +2 Perception (and Surprise) there. Size: 5 = shop/small house; 7 = large house/small complex; 9 = up to 0.5 km². Away >2 weeks: +1; >1 month: lost (up to 1 month on-site to restore). Stackable.

**Natural Leader (5 Karma):** +1 Leadership and Teamwork connected to Combat Maneuver tests.

**Observant (5 Karma):** Observe In Detail is a Free Action.

### Qualities (negative)

**Stay Out of My Way (9 Karma):** -1 social except direct superiors; opportunity to backstab: Composure (3) or do it.

**Corporate Pariah (7 or 10):** 7 = keep corp SIN, -2 social with corp; 10 = fired, -3 with former coworkers / that corp’s Johnsons.

**This Is Your Last Chance (3):** Narrative one-more-mistake firing; define trigger with GM.

## 09 - Lethal Arts

### Framework

**Mixed Unit Tactics (Knowledge):** Coordinates multi-unit maneuvers; recognizes enemy tactics. Default Intuition. Prereq: Small Unit Tactics + Leadership. Specs by unit type (Aerial, Armored Infantry, Artillery, Naval, Mechanized Infantry, Standard Infantry).

**Test:** Optional. Commander rolls MUT + Intuition [Mental]; other leaders contribute teamwork. Order before combat = Free; in combat = Complex. May stack with Small-Unit Tactics (*R&G* p. 99). Hold actions for same IP. Fail: defensive penalty = maneuver’s normal bonus. Team-leader glitch: that unit only out of position. Crit glitch: fail. Counter: kill C2 / jam / Opposed MUT. PI-Tac bonuses apply. Small groups use SUT; multi-unit uses MUT.
