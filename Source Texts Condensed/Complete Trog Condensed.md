### Positive qualities

#### Black Forest Native

**Cost:** 22 Karma  
Home Ground: You Know a Guy **and** Street Politics. Choose one: +2 when casting preferred Druidic spells (Camouflage, Control Pack, Invisibility, Resist Pain, Stunball) **or** +2 Infiltration and Survival in wooded areas.

#### Community Connection

**Cost:** 5 Karma (orks/trolls)  
Maintain Squatter lifestyle free, or Low at half cost (1,000¥/month orks; 2,000¥/month trolls).

#### Delicate Fingers

**Cost:** 5 Karma (trolls / troll variants only)  
No -2 for unadapted gear (SR5 p. 420).

#### Human Lifespan

**Cost:** 10 Karma (chargen only)  
Ork ages/matures at human rate. No other effect.

#### Nasty Trog

**Cost:** 5 Karma  
+3 Intimidation vs all non-orks and non-trolls (includes ork/troll variants as "non" for this purpose: i.e. vs other metatypes).

#### Trog Artisan

**Cost:** 8 Karma  
+2 Artisan when making items designed specifically for orks or trolls (not merely usable by them).

#### Trog Historian

**Cost:** 4 Karma  
+2 Knowledge tests involving a significant ork or troll historical event.

#### Trog Leader

**Cost:** 7 Karma (orks/trolls / variants)  
+2 Leadership when the group is entirely orks and/or trolls.

#### Trog Networker

**Cost:** 12 Karma (orks/trolls / variants)  
In neighborhoods majority of your metatype: +2 Etiquette, +1 Navigation.

---

### Negative qualities

#### Bad Credit

**Bonus:** 8 Karma  
Lifestyle costs +10%; treat gear Availability as +1 (not during chargen gear buys).

#### Elevated Stress

**Bonus:** 8 Karma  
-1 dice on Addiction and Toxin Resistance tests.

#### Force of Chaos

**Bonus:** 4 Karma  
Bonuses from tactics / group combat maneuvers halved (round up).

#### Trog Traitor

**Bonus:** 5 Karma  
Notoriety counts as +5 higher in ork- or troll-majority neighborhoods.
