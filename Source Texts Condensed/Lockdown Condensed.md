# Game Information (full mechanics)

## Overview: two CFD strains

Lockdown mixes **standard CFD** (slow overwrite, hides among normals) and a **Cerberus/Lockdown strain** (fast, violent, meant for a dragon). Many QZ head cases are the new strain (ragers, shamblers, plus rare intact personalities). Mainstream head cases often try to hide or control the ferals.

Infection is a major, lasting PC change. **No known full cure** in-universe yet. GMs should be careful who gets infected.

---

## The Standard CFD Virus

Not a biological virus. Infects machines (especially high Data Processing/Pilot) and meatsacks. Primary function: write Matrix Entities (AIs, e-ghosts, amalgam code) into hosts. Evo Dickens Program and NeoNET Project Imago are primary canon sources; other corps may have smaller programs.

### Nanite Volume (NV) and Matrix Entity Concentration (MEC)

| Source | NV (cumulative) |
|--------|-----------------|
| Nanohive | + Rating |
| Nanite system | + 1/2 Rating |
| Genetech | +1 per 2 genetic modifications |

**MEC:** GM sets concentration/cohesion of the dominant Matrix Entity in the nanites. One strong ME overwrites faster; many MEs make amalgam/crazy rides.

### Infection process (biological)

Weekly **Resistance Test:** Opposed **Charisma + Essence vs NV + MEC**.

- Character net hits = finite **Control Dice Pool** for that week (spend dice on Control Tests; pool shrinks when used).
- Virus net success: **Willpower -1** (permanent until raised with Karma). At Willpower 0, AI owns the body.

**Control Tests:** Simple **Composure (4)**. Fail: lose control for **(4 - hits)d6 hours**.

Minimum: **daily** Control Test. Extra tests when:

- 4+ boxes from a single attack
- 6+ boxes total (P or S)
- Unconsciousness (AI **auto** gains control; flee via Adrenal Control)
- Fatigue situations

Injured: CFD personality flees if it takes over. KO: AI wakes and flees.

Electronics/computers: infection speed is GM fiat.

### Nanite reproduction

Unlike normal nanotech decay (-1/month, often recycled), CFD nanites reproduce:

- Soft nanites: +1 NV/week if host eats food
- Hard nanites: +1 NV/week if host eats inorganic materials

### "Cures" (none complete)

| Method | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Hunter-killer nanites | **Worse.** CFD infects H-Ks. Each application **+1 NV** |
| Spells vs nanites | Drain **+2** min; often fail (too small, too processed) |
| Chemicals | Soft nanites only; hard nanites shrug nonlethal doses |
| Medical extraction | NV **halved (round up)**, min 1; extracted nanites can infect others |
| Combined treatments | Best delay: e.g. electricity stun + chemical + scrub. Creative GMs |

### Head case advantages (when CFD personality controls)

**Attribute Boost:** Simple Action. Roll **NV**. Distribute hits as +Physical Attributes for (hits) rounds. Then take Stun equal to hits.

**Toxin Resistance:** Add **NV** to Toxin Resistance or damage resistance vs illness/toxin.

**Adrenal Control:**

- Physical CM full: each Combat Round, **Willpower + NV (2)** to stay up (overflow still applies).
- Stun CM full: roll **NV**; remove Stun equal to hits for (hits) minutes. When time ends, all removed damage returns **+1 box per test made** (can overflow to Physical/death). If overwrite incomplete, indigenous personality may resurface after.

## Lockdown CFD (Cerberus strain)

### Infection and resistance

Designed short-term so Cerberus would not write into everyone forever.

**First 72 hours** after exposure: nanites are virally active (same as mainstream CFD rules) except **test frequency and side effects**.

Once infected: **hourly Resistance Tests for 48 hours**.

- If Willpower not dropped to 0 by end of infection stage: nanites die off rapidly. Lost Willpower stays (buy back with Karma).
- If takeover succeeds: nanites stop overwriting and **rebuild** the mind.

Nanites concentrate in/around the brain (Matrix network). After infection course: virulence mostly only in brain. Later infection sources:

1. Nanites that never finished a host course (surfaces, clothes, MIT&T storage vats)
2. **Brain matter spatter**

### Head case varieties (new strain)

1. **Intact:** Clean overwrite; Cerberus/Deus spectrum personality; rare, cunning
2. **Nanohive/heavy nano/extreme exposure:** Personality installs but wilder, chaotic, aggressive (not feral)
3. **Damaged:** Shamblers and ragers (bulk of Boston infected)

### Cures / resistance notes (Lockdown)

| Method | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Infected (HMHVV)** | Incompatible with CFD; death or resistance |
| **Overwriter H-K nanites** (NeoNET/Celedyr) | No wireless (uninfectable). Early-stage only. Can reduce **NV to 0** and stop infection |
| **Shambler/rager suppressed minds** | Original personality still aware; may surface on sleep/KO (rare, traumatized) |
| **Evo Mars / Overwriter combo (future)** | Rewrite personality (incl. buried original); originals may be damaged or gone |
| **Anecdotal willpower cure** | Rare reports of throwing off invasive personality after long struggle; unverified |

### Hacking the minds of madness

New head cases are always Matrix-active via nanites. Noise limits range; groups can push through. Use **technomancer-like** rules with Head Case Matrix Attributes.

**Head Case Matrix Attributes**

| Attribute | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Attack | Willpower + 1/2 NV |
| Sleaze | Logic + 1/2 NV |
| Data Processing | Intuition + 1/2 NV |
| Firewall | Charisma + 1/2 NV |

Some advanced types (manipulators, haters, hidden) can reconfigure mental-deck attributes like a cyberdeck.

**Hack levels:**

1. Surface thoughts (often "kill"/"eat"; sometimes orders)
2. Sensory feed (all senses; hot-sim shares wound/fatigue mods, hunger, pain; taste is awful)
3. Motor control (puppet; breath-hold/self-harm resisted: hacker **Data Processing vs target Willpower**). VCR rigger can jump in

Hackers/riggers take **dumpshock** if the head case dies while linked/controlled.

### Evolution abilities (all head cases, GM discretion)

**Faster Healing:** Healing Tests every **5 min** (Stun) / **2 hours** (Physical); add **NV** to test. Need **1 kg animal flesh per CM box** healed (ghoul-like atmosphere).

**Rapid Healing:** Spend 1 Body or Strength; gain pool of **(original Attribute x 10)** Physical boxes to repair. Heal up to **NV boxes/IP for 4 IPs** (even without Initiative left). Replenish spent Body/Strength by eating **10 kg** protein + Healing Tests vs attr loss. Stacks with Faster Healing.

**Damage Resistance:** Physical damage immediately reduced by **1/2 NV (round down)**. Stun unaffected. Electricity overcomes this; after Stun fills, Electricity overflow Physical is **not** reduced.

**No Wound Too Great:** Extremely hard to permanently kill; nanites can rebuild (even face). **Electricity** shorts nanites during repair (Damage Resistance off) and often kills host too.

**Inside the mind:** Original personality is a tortured passenger; madness is common escape.

## Medical Miracles (drugs)

### Pinpoint

- Vector: Injection / Inhalation / Ingestion
- Speed: Immediate / 1 CT / 5 min
- Duration: (12 - Body) hours, min 2 hours
- Addiction: Psychological
- Effect: +2 Intuition, +1 Mental limit; Perception not using Observe in Detail: **-10**
- Crash: -3 Intuition, -2 Mental limit for same duration as effect

### Numb

- Vector: Injection
- Speed: Immediate
- Duration: 10 x 1d6 minutes
- Addiction: Physiological
- Effect: High Pain Tolerance (14); stay conscious until drug ends or both CMs + Overflow full; -2 tactile Perception; -1 Agility tests
- If either CM fills while on drug: gain **Low Pain Tolerance**

### Buffout

- Vector: Injection / Inhalation / Ingestion
- Speed: Immediate / 1 CT / 10 min
- Duration: 10 x 1d6 minutes
- Addiction: Physiological
- Effect: +6 Strength for Lift/Carry and Grapple hold/damage; **-4 Strength** for standard melee DV
- Each bonus Strength use: 3S resisted with natural Body only (track; apply when drug ends)
- -2 Judge Intentions while up
- Crash: apply self-damage; -3 Agility, -2 Logic for duration of effect (0 Agility = locked; 0 Logic = can't think)

### Accelerator

- Vector: Injection, Inhalation
- Speed: Immediate
- Duration: 2d6 Combat Turns
- Addiction: Both
- Effect: +3d6 Initiative dice (base 4d6 before augs); -2 Body Damage Resistance; bleeding wounds: **2P unresisted/round** until First Aid or magic heal
- Compatible with Rating 1 initiative augs only (cap **5d6** total Initiative dice from this)
- Crash: hallucinations/paranoia for hours = Combat Turns the drug lasted

## New devices / weapons

### MADAR (Magnetic Deflection Array)

Active:

- -2 dice on ranged attacks from outside point-blank (melee range OK)
- -1 DV per range category
- Noise **-6** in 0.5 m radius sphere
- Attacks from above/below (head-foot axis): no attack penalty, **+2 DV** (prone = bad)
- Attracts ragers (and sharks)

### Microwave Gun

Light Pistol ranges. Peak-discharge packs (*Run & Gun*): 1 charge low-freq, 2 high-freq.

| Mode | Acc | Dam | AP | Mode | Notes |
|------|-----|-----|-----|------|-------|
| High frequency | 6 | 7P | -6 | SS | Cooks flesh |
| Low frequency | 4 | 10P* | - | SS | Matrix damage to electronics; Called Shot vs <=0.25 m² gear: -2 not -4; GM picks hit gear if no Called Shot |

Avail 20R, Cost NA (prototype)

### Repeating Laser

SMG ranges. Exotic Ranged Weapon (Laser Weapons). Underbarrel/top accessories only; no other mods. Peak-discharge packs (portable) or vehicle power.

- -1 DV per range category beyond Short
- -1 DV per Visibility level (Light/Moderate/Heavy)
- Acc 7, Dam 7P*, AP -8, SA/BF/FA, Ammo 10(c) or external, Avail 16F, Cost NA

### Crazy-Repeller

EM field: repels ragers/shamblers; smart head cases take distraction penalty = Field Rating. Battery: **10 power units**. Uses Rating units per hour at that setting. Recharge: plug **1/min**, solar **2/hour**.

## Dragon ley line (Boston)

Aligned to draconic magic/dragon totems:

- Aligned: add BG count to spell/power **limits**
- Unaligned: dice pool penalty = BG count on arcane rolls
- Adepts: add BG to power-use limits if aligned; else penalty. May turn passive powers off (Simple Action) to avoid bad tradeoffs

Path: Rox → South Boston → Hub → Revere → Saugus → Lynn → Salem. Heart ~15 m wide **BG 10**, drops 1 every 10 m; total width ~215 m. Assensing detects it.

**QZ Background Count:** Safe enclaves 0; elsewhere ≥1; heavy head-case infestation ≥3 (fear of buried minds).
