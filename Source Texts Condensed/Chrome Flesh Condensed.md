### Experimental CFD Treatment (Phalanx / Xenosapient Strain)

**Scope:** GM discretion only. Not automatic for all head cases. Dominant PF must descend from a **xenosapient AI** (AI alienated from human form). Does not work on fully overwritten head cases. Does not work on non-xenosapient PFs (Coriolis gives false positive on non-xenosapients; dangerous).

**Select head case ability:** Some CFD nanomachines can form a Matrix device (implanted cyberdeck-like) usable by the PF when in control.

**Four-step treatment:**

1. **Convince PF to leave** (host must consent to torture)
2. **Coriolis complex form** (technomancer)
3. **Reverser nanomachine colony injection**
4. **Restore brain function** via cyberware/bioware augmentation; monitor recovery

#### Step 1: Convince the Fragment

Force the xenosapient PF to experience starvation, dehydration, intense emotions (drugs or torture). Host warned and must accept.

- Host resists stopping torture: **Willpower + Body** test; failure resets process.
- Convince PF: **Negotiation + Charisma vs. Willpower + Charisma**, once per day for **7 consecutive days**. Most daily wins decides outcome.
- Xenosapient PFs: **-2** to resist Negotiation; **-3** if physical-body exploits apply (tailored pheromones, Influence spell, Kinesics, etc.).

#### Step 2: Coriolis (New Complex Form)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Target** | Persona |
| **Duration** | E |
| **FV** | L + 3 |

**Software + Resonance [Level] (5, 24 hours)** Extended Test to eject PF. Minimum several days; advise life support for technomancer and patient. If threshold not met within **one week**, Reversers lose effect; must restart with new Reversers.

- **Glitch:** Target **-2** to all tests during treatment.
- **Critical glitch:** Interval +48 hours; hits reset to 0.
- If PF cedes control or disconnects from Matrix: process disrupted; **host and technomancer take full Fading as unresisted Stun**.

PF must access Matrix via hot-sim or nanite-formed Matrix device.

#### Step 3: Reverser Nanomachines

- Prototype only; AAA-affiliated biochem labs; high demand; cannot be replicated on standard nano-fabrication arrays (per Nephrine/KAM debate).
- Injection process: standard nanite treatment (injection, food/drink for material).
- **Only works if:** PF is genuine xenosapient, agreed to leave, and Coriolis succeeded.
- **If fragment refuses or Coriolis not used:** Reversers have no effect.
- **If treatment fails anyway:** Reversers corrupted like any nanoware injection; CFD spreads further.

#### Step 4: Recovery

Reversers evacuate AI; infected brain portions become latent (not dead). Restore function with augmentation/gene therapy (risky). One successful case: host cured of blackouts but required heavy implant load; near-fatal Essence loss if host had prior augmentation or unclean lifestyle.

**Murai Holdings:** Reverser creator; main office Boston (lockdown complicates access).

### Programmable ASIST Biofeedback (PAB)

**Availability 14 | Cost 120,000¥**

Requires: PAB unit, DNI connection (datajack, trodes, etc.), time. Restraints recommended for unwilling subjects.

**Categories:** Event reprogramming, invoked reprogramming, reversing reprogramming, behavior modification.

**Test:** **Psychology + Logic [PAB Rating]** Extended Test at **1-week intervals**.

| Action | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Event reprogramming | Target Willpower + modifiers (Event Programming table) |
| Invoked reprogramming | Same; trigger specified before rolls |

**Daily requirement:** Programmer must work on subject **8+ hours/day** until threshold met; missing a day **fails** the attempt.

### Positive Qualities (full rules)

**Better to Be Feared than Loved (5 Karma):** Blackmailed Contacts (*Run Faster* p. 178) need no actual blackmail material; threat suffices. Add **Street Cred** to blackmailed Contacts' Loyalty (Leverage). If a blackmailed Contact turns, add character's **Street Cred** to Contact's Connection to determine Professional Rating of retaliation team.

**Biocompatibility (5 Karma):** Choose bioware or cyberware (once). Essence cost of that type reduced **10%**, rounded down to tenth; stacks with grade rebate (e.g., alphaware 0.8 → 0.72 → **0.7**).

**Cyber Singularity Seeker (12 Karma in entry):** **+1 Willpower** per **two full cyberlimb replacements** (partial limbs don't count), max **+2 Willpower**.

**Drug Tolerant (6 Karma):** **+2 dice** to all Addiction Tests (SR5 p. 414) to resist addiction; no bonus when drying out.

**Prototype Transhuman (10 Karma, character creation only):** Up to **1 Essence of bioware** at normal nuyen cost, **zero Essence cost** (genetically grown in). Must also take one Negative Quality **without Karma bonus:** Wanted, Allergy (Common, Mild), Astral Beacon, or Insomnia (10).

**Redliner (10 Karma):** **+1 Strength and +1 Agility** per **two full cyberarms or cyberlegs**, max **+2** each. Lose **3 Physical CM boxes** per two full arms/legs installed (net **-1** CM box after two limbs vs. normal +1 per limb).

**Revels in Murder (8 Karma):** When spending Edge on offensive combat and damage sends a willing, aware **metahuman** target into physical overflow, **immediately regain** that Edge point. GM final say on valid targets.

**Uncanny Healer (12 Karma):** Add current **Essence** as dice pool modifier to Natural Recovery tests (Stun and Physical, SR5 p. 207). Not magical healing or others' First Aid. Incompatible with Quick Healer (SR5 p. 77).

### Negative Qualities (full rules)

**Antipathy (8 Karma):** **-2** all opposed Social Tests.

**AIPS (10 Karma):** In spam zone: **-1 Perception dice per Noise Rating** (max -6). GM may require Composure test at threshold = Noise Rating in non-stressful situations.

**Blank Slate (15 Karma):** Personafix abuse; near-fugue without running personasoft; optional Amnesia. Buying off: original personality re-emerges or personasoft personality becomes permanent.

**Cyberpsychosis (10 Karma):** Requires Antipathy + **≥5 Essence lost** to augmentations. On Social Test glitch: inappropriate/violent reaction. Critical glitch: psychotic break (NPC until GM recovery). Must have Antipathy.

**Dead Emotion (5 Karma):** Cannot feel one specific emotion (player/GM pick).

**Dry Addict (2/5/10/13 Karma by level):** Substitute for bought-off Addiction; no withdrawal penalties but Composure tests when substance available (threshold 1 passive to 5 high-stress). On relapse Addiction Test failure: Dry Addict replaced by Addiction (no Karma bonus). Level must match former Addiction.

**Family Curse (5 Karma):** **-2 dice** on Addiction Tests.

**Lightweight (6 Karma in entry):** Addiction Rating of all substances **+2 levels** for this character.

**Poor Self Control variants (5 or 8 Karma):**

**Quasimodo (5 Karma):** **-3 Social Skill Tests** (non-Matrix); **+2 Intimidation**; negotiate deformity with GM.

**Tough and Targeted (10 Karma):** ~monthly random attack by rival; attacker's Professional Rating = character's **Street Cred**. If defeated (overflow, not necessarily death): quality removed free, **Street Cred → 0**, gain **1 Notoriety**.

## 07 - Shiny: The Latest Chrome

**Story/hooks:** Dr. Howard, an ork street doc in the Barrens, enforces gang neutrality in her clinic and pulls aside the chromed ganger Riot to pitch JackPoint software upgrades, not more hardware.

**Setting/corp flavor:**
- **2078 rollout primer:** New cyberware ad cycles, lab prototypes, and shadow ops (theft, sabotage, extractions) as corps race to market post-Nanopocalypse.
- **Why go chrome:** Cheaper than bioware; proven/repairable; machines do things flesh cannot (Matrix, radio, skillsofts). Catches: invasiveness (~1 in 20 reject cyber badly), availability (beta/delta wait lists, discrimination, illegal cyberweapons), befriending a cyberdoc essential.
- **Cybersports:** Baseball (2032+, reflex/muscle/eye limits); Basketball (no height/reach mods or pure cyberlimbs); Football (2025+ arms race, 200m x 80m field, full cyberlimbs since 2052, weapons banned); Soccer (near-total cyber ban, world's last unaugmented flagship sport); Urban Brawl (weapons and exotic limbs encouraged, #2 worldwide).
- **Cyberpsychosis:** Augmentation-triggered or worsened mental disorders (phobias, dissociation, OCD-like codes); shadow clinics often dump you after install.
- **Quality grades:** Standard (mass market); **Omega** (cheap knockoffs, field-test castoffs; optional rule below); **Alpha** (premium brands, psych staff); **Beta** (resort-clinic tier, Seattle has three); **Delta** (<20 black sites, CEOs/cybermancy); **Gamma** (does not officially exist; optional prototype rule below).

**Mechanics:**

### Cosmetic Cyberware

**Casemod:** Cosmetic casing for cyber (engraved spurs, glowing datajacks, etc.). GM sets pricing/availability by outrageousness: +0 Avail / +50¥ to +4 Avail / +10,000¥. No mechanical benefit; truly outrageous mods may impose Distinctive Style (no Karma bonus).

**Cosmetic Surgery:** Any appearance surgery. +100¥ (mole removal) to +20,000¥ (rib removal, facial reconstruction). Popular trends cost more, not harder procedures.

**Breast Implants 2.0 wireless:** Change size Free Action (instead of Simple).

**Metatype Reduction:** Orks gain **Human-Looking** quality. Trolls gain **+1 racial Charisma maximum**. Cannot combine with metaposeur genetech. Budget option: six treatments at 1,000¥ each.

**Skin Toner:** Wireless: change skin color Free Action (instead of Simple).

### Symbionts

Living augmentations sharing nutrients/water with host. Macro/leech forms largely shelved (NanoPocalypse, CFD PR); market shifting to micro/endovariety. **Genesis Consortium** introduced symbionts.

#### General rules

- **Damage/repair:** Damaged symbiont may turn parasitic, draining host blood/nutrients gradually.
- **Eaten from within:** On deadly trauma or **critical glitch** during active symbiont feature use, symbiont turns parasitic: host loses **1 Physical damage box/week** (unhealable until removed). Removal: **Medicine + Logic (12, 1 hour)** Extended Test.
- **Leech symbionts:** External/subdermal; visible on Perception if uncovered; contact reveals something unusual. Gradually move (host can feel it).
- **Endosymbionts ("endosonts"):** Internal; attach to organs or GI tract. Typical lifespan **10 years**, then pass naturally and need replacement.
- **Gut flora:** Swallowed; never turn parasitic; shorter lifespan, need regular exposure to target substance or die harmlessly.

### Genetech (general rules)

- **Permanent;** Essence loss is **permanent** (no refund even if augmentation becomes useless; revitalization does **not** restore genetech Essence).
- **Essence loss** reflects foreign nature / mana flow disruption, not just invasiveness.
- **Gene therapy:** Patient in nutrient tank; cost includes hospitalization and tailoring.
- **Therapeutic genetics:** Corrective treatments; **no Essence loss** for listed examples (cancer prevention, color blindness, genetic disorders).
- **Rejuvenation:** Limited repetitions before cumulative Essence toll; cannot fix neuron-connection diseases (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's).
- **Phenotypic variation (optional):** One phenotype adjustment as Positive quality at character creation: Karma = (cost / 2,000) rounded up + 3; no Essence cost if natural mutation.
- **Environmental microadaptation:** **One only**; second replaces first (no Essence refund).
- **Optional rule, Similar Agents:** GM may halve Power for related pathogens/toxins.
- **Immunization:** Complete immunity to 1 dose of specific agent; max agents = unaugmented Body; neurotoxins ineffective; multi-dose resistance rule truncated in print after first extra dose.
- **Transgenic alteration:** Cosmetic only; Distinctive Style applies; 1-3 months, Essence 0.3, Avail 10, 30,000¥+.
- **Complimentary genetics:** Removing/upgrading paired 'ware costs **+10%** of original 'ware cost; must repurchase optimization for upgrades.
- **Genetic infusions:** Quick, dangerous temporary augments from any category except restoration/phenotype masking lists; **2 hr** procedure, effects in **24 hr**; duration **(15 - Body)** days (min 1); Addiction Rating **5**, Threshold **3**; end-of-duration **Essence + Edge** test (no Edge spend) on Side Effects table; Avail **13F**, Cost **2,500¥**.

**Story/hooks:** Haze and Nephrine on street pharma, BTL resurgence, rumored corp drugs (Cereprax, PsychChips, BodyBenders, Ghost Chips), and customized designer cocktails for runners.

**Mechanics:**

### General rules

- **Speedballing/cocktailing:** Mixing drugs adds **+1 Addiction Rating** to **all** drugs involved.
- **Drug interactions:** While under a drug or its crash, taking another drug: roll **1D6 per drug beyond the first**, total, modify by grade, consult table. Street cooked: **+1 per street drug**. Designer (all designer): **-1**.
- **Portable Chemical Injector:** Wrist 3 doses 500¥ | Thigh 5 doses 750¥ | Harness 20 doses 2,500¥ (no chems included).
- **Grades:** Street cooked: half cost, double crash duration, +1 interaction. Standard: default. Pharmaceutical: double cost, half crash, Threshold -1; required for custom drugs. Designer: 6x standard cost (3x pharma), quarter crash, needs DNA on file; wrong user = street cooked; all designer = -1 interaction.
- **Custom drugs:** Foundation + blocks + optional enhancers. Chemistry + Logic Extended (Threshold = Avail x 2, 8 hr interval). Glitch = restart; crit glitch = destroy ingredients. Contact Connection **5+** for custom drugs and raw materials (raw = half drug price).
