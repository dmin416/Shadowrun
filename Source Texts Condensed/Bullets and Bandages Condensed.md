**Story/hooks:** Capt. David Hill (Seattle HTR Team 2) pep-talk for new HTR medics; JackPoint notes ex-military/civilian medics often land in the shadows.

### Training / loadout notes (no new stats)

- HTR medic training: care under fire, advanced life support, basic surgery, pharmacology, triage; penetrating / explosive / magical trauma; environmental exposure; infectious disease.
- Combat skills for medics: movement under fire, cover, CQB awareness, small-unit tactics; gunners add CQB, demolitions, breaching, fire direction.
- Platforms named: Ares Citymaster (armored), Hughes CRT helo, Aeroquip Dustoff medevac drone.
- Typical SRT medic armament: Defiance EX Shocker + Colt 2066 (gel + standard). HTR similar; gunners officially Colt M23, often mission-tailored (shotgun/SMG/etc.).

## 06 - Game Information

Full rules: [06 - Game Information](06%20-%20Game%20Information.md).

**Story/hooks:** Framing for dedicated medic PCs (combat medic, street doc, magical healer, ripper, traditional healer) to expand team roles and let GMs push lethality.

### Building a medic (guidance, no new stats)

- **Attributes:** Logic crucial (Biotech + Knowledge); Body/Strength for combat medics (ork/troll/dwarf); Edge vs medkit/autodoc; Charisma for many healing traditions (elves); initiative augs help Stabilization timing.
- **Skills:** Biotech group core (First Aid, Medicine; Surgery / Cybertechnology for downtime). Combat/Physical to reach patients. Electronics, Chemistry, Pilot (incl. Exotic/Remote Ops for rigged medkits). Magical healers: Spellcasting, Assensing, Enchanting/Alchemy; optional Conjuring/Counterspelling.
- **Knowledge:** Anatomy, Genetics, Pharmacology, Toxicology, Pathology (bonus dice via optional Knowledge rule in ch. 07).
- **Qualities / mentors:** Aptitude, Exceptional Attribute, Quick Healer, Toughness, Guts, Lucky; mentors Bear, Great Mother, Horse, Sun, etc.
- **Gear priorities:** Medkit, biomonitor / imaging, autoinjector + drugs; foci/fetishes/reagents for magical healers; Logic-boosting and sensory augs; snake-finger cyberhand / microscopic vision for surgery.

**Cost:** 5 BP or 2 Karma  
+1 dice pool to one Care Under Fire test type only (Stabilization, Diagnosis, or Treatment). Magical or mundane. Take once only.

**Bonus:** 5 / 10 / 15 BP, or 7 / 14 / 21 Karma (ages 50-59 / 60-69 / 70+)

### Care Under Fire (optional)

Ideal care order: Stabilize → Diagnose → Treat. Under fire, often stabilize only until safe.

#### Damage Progression

If a single attack deals **5+ Physical boxes**, damage progresses +1 box every (Body) Combat Turns until Stabilized (like Overflow timing). Wound mods accrue normally; consciousness until Physical CM exceeded, then Overflow continues. Death if Overflow > Body before Stabilization.

#### Stabilization Tests

- First Aid + Logic Extended (Complex Action); threshold = total Physical boxes (incl. progression during the test + Overflow if any). Use Healing Modifiers table (SR4A p. 253 / SR5 p. 208).
- Success: stops Progression/Overflow; does **not** remove damage. Each net hit reduces wound modifiers by 1 (can continue until all negated). Negation lasts (First Aid skill) hours.
- Non-progressive damage (incl. Stun): Stabilization can negate wound mods; threshold = total boxes on both Condition Monitors.

#### Stabilization and Magic

- **Stabilize spell:** Force ≥ Progressive + Overflow boxes at cast. DV = (Progressive + Overflow) / 2 round up. Sustain turns = those boxes; each net hit -1 Combat Turn sustain. Further Progression while sustained adds to Drain and sustain time. Success stops Progression/Overflow.
- **Resist Pain:** Unchanged for reducing damage modifiers.

#### Trauma Patches and Crash

Under Care Under Fire: trauma patch or Crash lets the patient roll Stabilization with Body at end of each Combat Turn. If medic doses before aiding, add patient's Body to medic's Stabilization pool.

#### Diagnosis Tests

- Care Under Fire: Complex Action; Medicine + Logic or First Aid + Logic vs GM threshold. Biomonitor: +1 (not stackable with medkit bonus).
- Success: +2 to subsequent Stabilization / Treatment. Glitch: +1 or 0. Critical glitch: -2.
- Magic: Assensing + Intuition (Complex) or Diagnose spell (*Street Magic* p. 123); same +2 if successful.

**Diagnosis Threshold Table**

| Threshold | Injuries | Illness / intoxication |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Light (1-2 boxes) | Obvious etiology or toxin |
| 2 | Moderate (3-5 boxes) | Common etiology or toxin |
| 3 | Severe (6-9 boxes) | Rare etiology or toxin |
| 5+ | Critical (10+ boxes) | Exotic etiology or toxin |

#### Treatment Tests

- First Aid + Logic (2) per core (SR4A p. 252 / SR5 p. 205), with exceptions:
  - If Physical CM not exceeded: success also Stabilizes (stops Progression).
  - If in Overflow: still need separate Stabilization.
  - If previously Stabilized: each net hit removes **2** boxes (max net hits = First Aid or Medkit rating, higher).
- Out-of-combat Medicine healing unchanged.
- **Heal spell:** Unchanged, except success also Stabilizes unless already in Overflow (then Stabilization still required).

### Advanced Medkit and Autodoc Rules (optional)

- **Medkit:** supplies + expert system; often needs operator for hands-on tasks.
- **Autodoc:** self-propelled (usually) with robotic arms; independent hands-on care (Crash Cart Autodoc, Evo Orderly, Valkyrie module). Valkyrie: no self-move, else autodoc in a vehicle.
- Pilot Rating = Device Rating (unless stated). Profession [First Aid/Medicine] autosoft = Rating. Rigger adaptation standard on Rating 4+ medkits; lower: +250¥, Logic + Hardware (4, 2 hours) Extended.
- Setup for autonomous use: Logic + First Aid (4; 1 Complex Action) Extended (leads/IV/etc.).
- Stocked kit/autodoc negates -3 lacking-supplies mod.
- **Advanced vs basic:** Rating is **dice pool modifier only**; does **not** substitute for Biotech skill / prevent defaulting when unskilled.
- Remote: Biotech + Command (remote) or + Response (jumped-in), -2, + Rating.
- Autonomous: Pilot + Rating.

#### Expending Medkit Supplies

Treat/stabilize patients up to Rating times before supplies empty (Rating/dice bonus unchanged). At 0 supplies: no longer negates -3 lacking-supplies. Restock per SR4A p. 337 / SR5 p. 450.

#### Upgrading Medkits (optional)

Rating 4 or lower only. Pay kit cost again; Cybertechnology + Logic Extended (Rating + 2, 1 hour). Success: Rating +2; size/conceal and supply limit stay at **original** Rating. Autodocs: upgrade Pilot/autosoft as normal drone software instead.

#### Improvised Medical Supplies (optional)

Complex Action: First Aid + Intuition; each hit reduces -3 lacking-supplies by 1 (min 0). First Aid time × 1.5 afterward. GM decides if materials exist.
