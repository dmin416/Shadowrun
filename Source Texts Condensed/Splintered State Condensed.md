## 08 - Scene 3: Lying Down on the Job

**Story/hooks:** Research Dietrich’s Hermes Ikon; word leaks to factions; downtime to rest and reload.

### Analyzing the commlink

- Unlocked initially. **Matrix Perception (Computer + Intuition [Data Processing])**, **3 hits**, **1 hour**: find hidden second device DR **5**, encrypted + fragmented.
- Mark: Brute Force (Cybercombat + Logic [Attack]) or Hack on the Fly (Hacking + Logic [Sleaze]) vs device DP **10**. Sleaze marks on the hacker do nothing / no Dietrich alert.
- Crack File: Hacking + Logic [Attack] vs **2× protection** (DP **10**).
- Edit File: Computer + Logic [Data Processing] vs DP **10**.
- OS **40** = Convergence (SR5 p. 231). Special: OS **≥20** alerts **Ares and Mitsuhama**.
- Alt: Extended Hardware + Logic [Mental] **(24, 1 hour)** to repossess; still must Crack File.
- Result: Handout #1 fragments; keywords Operation Daybreak, Seth Dietrich; ~half missing.

### Contacts / betrayal

| Contact type | Loyalty ≤3 | Loyalty 4 | Loyalty ≥5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blind sale of Hermes Ikon | Book ~3,000¥ (Loyalty &lt;4: 1–2k). Conn ≤4 buyer dies in days; Conn 5–6 safe. | — | — |
| Corporate | Tip Ares or MCT | GM call (e.g. buy 10k then flip) | Help + warn |
| Street | Info only; no betrayal / no buyer | same | same |
