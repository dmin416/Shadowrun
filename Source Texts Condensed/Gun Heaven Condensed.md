**Story/hooks:** Glitch dumps civilian-catalog and paramilitary guns for JackPoint; Bull shrugs at firearm fascination; auction alert for an antique Lorenz rifle.

**Mechanics:** None.

## 03 - New Weapon Upgrades / Traits

### Vintage

Not compatible with modern electronics (outdated manufacture, age, or bad design). Physical upgrades allowed at **2x** normal listed cost.

### Cap & Ball

Hand-loaded powder, shot, and percussion cap. **3 Complex Actions** to reload each round; stopping mid-reload forces a restart. Ammo annotated **(cb)** / **(CB)**.

## 04 - Using This Book with SR5

**Accessories:** Standard mods listed on weapons are not all in SR5 core; see *Run & Gun*.

**Sporting Rifles:** Skill **Longarms**. Ranges: Short 0-50 / Medium 51-250 / Long 251-500 / Extreme 501-750.

**Flamethrowers:** Skill **Exotic Ranged Weapons (Flamethrowers)**. **Complex Action** to ready. May use Suppressive Fire (*SR5* p. 179). Multi-target fan: Complex Action, up to **3** targets within weapon range and within **4 m** of each other; costs **2** ammo units. Fire damage (*SR5* p. 171). Range per weapon entry.
