### Blight (toxin)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Vector | Injection; Special |
| Speed | Immediate |
| Penetration | 0 |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Awakened: besides Essence→Magic loss, **-1 Magic and -1 Magic max per GreyWare item**. If item cannot be obvious and has no wireless, it cannot be GreyWare.

### Spells

#### Astral Disruption (Mana)

Type M | LOS | Sustained | Drain F-2

Opposed: Magic + Spellcasting vs Willpower (+ Counterspelling). Force must ≥ target Willpower. Per net hit, disable one passive power while sustained: Immunity to Normal Weapons, Concealment, Energy Aura, Guard, Regeneration, Magical Guard, Innate Spell (one per net hit). Dual-natured may lose astral perception: **-4** all actions while sustained.

#### Mass Astral Disruption (Mana, Area)

As above; Range LOS (A); Drain F.

### Adept powers

#### Mystic Aptitude

**Cost:** 0.75 PP per level (max 3)  
**Activation:** Simple Action  
Substitute Magic + Rank for one Physical Attribute (chosen on activate); affects pools, limits, Strength DV, Initiative. Duration Magic Combat Turns; then Drain = Rank x 2. Cooldown: Rank 1 / 30 min; Rank 2 / 15 min; Rank 3 / 1 min. No stack with Attribute Boost or Supernatural Prowess. Power foci do not boost Magic for this effect.

#### State of Purity

**Cost:** 1.5 PP | **Prerequisite:** Essence 6  
**Activation:** Complex Action  
Unarmed DV = (Magic + Strength) P or S; AP -(Magic x 0.5). Lasts Magic Combat Rounds or until Complex deactivate / dead / unconscious. Then Drain [(Magic x 0.5) + rounds active]. Elemental extras may stack; no extra DV.

### New uses for Karma and Street Cred

- Raise contact **Loyalty** by 1: sacrifice Karma/Cred equal to new Loyalty (max Loyalty 4); takes that many months; only one such change at a time.
- Raise contact **Connection** by 1: sacrifice 10 Karma/Cred total; need Loyalty ≥ 3; once per contact.
- Influence skill group test: sacrifice 1 Karma or Cred per +1 die, max 3/test.
- Faction Reputation +1: sacrifice equal to new score (max 3 this way); months = new score; one change at a time.
- Lifestyle payment -1 level: 1 Karma or Cred per 1,000¥ saved; ends if lifestyle changes.
- Call favor (GM OK): sacrifice 2 x Favor Rating (SR5 p. 389); capped by contact Loyalty; owe equal/lesser favor back or lose Street Cred equal to spend.
