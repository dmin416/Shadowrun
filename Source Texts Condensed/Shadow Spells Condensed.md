## 05 - Threats

**Story/hooks:** Metaplanar "order" entities via AF/Black Lodge; Creedus Maximus hunting talismongers; Lone Star Project Wampum flipping captured mages into assets.

### Rifts and Guardians of Order

Long-term dimensional rifts rare; two recent attempts failed ~10 hours; rumored third in Antarctica. Order spirits impose structure via mental manipulation, then Crystalize nonconformists. Freedom risk high (esp. AF mages vs Black Lodge). Order level scales with services owed.

### Crystalize (spirit power)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | P |
| Action | Complex |
| Range | LOS |
| Duration | Permanent |

Ranged attack, increment Magic meters; Exotic Ranged Weapon + Agility [Physical]. Hit: (Force x 2)P, AP = Force; immobilized as transformation starts; net hits add damage; Body + Armor resist. Continued hits fill Physical CM until fully crystallized; no Overflow from this power (cannot kill directly). Crystallized form is brittle to other damage; severed extremities stay missing if restored to flesh.

## 07 - Rituals

**Story/hooks:** AF Decrystalize vs crystalline allies; Astral Preservation Society Mana Flow free on Matrix (late 2075); PCC sells Mana Ebb formulae.

### Decrystalize

*Material Link, Spotter*

Requires complete intact crystalline statue; damage to statue transfers on restore. Spotter OK; usually lodge with subject present. Time: 1 hour per Body (flesh value).

### Mana Flow

*Anchored*

+1 background count for Force hours in negative-count areas only. Radius Force x 100 m. Time: 1 hour. Fails (Drain still paid) if count is neutral or positive.

### Mana Ebb

*Anchored*

-1 background count for Force hours in positive-count areas only. Radius Force x 100 m. Time: 1 hour. Siphons to a known metaplane or deep astral (by formula). Plants wilt; animals leave. Print also says fails on neutral/positive (likely meant neutral/negative).
