# Run Faster

Player companion: more character options and creation methods.

## Sections
- **More Than Skin Deep**: Playing different metatypes and cultures
- **Construction Kits**: Alternate character creation systems (incl. life modules)
- **The Mess of Metahumanity**: Metavariants, metasapients, shapeshifters, changelings, metagenic qualities
- **Into the Night**: Playing Infected (ghouls, vampires, etc.)
- **As You as You Can Be**: Extra positive/negative qualities
- **Who You Know**: Expanded contacts and favors
- **Bosses and Betrayers**: Working with Mr. Johnsons
- **A Dump of One's Own**: Expanded lifestyles and housing
