## 06 - Check Your Head

**Story/hooks:** Butch's street-doc report on Cognitive Fragmentation Disorder: infection stages, nanite rewrite of brains/electronics/nanostructures, test notes on meta/Awakened/EK/animal hosts, and JackPoint fallout (FastJack, Riser, Plan 9, Lanier).

### CFD progression (in-universe; hard rules in Game Information)

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Initial infection | Contact with infected nanites; onset speed scales with nanite volume already in the host |
| Stage 1 | Debilitating headaches, memory faults, motor problems, extreme emotion, sensory hypersensitivity |
| Stage 2 (Fragmentation) | Blackouts; emergent personality fragments (PFs) take control; sleep deprivation accelerates overwrite |
| Stage 3 | Full DID-like switching; PFs aware of all activity; subject often despondent |
| Stage 4 | Original personality fully subjugated; PF in full-time control |

**Other notes (no stats):** Virus is non-biological; infects biological hosts, electronics, and nanofabricated materials; hunter-killer nanites get repurposed; Awakened/EK hosts can resist longer (EK can die of CNS overstress); larger mammals/paranimals often keep paranormal abilities.

## 15 - Extractor's Toolkit

**Story/hooks:** Cold open of a fake-NYPD extraction; Sticks lists bounty-hunter tools for keeping targets under, spoofing authority, and moving living cargo.

### Houdini Machine (rules of thumb)

- Two strap-on auto-injectors (extra-dose mod, 6 doses each): typically one sedative line, one saline/nutrient line.
- Version I (commlink + biomonitor): up to ~2 days unconscious before refill; trolls/dwarfs may wake early unless dosages doubled (halves duration) or Version II is used.
- Version II (medkit computer): up to ~3 days; better dosing for troll/dwarf physiology.
- Saline/drugs: IV via First Aid. Liquid nutrients: NG tube or stomach shunt requires Medicine (not First Aid default).

### Optional rule: Drug Interaction

If a sedative clashes with the target's existing meds/street drugs, GM picks severity:

| Severity | Effect |
|---|---|
| Mild | Cosmetic (rash, discoloration, breathing discomfort); not life-threatening |
| Moderate | Resist 3P every Combat Turn (Body) until one drug wears off |
| Severe | Resist half the offending drug's Power as P every Combat Turn until it wears off (round up; e.g. Narcoject Power 15 → 8P) |
| Extreme | Immediate cardiac/respiratory crash: 1 box overflow; stabilize or die; lasting organ/brain damage likely |

Onset of interaction symptoms: 1D6 minutes. Allergic reactions use Allergy quality (*SR5* p. 78).

### Drug rules

**Chloral hydrate**  
Vector: Ingestion | Speed: 2 CT | Pen: 0 | Power: 12 (15 if with alcohol or other drowsiness med)  
Duration: (14 - Body) hours; (18 - Body) if mixed with drugs/alcohol  
Effect: Stun Damage

**Chloroform**  
Vector: Inhalation | Speed: 1 CT | Pen: 0 | Power: 7  
Duration: (10 - Body) hours  
Effect: Stun Damage  
Poor vs orks/trolls; dwarfs immune. ~1 in 20 risk arrhythmia.

**Caldwell lily extract**  
Vector: Ingestion (hallucinogen), Contact (local anesthesia), Injection (general)  
Speed: 1 CT | Pen: 0  
Power: 4 (hallucinogen/local), 8 (injected), 14 (concentrate)  
Duration: (6 - Body) hours, min 1 hour  
Effect: Stun Damage  
Normal inject may need 2 doses to KO; concentrate usually 1. Very rare adverse reactions (~0.1%).

**DMSO**  
Carrier: any compound mixed with DMSO gains Contact vector (*SR5* p. 408).

**Gamma-scopolamine**  
Vector: Injection | Speed: Immediate | Pen: 0 | Power: 12  
Duration: 1 hour paralysis, then 1 hour truth-serum residue  
Effect: Paralysis + truth serum (Willpower -3, min 1, for remaining toxin effect)  
~1 in 60 stop breathing. Magicians/technomancers may still act while paralyzed.

**Laés / Laésal wine / Leäl**  
Vector: Ingestion, Injection | Speed: 1 CT | Pen: 0  
Power: 12 (laés/laésal), 10 (leäl)  
Duration: 20 x 1D6 minutes (laés); 5 x 1D6 minutes (leäl)  
Effect: Stun; if any Stun taken, erase last (12 - Body, min 1) hours (laés) or last (120 - Body, min 100) minutes (leäl). Memories unrecoverable by tech or magic. ~1 in 30 suffer decades-scale memory loss.

**Liquid nutrients**  
Medicine + Logic (2) to administer via NG tube/shunt already in place; no First Aid default. Needed for full Houdini nutrition function.

**Narcoject**  
Vector: Injection | Speed: Immediate | Pen: 0 | Power: 15 | Effect: Stun Damage  
~1 in 80 allergic (can be life-threatening).

**Normal saline**  
Safe with medkit/biomonitor. Critical glitch on Medicine + Logic when administering can cause metabolic acidosis (coma/death risk).

**Slab**  
Vector: Injection | Speed: 2 CT | Pen: 0 | Power: 16  
Duration: (10 - Body) hours, min 1 hour  
Effect: Suspended animation (looks dead). Perception + Intuition (6) or Medicine + Logic (4) without proper gear (medkit insufficient). Afterward: -4 Reaction for half duration (round down). ~1 in 30 never wake.

### Adept powers

**Commanding Voice** (1 PP)  
Complex Action, ≤5-word command. Opposed Leadership + Charisma vs Willpower + Intuition. Success: target uses next action to obey or stands confused (more net hits → more likely to obey). Metahumans who hear/understand only; no tech amplification. Cumulative -2 per prior use on same target in 24 hours. Multi-target: largest defender pool +1 per extra target (max 6).

**Light Touch** (0.25 PP/level)  
-1 Perception dice per level vs observers/targets of the adept's palm/pickpocket attempts.

**Mimic** (0.25 PP/level, max Rating 6)  
Copy fingerprints/palm/retinal prints. 1 minute contact per Rating die vs print scanners (max Rating 6 = 6 minutes). Copies last Magic minutes.

**Nerve Strike** (1 PP)  
Unarmed attack: instead of damage, each net hit -1 Agility or Reaction (attacker choice). At 0, target paralyzed. Critters: -1 per 2 net hits. Spirits/drones immune. Recovery: rest 1 hour, then Body + Willpower each hour (1 attribute point/hit); First Aid/magical healing help.

**Nimble Fingers** (0.25 PP)  
+1 Palming / sleight-of-hand. Insert Clip, Pick Up/Put Down Object, Remove Clip, and Use Simple Object become Free Actions.

## 16 - Game Information

**Story/hooks:** GM-only CFD secrets: Evo Dickens Program and NeoNET Project Imago as primary personality farms; infection is character-changing with no canon cure yet (player infection only with player buy-in).

### CFD virus (hard rules)

Two ratings:

- **Nanite Volume (NV):** how many infected nanites (see table).
- **Matrix Entity Concentration (MEC):** GM-set cohesion of the dominant Matrix personality(s). High MEC = faster clean overwrite; low/multiple = amalgam / longer switching.

**Nanite Volume table (cumulative)**

| Source | NV |
|---|---|
| Nanohive | + Rating |
| Nanite system | + half Rating |
| Genetech | +1 per 2 genetic modifications |

Infected nanites reproduce: +1 NV/week if the head case eats (soft nanites); +1 NV/week more if they also ingest inorganic material (hard nanites). Natural degradation -1 NV/month (often recycled if not flushed).

**Weekly Resistance:** Opposed Charisma + Willpower + Essence vs NV + MEC. Net hits for the character become a finite **Control Dice Pool** for that week (not a standing bonus).

**Control Test:** Simple Composure (4). Fail → lose control for (4 - hits)d6 hours. If Control Pool dice or Edge were spent and the test still fails, 6s explode on the duration roll. Minimum one Control Test/day; also trigger when:

- ≥4 boxes in a single attack (P, S, or mixed)
- ≥6 boxes total damage
- Knocked unconscious (CFD personality auto-gains control; use Adrenal Control)
- Forced into Fatigue territory

Daily fails can mean "lost sleep" (more Fatigue) or waking mid-firefight / in a KE cruiser. Cap routine Control Tests around 6/day plus special triggers. Injured CFD personalities who seize control typically flee.

**Infection of machines:** Device Rating 4+ / Pilot 4+ are prime targets; timing is plot-based. AIs struggle with hormones, eating, sleeping; e-ghosts adapt faster.

### Slowing CFD (no true cure yet)

| Method | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hunter-killer nanites | Make it worse: each application +1 NV |
| Spells targeting nanites | Drain +2 minimum; often fail (too small / too processed) |
| Chemicals | Can hurt soft nanites; hard nanites resist at non-lethal doses |
| Medical nanite extraction | Halve NV (min 1); extracted nanites can infect others or be studied |
| Combined multi-treatment | Best delay (e.g. Pulse + chemicals + blood scrub); duration is GM judgment |

### Head case advantages (CFD personality only)

**Attribute Boost**  
Simple Action. Roll Nanite Volume. Each hit: +1 to any Physical Attribute (distributable). Lasts hits rounds. Then take Stun equal to hits on the NV roll.

**Toxin Resistance**  
Add NV to Toxin Resistance or Damage Resistance vs sickness/toxin.

**Adrenal Control**  
Each Combat Round while Physical Condition Monitor is full: Willpower + Nanite Volume (2) to stay conscious (overflow rules still apply; enough damage can go conscious → dead).  
While Stun Condition Monitor is full: Simple Nanite Volume Test; remove Stun boxes equal to hits for hits minutes. When time ends, all removed damage returns +1 box per test made (may overflow into Physical / death). If overwrite still contested, original personality resurfaces after the CFD personality blacks out.
