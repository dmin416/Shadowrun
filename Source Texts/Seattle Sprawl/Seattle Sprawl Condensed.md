## THE METROPLEX — OVERVIEW

Seattle is a UCAS city-state carved out of Salish-Shidhe Council (NAN) territory by the Treaty of Denver, making it a UCAS island surrounded by NAN land and near Tír Tairngire. This isolation plus heavy Asian trade/immigration ties gives every megacorp a reason to plant a flag here — even Aztechnology and Saeder-Krupp compete in Seattle despite having the least natural claim. Combined with entrenched organized crime (Mafia, Yakuza, Triads, Vory, Koshari) and resident dragons, Seattle is considered **the shadowrunning capital of the world**.

Competition among runners is as dangerous as the powers that hire them. Any gun, including a chummer's, may turn on you.

**Geography:** Nestled between mountains (east) and Puget Sound (west); Mount Rainier looms over the sprawl. Mild, cloudy, rainy climate (frequent light rain more than downpours; Everett especially); Tacoma is the smelliest district. Fog common morning/evening (often foul from Barrens fires + industry). Temperature extremes rare. Wealth doesn't only cluster at the waterfront (Downtown's coast is rich; Tacoma/Everett coastlines are not). Bellevue wealth hugs Lake Washington; Redmond dumps trash/bodies into Lake Sammamish.

**Food:** Seattle imports most food (from Salish-Shidhe lands and fishing); vulnerable to supply disruption. Snohomish is the only major in-metroplex agricultural zone. Play nice with neighbors or starve.

**Infrastructure fragility:** Ports, SeaTac, and bridges over the Snohomish River are soft points. Shut enough of them and the sprawl spirals.

**Districts (13 total + islands):** Downtown, Bellevue, Tacoma, Everett, Renton, Auburn, Snohomish, Fort Lewis, Redmond, Puyallup, Council Island, Outremer (5 islands), Ork Underground (official district after Prop 23).

### Politics

- **Governor Kenneth Brackhaven** is the metroplex's top elected figure (not a citywide mayor). Humanis-adjacent politics without open formal ties; home in Snohomish. Has pressured Tacoma businesses away, run Tempo crackdowns, steered Camp Sealth (Fox) toward an all-human youth military pipeline, and left the Ork Underground reconstruction debt as a political bomb for successors.
- District mayors matter locally (e.g. Bellevue's Jonathan Blake; Redmond's Sonya Scholl).
- Corps and extraterritoriality sit beside UCAS law. Government can't legally kill you outright; corp turf can.

### Culture (runner-useful)

- **Change:** Ownership and Barrens geography shift constantly; "permanent address" is a joke in Redmond/Puyallup.
- **Openness:** More mixed teams/bars than many sprawls, despite Humanis and Brackhaven. Bystanders often "live and let live" (good for crime, bad if you're the victim).
- **Seattle Freeze:** Acceptance ≠ friendship. Warm intros thaw it; cold-calling strangers for a team does not.
- **Loyalty:** Clique/neighborhood loyalty over institutions. KE struggles to find informants; physical evidence matters more than witnesses. Limits still apply: Johnsons and teammates can sell you out.

- Everett sits on the north SSC road/checkpoint axis; astral watch common.
- Council Island forces down unauthorized aircraft (often magically) into the lake.
- Fox Island Bridge: daily migrant workers + Koshari/smuggler traffic; NAN-annexation pressure.
- Hardpoint rule of thumb: don't drag a KE chase into NAN airspace or Salish soldiers join the party.

## DOWNTOWN

**Pop 555,000** | Human 63% Elf 13% Dwarf 3% Ork 18% Troll 2% | Income 129,000¥/capita | 88% corp-affiliated

- Was literally "Seattle" before the Treaty of Denver; still the beating heart/skyline.
- **5 of the Big Ten** have regional HQs here (incl. Aztechnology Pyramid).
- Overlapping corp/government security zones; more shadowruns here than any other district.
- **Crime:** Finnigan Family (Dona Rowena O'Malley) controls docks/smuggling (consigliere Al Cavalieri ill since 2072; Ciarniello/Gianelli waiting). Yakuza (Shotozumi-gumi; Oyabun Hanzo Shotozumi; enforcer Katsu Takashima recently summoned) runs bunraku/protection. Vory via Gregory "Red Stare" Orlov: biggest black market/gunrunning in the sprawl.
- **Gangs:** Disassemblers (organleggers, Tamanous front, grey/white); Halloweeners (largest Downtown gang, orange/black, pyromaniacs, leader Nightmare); Troll Killers (all-human, Humanis-backed, red/green; wiped Bloody Screamers).
- **Key sites:** Dante's Inferno (9-level mega-club + Matrix host); Eye of the Needle (Space Needle restaurant, popular Mr. Johnson meet); Cuppa Joe's (neutral crime ground); Big Rhino (ork BBQ, Ork Underground gate); Aurora Mall; Northgate Mall; Pike Street Market.
- **Security:** Knight Errant's best officers; extremely proactive (2–3 units minimum).
- **Holidays:** Arcology Shutdown Remembrance Day (Dec 19, Renraku Arcology/Deus); Emerald City Music Festival (first weekend of June).

## BELLEVUE ("The Belle")

**Pop 213,000** | Human 74% Elf 17% | Income 115,000¥/capita | 94% corp-affiliated

- Where the rich sleep, between Lake Washington and Lake Sammamish; bordered by Redmond, Renton, Snohomish.
- Horizon-branded "idealized suburb" under mayor Jonathan Blake (rumored S-K plant / possibly not entirely human / not the first "Blake").
- Ork population engineered down from ~20% via redlining/gentrification. 16% below poverty despite near-universal corp affiliation.
- **Corps:** Ares ("the strip"); Gaeatronics Mountain (eco-tech HQ w/ underground reactor); Microdeck (the Node); Centurion SSG (Blake's private security, bought ex-NeoNET labs after AI-escape).
- **Crime:** Finnigan/Ciarniello Mafia rivalry (casino turf); Yakuza (Shotozumi-gumi) staffs Powerline Sex Club with bunraku; Kumon'go pushing drugs.
- **Key sites:** Gates Casino; Powerline Sex Club; Thomas Vintners (magically-enhanced winery, Ciarniello-linked); Main Place Arcade.
- **Security:** Split jurisdiction — Knight Errant (citywide) vs Lone Star (privately retained by Corporate Council over Ares's objection) — real interagency tension. Centurion operates with near-impunity. Toughest Matrix grid outside Downtown.
- **Holidays:** Snowflake Celebration (late Nov–Dec); Snowflake Festival (last Fri of Feb).

## TACOMA

**Pop 375,000** | Human 67% Ork 19% | Income 56,000¥/capita | 87% corp-affiliated

- Built around Shiawase's needs; declined after Nicaragua Canal diverted shipping.
- Worst Night of Rage violence; Sheraton Tacoma sheltered fleeing metahumans; **Crying Wall** memorial. Smelliest district (industrial + trash fires + fog).
- Brackhaven coaxed businesses away after Tacoma refused to play ball; 2-7 remembrances keep that tension alive.
- **Crime:**
  - **Shotozumi-gumi** dominant via **Nightrunner Security** front (Shiawase no-crime-association policy). Local So-honbucho / CEO: **Akio Shizuka**.
  - **Gianelli Mafia** clings to enclave around Gianelli's Restaurant (Tony Gianelli still works the floor; Don Joseph's grandfather). Lost DeClerry's to Kumon'go.
  - **Kumon'go** (Seoulpa): Tempo money; old leadership shipped to Silcox by Brackhaven task force. Now run by **Eric Kon** (money man) with First Nations shaman Booboo Littletree and the Yee twins (Neo-Tokyo wetwork fame).
- **Key sites:** Villa Plaza (Salish imports); Tacoma Mall; SeaTac Mall; Crying Wall; Silcox Island Correctional (corp black-site prison loophole); Fenris Nacht; Cathode Glow; Basil's Faulty Bar.
- **Holiday:** 2-7 Remembrance Day (Feb 7) — Night of Rage march to the Crying Wall.

## EVERETT

**Pop 230,000** | Human 71% Elf 14% | Income 59,000¥/capita | 70% corp-affiliated

- Balanced between gentrification and Barrens-style collapse; Federated-Boeing investment; Naval Station Everett (USS Colin Powell supercarrier as martial-law reminder).
- SSC border proximity: smuggling + TripleTree as Casablanca-style underworld crossroads.
- **Crime:** Mafia (Ciarniello/Finnigan) runs Casino Corner gambling/drugs; Yakuza casino **Breckenridge** (non-Japanese brand) for BTL muscle-in; Triads run prostitution near Naval Station. Alex Harrison's independent casinos (Garden of Eden / Shangri-La) hire runners to stay Mob-free; Mafia may fix urban brawl to break him.
- **Key sites:** Casino Corner; **Boeingville** (on-site housing, heavy ID/patrols, critter sewers); **Bicson Biomedicals** (Shiawase bioware/cyber adaptation; post-CFD clearance); **Danny's** (Infected-hunter front after de Vries cleared a vampire); **TripleTree Inn** (troll Colton's semi-legal squat hotel, contact hub); **Kondorchid Shipping Depot** (drones, false bottoms, Wuxing pressure).
- **Security:** KE most places; Boeingville and Naval Station are separate hard zones. Dilapidated edges get slow response (not Barrens-level, but dawdling).
- **Holidays:** Super Brawl Sunday (Casino Corner gambling); Fleet Week (early July).

## RENTON ("Seattle's Apartment")

**Pop 220,000** | Human 64% Ork 21% | Income 91,000¥/capita | 68% corp-affiliated

- Bland aspirational middle-class suburbia; wageslave retirement belt. Hit hard by Crash 2.0, recovering with new Matrix.
- Quiet but persistent Humanis stronghold since Night of Rage; anti-metahuman bias is subtle/systemic.
- **Crime / street:**
  - **Neighborhood Watch:** Civic veneer, mostly Humanis allies; "help" KE; patrols escalating to lethal ammo; KE often looks away.
  - **Blood Mountain Boys:** Go-gang; KE nearly crushed them 2074–75; survivors went pseudo-underground with custom ballistic "war masks"; heavy meta recruitment feeds Renton paranoia.
  - **Night Hunters:** Loose human thrill-packs (not one gang); cyber blades; torture/murder metas; BTL money; do favors for Neighborhood Watch so KE stays soft.
- **Key sites:** BBC Weapon Works (Dorothy "Momma Dot" Simms, troll armorer; sprawl-wide clientele); KE training academy presence; Auburn Center / Renton Center Malls.
- **Holidays:** Black Friday shopping riots (legendary, "tactical" shopper teams); Family Day.

## AUBURN

**Pop 210,000** | Human 54% Ork 27% | Income 34,000¥/capita | 59% corp-affiliated

- Industrial "hum": 363 sq km of nonstop factory equipment. Blue-collar, insular; outsiders need local connections.
- Cheapest metroplex gear/services via automated manufacturing. Boeing manufacturing base; "new Silicon Valley" startup tension with old locals.
- Race tensions; struggling Mafia foothold; Don Joseph Gianelli also stretched across Tacoma/Auburn/Puyallup.
- **Holidays:** Boeing Days (2nd Sun of May–Memorial Day carnival, partly layoff-funded); Get Stuck (Mitch Stuck's July 29 carnival/street performer competition).

## SNOHOMISH ("Quiet Death" in Salish)

**Pop 118,000** | Human 88% (least diverse) | Income 35,000¥/capita | 73% corp-affiliated

- Agricultural breadbasket: wheat, corn, soy, apples, berries, greenhouses, modified salmon/seaweed pens, NatVat facilities. Magical antiques trade. Popular for corp/gov black-site safehouses and black-ops camps on abandoned farmland.
- **Governor Brackhaven's home district.** Humanis Policlub + Human Nation (Flaming Sword rumors) are the real order-keepers: more violence and crime protection than any street gang, rarely arrested. KE precincts overwhelmingly human.
- **Mafia:** Capo **Angelo Luposcelli** (Finnigan): all-human soldatos with heavy aug; guns/drugs to Humanis; BTLs (hot Calhots); discreet safehouses (cabins to private hotel floors). Has stonewalled even Dona O'Malley on who he's hiding.
- **Gangs (rising, not gone):** Food-scare era saw corps/locals quietly erase turf grabs; **present change** is footholds near Monroe (SSC border) and Lord Hill Park. Suspect backers: SSC and/or Urubia. Active: **First Nations**, **Rusted Stilettos**, **Crimson Crush**, **Ancients** (avoiding each other, possible collusion).
- NatVat / Aztech training-farm rumors; Thrashers Correctional is hell for non-humans.

## FORT LEWIS

**Pop 99,250** | Human 60% | Income 30,000¥/capita | 38% corp-affiliated (lowest of upper-tier districts)

- Military dystopia: clean, orderly, authoritarian. Lush, eerie quiet under boots and "Taps." Vice exists but stays hidden.
- **Paydays 1st and 15th** (mood crashes in the lean days before). McChord Airfield adjacency.
- **Key sites:** Fort Lewis Zoological Gardens (family attraction + covert critter research/weaponization; Britta Styk hires for rare catches); **Urban Combat Simulator** (detailed Seattle Center replica incl. camera placement: dry-run gold if you can fake access).
- **Crime middlemen (not based on-base):** Connie "Two Spikes" Dutchman (Tacoma) supplies narcotics; **Tony Lee** (Yakuza, Puyallup brothels) handles trafficking into Fort Lewis and hires runners to protect that pipeline.
- **Opposition:** Military Police / CID under **Col. Wayne Frank** (no use for runners; marshals, plainclothes, SWAT). I-5 corridor friction with KE jurisdiction edges.

## REDMOND (The Barrens)

**Pop 428,000 (est.)** | Human 74% Ork 12% | Income 6,400¥/capita | 23% corp-affiliated | 78% without 12 years schooling

- Former tech district; collapsed after 2029 Crash; classic Barrens. Gang fiefdoms + wasteland; utilities jury-rigged; astral space degraded ("swimming in jelly" for non-natives).
- Mayor **Sonya Scholl** (Socialist, unopposed, 10+ years): fights corps; hires runners herself.
- **Organized crime:** Finnigan presence; Yakuza **Shigeda** clan (Yuuma Shigeda, banished scion earning honor back via Barrens work). Real power is gangs.
- **Gangs:**
  - Brain Eaters (Touristville; electronics scavengers; black/white + red fezzes)
  - Crimson Crush (Central Redmond; all-ork; oldest; red; heavy taxes, keeps order)
  - Red Hot Nukes (dwarf go-gang; gray/red)
  - Halloweeners (citywide chaos; orange/black; **Jackal's Lantern** bar/HQ for Halloween riots; not a permanent Redmond turf empire)
  - Rusted Stilettos (Glow City; black/rust-red; mostly troll/ork)
  - Death Heads (520 Freeway; black/chrome go-gang; smuggling → protection)
  - Spiders (Brain Heaven HQ; black/brown + red webs; insect-spirit hunters under Widow; numbers thin, hunted by other gangs)
- **Notable sites:** Funhouse (Urubia's lair); Glow City (2013 Beaver Lake meltdown; Isotopes toxic shamans for Kalanyr); Mana (free magical training for gangers); Hollywood Correctional (largest prison; oddly desirable: food/shelter).
- **Security:** KE patrols Redmond/Bellevue border only; otherwise no-man's-land.
- **Holidays:** Halloween (Halloweeners citywide from Jackal's Lantern); **Black Mondays** (3rd Monday monthly: Anarchist Black Cross free clinics/food).

## PUYALLUP (The Other Barrens)

**Pop 512,000** | Human 47% Elf 21% Ork 22% | Income 6,500¥/capita | 18% corp-affiliated | 81% without 12 yrs schooling

- Second Barrens-tier district (~60 km from Redmond). Farmland → Mt. Rainier refugees, Night of Rage victims, Tír exiles/"leftovers." Outsiders treat it as a place to slum, buy drugs, race (**Route 7** smuggling run), or "bash keeblers." Seattle's "dirty secret" to people who don't live there.
- **Tarislar:** Elf city-within-a-city. Night of Rage survivors + NAN rejection + Tír indifference left elves stuck here since 2039. Community council hired KE; locals run sanitation/water/power/food. Young mages often volunteer at **Deireadh An Tuartheil**; others join gangs. Angry, long-lived, chip-on-shoulder population.

### Crime Scene

- **Ancients** (HQ Tarislar; green mohawks; citywide tags/sub-chapters): Seattle's flashiest elf go-gang. Rumored Tír Peace Force alumni / Princes' muscle. Leader **Belial** (after father Green Lucifer promoted); advisor **Sting** (old guard); bosses **Rook** (combat mage, elf supremacist) and **Bushido Blitz** (razorboy).
- **Laésa:** Elven smugglers; laés (memory-wipe drug); fluid HQ; less fixed than Ancients.
- **Gianelli Mafia:** Don **Joseph Gianelli** (also Tacoma/Auburn); day-to-day Capo **Enzo Gianelli** (bodyguard Uranus of Order of Merlyn). Hackers on retainer include Wendy Wyld, Two-Bit; Highball freelances.
- **Kenran-Kai Yakuza:** Oyabun **Kosuke Tomizawa**; metahuman-open ranks; slow turf take from Gianellis; street racing as informal interview; Spirit Focus is a Tomizawa hangout.
- Other color: Chulos, Reality Hackers, Blue Dragons (poseur/weed gangs).

### Key sites

- **Crime Mall:** Failed '29 shopping center; community black market. **"The Management"** (5-seat council) flat-taxes dealers.
- Bump and Sleep (Gianelli dive/safe flop); Spirit Focus (Kenran-Kai watched).
- Rainier ash / Aug 17 ribbon memorials (local color).

## COUNCIL ISLAND

**Pop 4,000 (+ ~150 unclassified/unknown sapients)** | Ork 31% Elf 25% Human 34% | Income 56,175¥/capita | Only 2% corp-affiliated

- Formerly Mercer Island; ceded to Salish-Shidhe as ambassadorial territory after First Treaty of Denver.
- **Primary NAN diplomatic seat** in Seattle after Denver negotiations collapsed.
- Salish razed modern development for restored "natural" look with hidden modern tech (wireless in art/fountains).
- Heavy Salish-Shidhe Battalion presence (mostly ork troops, Cascade Ork tribe notable).
- NAN airspace: unauthorized aircraft forced down (often magically) into the lake.
- **Crime:** Only **First Nations** gang tolerated (leader Blood-of-the-Buffalo bases in Everett to avoid provoking island authorities). Drugs/BTLs tolerated; prostitution and protection rackets are not.
- **Holiday:** Indigenous Peoples Day (2nd Monday of October).

## OUTREMER (The Five Islands)

Collectively one district; Puget Sound islands in the Metroplex but functionally detached. North to south: **Bainbridge, Vashon, Fox, McNeil, Anderson.** Bainbridge dominates politics via population. Natives drop "Island" from the name when speaking.

### Bainbridge Island

- MCT-developed since 2074 (ahead of Olympics); grown skyscraper towers where upper-echelon corporate/government/crime elites live self-contained (never need to go below the 6th floor). Extremely self-isolating even before MCT.

### Vashon Island

- Largest and richest; effectively no service underclass (wealthy pay high wages to human staff; drones/riggers do the rest). Home of the Vashon fashion label; Fashion Week four times a year.

### Fox Island

- Most NAN-friendly Metroplex piece; migrant workers cross Fox Island Bridge from SSC daily.
- Rising NAN-annexation movement, increasingly violent. **Koshari** dominate local crime (pushed in from SSC).
- **Camp Sealth:** Former kids' camp → youth military training; under Brackhaven, enrollment spiked and went all-human ("Aryan youth army" rumor).
- Go-gang color: Kerrigan "Mustang" Blue-RiverDeer / Warpath-linked Native go-gang pressing annexation.

### McNeil Island

- Historic prison (est. 1875), still Lone Star–run; town of Reflection (prisoner labor) + Reflection's Edge outside the wall. McNeil Regattas (July–Aug).

### Anderson Island

- Old-money isolationist (vs Vashon new-rich / Bainbridge corp). Political fights: Metroplex housing expansion vs wealthy residents blocking development.

## THE ORK UNDERGROUND

**Official count: 40,000 | Skraacha independent count: ~200,000** | Ork 82% Troll 7% (official) | Income 13,000¥/capita | 7% corp-affiliated | ~94% SINless (pre-Renraku SINs)

### History (compressed)

1. **1889** Great Fire → elevate city two stories; lower levels sealed.
2. **1907** disease scare → further seal (JackPoint aside).
3. Late 20th c. tourism/transit patches; most left raw.
4. **2011** Mt. Rainier quake → closed again (collapse/poison-gas fear); homeless then goblinized reopen pathways.
5. **2039 Night of Rage:** Gov. Victor Allenson's camps burn Feb 7; underground passages save thousands. Allenson burned in effigy every year since.
6. Mid-'40s ork/dwarf falling-out → dwarf exodus → "Ork Underground."
7. **Prop 23:** Official city district. Renraku SINs/admin; gentrification; Reclaimer return.

### Present conflict

- **Renraku:** 44,000 non-criminal SINs allotted (running low); "helpful lawyer" PR while enabling displacement; paid by city while debt is blamed on "uppity orks"; first hospital breaking ground. Coolhunters watching fashion ('50s ork callback).
- **Reclaimers:** Dwarfs returning with deeds; **Gimli Harris** is the angry face; hire **Hard Corps** to evict ork families. Heritage claim vs hate claim.
- **Evo:** Only mega with a pre-Prop-23 toehold; slow to react to Renraku.
- **Crime:** Mafia, Triads, Yakuza all failed to stick. **Seoulpa Rings** succeeded (tech + goblin acceptance). **Skraacha** ("Scorchers"): largest gang / ethnic-mafia protectors. **Bot'Kham:** next largest; predators resisting Seattleite invasion. Dozens of smaller gangs.
- **Security mix:** **Neo-PD** on Tourist Highway (mall-cop look, strong Matrix/intel; often human). **Hard Corps** for dwarf shops/evictions. Skraacha as community muscle (older residents distrust them; hope Neo-PD brings "normalcy"). Mapsofts/GPS useless; hire a guide.
- **Politics:** "UnderGov" forming; first official UnderMayor elections contested (Gimli Harris among candidates; Mongo the troll DQ'd on paperwork).
- **Entrance:** Big Rhino Downtown (primary gatekeeper; owner unknown), among others. Tourist Highway / The Biz for commerce. Thermographic guide-strips in carved tunnels (120 cm height). Rat-on-a-stick is the local culinary crime.
- **Holiday movement:** Recognition Day around Prop 23 (date not fixed; election-tied). Night of Woe / 2-7 remembrances matter here too.
