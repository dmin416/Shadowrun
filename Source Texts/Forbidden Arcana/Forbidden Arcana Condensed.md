## 05 - Magic Mastery

**Pointer:** Yucatan rebels' "magic bullets" frame alchemical armorer research; mastery as specializing where dragons/elves outlive you.

### Mastery quality rules (general)

- Require Magic; usually Magician / Adept / Aspected / Mystic Adept.
- Buyable at chargen if all requirements bought first.
- **Cost never doubles** after chargen (unlike normal qualities).
- Count toward positive quality Karma cap.
- May trade Priority Magic spells for **5 Karma each** toward mastery qualities (leftover Karma cannot be kept).
- Skill specialization req: may skip if skill is **2 ranks higher** than listed.
- Tradition tag in brackets: that tradition lowers **all skill requirements by 2**.

### Core / addiction (SR5)

- **Martyred (self-harm for Blood Magic Points):** no Addiction test; damage penalties apply after spell finishes.
- Addiction: Rating = Magic, Threshold 2 (+1 if BMP spent on one effect > Magic).
- Addiction triggers: overcast with BMP; spend > 2x Initiate BMP on one effect; kill for BMP; harm helpless for BMP; use victim's BMP to harm them (+2 resist if defending).
- Burnout fail Addiction: must kill next sapient (not lose Body/Will).

## 10 - Where the Wild Things Are

**Pointer:** Post-Yellowstone wild spirit ecology essay (nature, urban, vehicles, elementals) plus calling/leashing rules.

### Wild Reputation / Index

- Wild Reputation +1 per 25 Wild Index (starts 0).
- +Wild Rep dice to calling / social vs wild spirits.
- May burn 2 Wild Rep to clear 1 Astral Rep.

**Sample Index gains:** Disrupt toxic = Force; destroy on home plane = Force x2; cleanse = Force x3; cleanse toxic site = Rating x10; free ally = Force x3; bind any spirit = Index loss (per chart); etc. Same action once / 24h.

### Calling wild spirits

Cannot Conjure normally.

1. **Force:** Summoning + Magic [Force] vs Force; need hits ≥ 1 + Astral Rep → appears with **0 services**.
2. **Social at site:** Con/Etiquette/Negotiation/Performance hits ≥ Force (Intimidation = challenge).
3. **Sacrifice:** 1 Karma (blood/hair/emotion) always attracts if present.

### Spirit markers (Rating 1-6)

Usually offered at Wild Rep + 1. Trade Karma ≈ Force, Status behavior, favors. Functions like bound services; anyone (even mundane) may hold. Marker party always "present" for calling.

### Banishing / binding wild

Banishing + Magic [Astral] vs Force x2 (cumulative); Drain = hits on spirit defense (min 2). After disrupt: until end of next action may Bind as normal (any tradition). Bound wild in domain cannot leave; will seek revenge after.

### Domain quality

Bound to location; disrupted linger astrally (destroyable); Accident/Guard/Search use Magic x2.
