## Tools of the Trade

### Gear guidance (no new stats)

- Prefer mission-appropriate weapons over attachment to one class; avoid full-auto if collateral is a concern.
- Accessories: silencers, smartlinks over laser sights, concealable holsters/arm slides, recoil compensation if using automatics.
- Ammo: gel/shock, subsonic with silencer, frangible for over-penetration risk, tracking-agent loads, capsule rounds (chemist-dependent).
- Always carry a melee option (or implanted spur/razor).
- Armor: armor-lined trenchcoat for street look; chameleon suit for sneaking; Zoe / Mortimer of London for formal armored clothing.
- Augmentations: subtle only; cyberguns, spurs/razors common; avoid obvious cyberlimbs, flashy cybereyes, dragonhide-style skin.
- Electronics: hardened commlink; RFID tags; jammers; maglock passkeys/sequencers, cellular glove molders, keycard copiers, autopickers.

**Pointer:** Quietus' implant list and Clockwork's Triad-dock / combat-drone job gone wrong (honor vs collateral). See [05 - Tools of the Trade](05%20-%20Tools%20of%20the%20Trade.md).

### Assassin's Creeds (new Qualities)

Expands Code of Honor: Assassin's Creed (p. 79, SR5). These are separate qualities: Karma costs/bonuses below do **not** include the base Code of Honor bonus; that quality is not a prerequisite.

Pick the creed that matches what matters most to the character (innocents, country, professionalism, etc.).

#### Positive Qualities

##### Deus Vult! (Cost: 4 Karma)

Religious crusade against enemies of the faith (any religion).

**Advantage:** At the start of the character's Action Phase, if they took Stun or Physical damage since their last Action Phase, roll Willpower vs threshold = wounds taken in that time. Success: ignore all wound penalties. Keep rolling each Action Phase; threshold grows with cumulative wounds since the advantage started (e.g. 2 wounds then 2 more → threshold 4). On failure, all wound penalties apply immediately and this advantage cannot be used until the next combat.

**Disadvantage:** To act against their religion's tenets, roll Charisma + Willpower; threshold scales with severity (theft ~1; blasphemy/renouncing much higher). Failure: refuse the action and actively stop others attempting it.

##### My Country, Right or Wrong (Cost: 4 Karma)

Patriotism; kills for the nation's peace and security.

**Advantage:** Same wound-penalty ignore Willpower check as Deus Vult! (threshold = wounds since last Action Phase; grows; failure ends benefit until next combat).

**Disadvantage:** To act against their country, roll Charisma + Willpower; threshold scales (steal from a military base ~1; sell classifieds to the enemy much higher). Failure: refuse and stop others attempting it.

##### Out for Myself (Cost: 4 Karma)

Looks out for Number One only.

**Advantage:** +3 dice pool to all Surprise Tests.

**Disadvantage:** Double all Notoriety from acts of selfishness or betrayal.

##### Strive for Perfection (Cost: 12 Karma)

Every kill done efficiently, quickly, without missteps.

**Advantage:** Halve Called Shot cost: -2 dice pool instead of -4; still costs a Free Action.

**Disadvantage:** Must make Called Shots unless providing covering fire or similar cases where Called Shots are impractical.

#### Negative Quality

##### Consummate Professional (Bonus: 3 Karma)

Never discusses jobs; hard to find, trusted when found.

**Advantage:** +2 dice pool to social rolls when interacting with employers.

**Disadvantage:** Street Cred = total Karma / 20 (round down), instead of / 10.
