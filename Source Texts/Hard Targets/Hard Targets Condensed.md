### Sukuyan (Infected quality vs Vampire, *Run Faster* p. 140)

- Remove: Induced Dormancy (Lack of Air); -4 swimming.
- Add: Dietary Requirement (Salt); Vulnerability (Wood).
- May drink alcohol/fruit juice without nausea.
- Immunity (Toxins) does **not** protect vs Awakened drugs or Awakened critter toxins.
- Market: ~50,000¥/vampire; maintenance doses 7,500¥; Zobop monopoly.

### Januarius (Sukuyan-only control drug)

- Vector: Ingestion, Injection | Speed: 1 CT | Duration: 7 days | Addiction: Both
- Effect: +1 Physical Limit; Euphoria (8 - Body hours, min 1)
- Addiction Rating 8 / Threshold 3
- Prep: Alchemy + Magic [Astral] (12, 8 hours) Extended; glitch = fail; critical may harm preparer
- Addicted: diet once/7 days; sunlight damage interval doubled (every 2 min vs 1)

### Renfield (vampire pawn drug)

- Vector: Ingestion, Injection | Speed: 1 CT | Duration: 7 days | Addiction: Both
- Effect: A+1, I+1, S+1, +1 Physical Limit, Euphoria (8-Body hrs min 1), +1D6 Initiative
- Addiction Rating 8 / Threshold 3

- Addicted: Immunity (Age) while dosed regularly; Essence Loss weakness; 1D6 Essence/dose, max 2× natural Essence
- Sense vampire within Essence meters

### Corps Cadavre (Minion ritual)

- Homunculus-style on corpses; Body total ≤ Force × 10; duration = net hits × sum Magic
- Skills = half Force (round up); control limit: 2 × Charisma corps
- Stats: B = corpse Body; A = F-2; R = F-2; S = F; W/L/I/C = 1; Init (F+1)+1D6; Move A×2/A×3
- Skills: Assensing, Astral Combat, Perception, Unarmed Combat; Powers: Sapience (+ optional Magic Resistance, Skill)
- Free corps = **Grand Zombie**; +1 power per 2 Willpower; Force substitutes Magic for powers
- Resist once/day: Logic + Willpower Extended vs Force; end of duration → dust; no mage/decker corps

### Zubembie powder

- Contact | Immediate | Pen 0 | Power 12 | Paralysis + Zombie Prep
- -3 Willpower (min 1) for toxin duration
- Prep: Chemistry + Logic [Mental] (8, 4 hours) Extended

### Zombie (Minion) Ritual

- Min Force = subject Willpower; if no powder, Seal resists with (Force×2) + Essence or Magic (higher); else Force×2
- Duration: net hits × sum Magic; control max 2× Charisma zombies
- Physical attrs unchanged; mental halved; Force as counterspelling vs mana; no wound penalties; no speak
- Resist once/day Logic + Willpower Extended vs Force
