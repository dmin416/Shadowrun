### New Mentor Spirits

#### Raven (Alt)

Followers collect facts, data, arcana, and useful gear.

**Advantages:** All: +2 dice pool to knowledge skill tests. Magician: +2 dice to Detection spells, preparations, and rituals **or** +2 dice to summoning spirits if summoner also has Code of Honor: Harmony with Nature, The Shaman's Code and bargains with the spirit (choose one). Adept: 2 free levels of Enhanced Perception.

**Disadvantages:** When presented with valuable data, knowledge, or gear (GM discretion), Charisma + Willpower (3) or unable to resist trying to acquire it.

**Similar:** Crow, Collector. (*Forbidden Arcana* for Alternate Mentor Spirits.)

#### Lion

Proud warrior; pride before all; no subtlety or cowardice when it is time to fight (ambush/stealth OK until then).

**Advantages:** All: +2 dice to Survival, Running, or Tracking (choose one). Magician: +2 dice to Combat spells, preparations, and rituals. Adept: 2 free levels of Attribute Boost (Strength).

**Disadvantages:** Never leave comrades behind; fight to death to protect allies. Charisma + Willpower (3) to flee before team is safe. Actions that don't benefit the team: Physical and Mental limits −2.

**Similar:** Tiger, Strength.
