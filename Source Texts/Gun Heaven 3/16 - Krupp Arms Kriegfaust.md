# Krupp Arms Kriegfaust

**Source:** Gun Heaven 3 | `Source/PDF/Shadowrun_5E_Gun_H(e)aven_3.pdf` | PDF page index 14-14

**(ASSAULT RIFLE)**

A veteran of the Kilamanjaro cleansing, the Kriegfaust was designed to equip the larger members of a military command with a robust weapon suited to their needs.

The drum magazine is better suited to larger hands and less likely to be dropped in high-adrenaline situations.

The Kriegfaust also has a frame-mounted imaging scope, to ensure the shooter is able to get the maximum ballistic performance from the weapon's large-caliber rounds.

Standard Upgrades/Accessories: Metahuman Customization, Melee Hardening, Imaging Scope (w/ Vision Enhancement 1, Vision Magnification)

> I'd be impressed if the ad didn't read like a Humanis "You're too stupid to own a gun" brochure. Not every one of us needs to have reinforced weapons just to carry them. You treat them with respect and they don't fail you when needed.
