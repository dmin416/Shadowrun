# Krime Spree

**Source:** Gun Heaven 3 | `Source/PDF/Shadowrun_5E_Gun_H(e)aven_3.pdf` | PDF page index 9-9

**(SUBMACHINE GUN)**

A new telescoping bolt design intended for those needing alternative ergonomics, the Spree is an economic choice for the discriminating shooter. The design is intended to allow for the easy mounting of a wide array of external aiming and tactical aids. While Krime does not currently manufacture such devices, a call to our friendly Customer Service department will provide a number of helpful suggestions based on the caller's location.
