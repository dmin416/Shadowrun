# New Weapon Upgrades/Traits

**Source:** Gun Heaven 3 | `Source/PDF/Shadowrun_5E_Gun_H(e)aven_3.pdf` | PDF page index 2 (New Weapon Upgrades/Traits)

## Vintage

The weapon is not compatible with modern electronics. This can be the result of outdated manufacturing, physical age, or bad design. Physical upgrades are possible, but adding them requires additional work that costs twice the normal listed amount.

## Cap & Ball

The weapon is designed to be hand loaded with separate powder, shot, and percussion cap.  It takes three Complex Actions to reload each round; stopping before completing the reload activity requires the user to start over again. These weapons are annotated with "(cb)" in their Ammo entry.
