# Cavalier Arms Gladius

**Source:** Gun Heaven 3 | `Source/PDF/Shadowrun_5E_Gun_H(e)aven_3.pdf` | PDF page index 11-11

**(SUBMACHINE GUN)**

A simple design intended for street officers requiring temporary increases in firepower, the Gladius combines modern electronics with an easy-to-maintain design. The weapon can also be used to equip a hastily raised militia; its simple operation and maintenance requirements make it ideal for citizen-soldiers. The stamped metal receiver is key to the weapon's strength, while proprietary recoil absorbers ensure accuracy and the electronics package retains calibration.
