## 06 - Magical Traditions

**Story/hooks:** Quaha and Lyran investigate an Aztlan murder via book lore vs astral reading; Jimmy No surveys why belief shapes mana (theories only).

**Core tradition rules** (*SR5* p. 279; this chapter expands):
- Preferred spells / adept powers are **flavor only** (no mechanical bonus).
- Mage may only summon the spirits listed for their tradition; tasks outside that spirit’s category get no response.
- **Possession tradition:** replace Materialization with Possession (*SG* p. 197) on summoned spirits.
- Magical lodge must match the user’s tradition or it does not work.

## 07 - Magical Societies

**Story/hooks:** Soft Seven fiction (Evo kids seeking a shadow patron); Lothan the Wise catalogs society types and named groups for runners.

**Society types (pointers only):** Social/networking; informational (legal Matrix vs black magic exchanges, often need member intro); causal (shared cause, e.g. Mystic Crusaders); conspiratorial; initiatory (small, hierarchical; distinct from a simple initiatory group).

**General mechanical notes:**
- Public membership can pad a fake SIN (cheap, scan-friendly detail).
- Mundane / periphery members usually pay dues, attend when asked, and may provide security; rival-society members rarely allowed at private meetings.
- Exact discount percentages and lodge Forces are rarely quantified; treat listed dues, requirements, and access as the hard numbers.

### Toxic Magic

**Tradition shift:** Becoming toxic abandons prior tradition for a new Toxic Tradition (*SR5* p. 279): choose any five toxic spirits to summon + any Mental Attribute (+ Willpower) for Drain.

**Avenger vs Poisoner (agenda only):** revenge/broad guilt vs revel in spreading pollution.

**Toxic adepts:** Same motivations; appearance universally loathsome; normal adept rules + toxic-exclusive powers.

**Toxic mentor spirits:** Optional; use mentor rules (*SR5* p. 320).

#### Toxic mentor: Doom

- **All:** +2 Demolitions or one Combat skill  
- **Magician:** +2 Combat spells/preparations/rituals  
- **Adept:** Free Killing Hands  
- **Disadvantage:** Willpower + Charisma (3) to avoid/stop a fight once begun or to not enact a violence plan; on fail, fight until foes defeated or flee  
- Archetypes: Destroyer, Reaper

#### Toxic mentor: Disease

- **All:** +2 resist pathogens and toxins  
- **Magician:** +2 summon/bind plague spirits (toxic spirits of man)  
- **Adept:** Free Plague Cloud (*SG* p. 173)  
- **Disadvantage:** Charisma + Willpower (3) to not flee/seek cover in combat unless outnumbering foes (re-test if numbers drop)  
- Archetypes: Pestilent Rat, Contagion

#### Toxic mentor: Mutation

- **All:** +2 to one Physical skill  
- **Magician:** +2 Health spells/preparations/rituals  
- **Adept:** Free Attribute Boost (level 2) for one Physical Attribute  
- **Disadvantage:** If inferior to an opponent, −1 all actions until that rival is beaten or the gap is otherwise closed  
- Archetypes: Mutant Beast, Dark Darwinist

#### Toxic mentor: Pollution

- **All:** +2 Chemistry with pollutants; may default even without Chemistry  
- **Magician:** +2 summon/bind toxic air, earth, **or** water spirits (choose one)  
- **Adept:** Free Toxic Strike (*SG* p. 176)  
- **Disadvantage:** In cleaner-than-home areas, Willpower + Charisma (3) daily; fail = spend ≥8 hours that day despoiling the land  

#### Sites of corruption

- Background count typically **1–6**; always **toxic Domains**
- Toxic Awakened at least acclimated, usually aligned: Domain Rating raises Magic-linked limits (see Background Count, *SG* p. 30)
- Magical lodges often built on polluted sites

#### Twisted Arts (toxic-exclusive)

| Who | Access |
|-----|--------|
| Toxic magicians | Spells: Pollutant Stream, Pollutant Wave (p. 106); Radiation Beam, Radiation Burst (p. 105). Metamagics: Corruption, Taint (below). Ritual: Leeching (p. 133) |
| Toxic adepts | Toxic Strike (p. 176); Plague Cloud (p. 173) |

**Corruption (metamagic):** Banishing Test with +initiate grade; if Summoning then succeeds after services reduced to 0, spirit becomes toxic version.

**Taint (metamagic):** As Cleansing (*SG* p. 122) but **increases** background count.

### Blood Magic

- Blood mage in play terms: has learned select metamagics/rituals; generally once **Sacrifice** is known.
- Cannot self-teach Sacrifice: only same-tradition teacher who knows it, or a spirit that knows it.
- Aura shows blood magic (strongly negative to most assensing).
- Illegal nearly everywhere; Aztlan exception (religious).

#### Metamagic: Sacrifice

1. Inflict Physical damage with a **melee weapon that draws blood**.
2. Melee attack; if victim restrained/unable to defend: unopposed + **+4 dice**.
3. **Blood Magic Points:**
   - Non-sapient or **willing** victim (incl. self): **1 point per 3 boxes** Physical
   - Sapient critter or **unwilling** metahuman: **1 point per 1 box** Physical
   - Spirits never valid victims (even possessing a body)
4. Unused points lost after the following Combat Turn.
5. Spend points (any mix):
   - +1 Force of spell per point (may exceed 2× Magic)
   - +1 effective Magic for Drain Stun vs Physical threshold per point
   - −1 Drain per point (min 0)
   - Store in athame
   - Summon blood spirit

#### Metamagic: Cannibalize (adepts / mystic adepts)

- Generate Blood Magic Points as Sacrifice.
- Spend **3 points** → +1 to a Physical Attribute (up to augmented max); mix attributes; **one Simple Action**.
- Duration: (Magic × initiate grade) Combat Turns; spend Karma = total attribute points raised → duration becomes (Magic × initiate grade) **days**.

#### Metamagic: Power Bleed (adepts; prereq Cannibalize)

- As Cannibalize, but instead of 3 points → siphon **one** critter or adept power from victim; use as own.
- Duration (Magic × initiate grade) CT; spend **3 Karma** → days.

#### Focus: Athame

- Blood-mage exclusive; must know Sacrifice to bond.
- Stores Blood Magic Points from **Blood Rite** ritual; Free Action to spend as Sacrifice uses when casting.
- Capacity = Force.
- Decay of stored points (from initial Blood Rite): after **1 week**, **1 month**, and **3 months**, **half** of current unused points are lost each interval.

#### Blood spirits

- Summon with Sacrifice; Blood Magic Points generated = **max Force**.
- Bindable normally.
- Need Essence Drain to remain; Essence 0 → depart native metaplane.

**Blood Spirit**  
B A R S W L I C M ESS EDG: F+2 / F+2 / F / F+2 / F / F−1 / F / F / F / F / F/2  
Phys (F×2)+2D6; Astral (F×2)+3D6  
Skills: Assensing, Astral Combat, Perception, Running, Unarmed Combat  
Powers: Astral Form, Binding, Energy Drain (Essence, Touch, Physical Damage), Fear, Materialization  
Optional: Concealment, Confusion, Guard, Movement, Natural Weapon (DV = F P, AP -), Noxious Breath  
Weakness: Essence Loss (1/day)

#### Blood rituals

- Ritual leader with Sacrifice may generate points and spend them **1:1 as reagent drams** (Give the Offering).
- Group all knowing Sacrifice: **Blood Rite** stores points into athames as desired.

#### Blood Spirit Invocation

- Sacrifice + Invocation → Summon Great Form Spirit ritual with **metahuman victim killed** to fuel ascension.
- If Greater Power granted: unique **Hemorrhage**.

**Hemorrhage:** Type P; Complex; Range [Magic] m; Instant. Magic + Willpower [Force] vs Strength (unaugmented) + Willpower per living target in range. Net hits = unresisted Physical boxes; any damage also Nausea (*SR5* p. 409); incapacitate by nausea only if boxes &gt; Willpower.

### Insect Spirits

#### Summoning / inhabitation

- Need metahuman agent initially; without vessel, summoned insect fades in hours (Evanescence).
- Prepare vessel for Inhabitation (*SG* p. 195); cocoon by Queen/Mother, shaman, or ally.
- Placing spirit in host stops Evanescence.
- Shaman may add Binding skill to either Inhabitation dice pool; Queen/Mother may add Force to spirit or vessel pool.

#### Insect shamans

- Contact insect mentor of one type; abandon prior tradition; summon only that insect’s spirits.
- Mentor gives **no** numeric bonuses/penalties.
- Build hive; hive insects obsessively work toward Queen.

Lodge often = breeding ground; fetishes from insect materials; bizarre gestures/clicking; shamanic mask common.

#### Queen / Mother summoning

1. Know **Invocation**; lodge Force ≥ desired Force.
2. Create spirit formula as for ally (*SG* p. 200).
3. Transform an honored **nymph** via normal Summoning; becomes Queen/Mother.
4. On success: automatically **free spirit**; becomes hive focus; gains Astral Gateway (no longer needs shaman to summon).
5. May spirit-pact with shaman; shaman summons only one Queen/Mother ever.
6. Disrupted: returns home; same process to bring back.
7. **Destroyed:** shaman Magic → **0** (cannot raise); mental wreck. True-forms of hive destroyed; flesh/hybrid forms lose cohesion/intelligence.

### Evanescence (Weakness)

- Every 24 hours in full astral form: permanently −1 Force; at 0, dissipates/returns home.
- After ≥1 Force lost: −3 Assensing to spot them; +3 to pass mana barriers.
- Negated while inhabiting/possessing a vessel; resumes if driven out.
