### Astral topography (perception notes)

Astral mirrors physical as grey shadows; no hearing meat din; no reading text/tech displays; lifeless objects dull and passable; auras illuminate; emotions color; perception is psychic (sense modality varies by mage).

### Astral rifts

- Thin barrier between metaplanes; bridge for Awakened and mundanes.
- Uninitiated may cross without Dweller on the Threshold (SR5 p. 317).
- May be unstable; if closed, Awakened need another way back.
- Awakened may remain on metaplane without fading.
- **Mundanes:** Bound to the rift. If body leaves rift area or rift closes, fade in **hours = Essence**.

**Samples:** Watergate Rift DC (2057-2073; Ghostwalker 2061; closed via four artifacts); Nazca Lines (mana lines that flared into metaplanar rifts in 2061).

### Astral shallows

- Thin astral/physical barrier; mundanes can window-view astral (see forms, cannot touch).
- Visual/audible communication through shallow works.
- Usually temporary (hours/days).

**Game:** Awakened who can astrally perceive switch astral <-> normal vision as a **Free Action** (instead of Simple).

**Samples:** Aberdeen Sky Tower shallow ~5 km dia; Fuzhou ~2 km; Taj Mahal (great spirit + emotional domain).

### Alchera

Complex astral terrain that can appear physically. Three types:

#### Manifest alchera

Ghostly on both planes; insubstantial physically; no interaction; may loop events.

**Samples:** Sears Tower, Chicago (Feb 10, 24 hours); North Manitou Island ethereal fires (August).

#### Materialized alchera

- Dual-natured; interactable; only in unoccupied space; may have unnatural physics.
- **Caught when it appears/vanishes:** Intuition + Willpower (3) or disorientation **-2 all actions for 10 minutes**.
- Living beings shoved aside on materialize (rarely lethal); dematerialize underfoot can drop you.

**Samples:** Victoria Falls water that vanishes after descent; phantom Caribbean islands.

#### Displacement alchera

- As materialized, but **replaces** existing terrain/objects.
- Living creatures do not go with displaced terrain; moved + disoriented as above.
- Dual-natured and interactable.
- **Game:** Anchored rituals and quickened spells on original terrain **collapse**. Preparations on displaced terrain: **Potency -> 0**.

**Samples:** Denver floating mountains; Deep Lacuna tunnels (San Diego / LA).

### Background count (core rules)

Scale **-24 to +24**; **0** = normal. Negative = low mana (ebbs/voids). Positive = aspected mana (domains/warps). Use **absolute value** for magnitude of effects.

#### Dice and magic effects

1. **-Rating** dice pool to all magic-linked tests (Spellcasting, Summoning, skill tests using active adept powers such as Killing Hands or Improved Sense).
2. **Domain exception:** Tradition, person, skill group, or skill aligned/used to the domain may be exempt from the penalty.
3. Dual-natured or purely astral beings: **-Rating to all actions** (domain may exempt spirit/creature types).
4. Pre-existing active foci, sustained spells, quickened/anchored spells, and rituals: Force/effectiveness reduced by Rating. If reduced to **0 or less**: spells fizzle; wards and rituals collapse; foci deactivate.
5. Foci **cannot activate** while under the background count.
6. Anchored rituals and quickened spells that have not expired: revive at **+1 Force per hour** up to preexisting Force once clear of the count (or as count allows).
7. Preparations triggered in a background count: **Potency immediately reduced by the Rating**.
8. Adepts may use a **Simple Action** to turn a passive power on/off when BG penalties would exceed power bonuses.
9. Assensing, Astral Perception, and Astral Combat: **-Rating** to associated tests.
10. Astral tracking through an area where BG **> target's Force or Magic**: lose the astral link at that point.

#### Dangerous power (|Rating| >= 12)

Anyone magically or astrally active (dual natured, astrally perceiving, casting, active adept power, etc.) takes **(Background Count - 12) Stun Damage** each Combat Turn. Resist with **Willpower** Test.

#### Acclimated

- Ignore BG for magic use but gain **no** domain benefit.
- Rough guideline: **1 week exposure per Rating point** (BG often changes first).
- **Home Ground** (SR5 p. 74): ignore customary local BG; count only **half** of temporary-event BG.
  - Example: Barrens customary 1 ignored; riot raises to 4 -> temporary portion halved by 2 -> effective temporary contribution 2.

#### Alignment

- Mesh with domain aspect: BG Rating applies as a **positive modifier to the Limit** of aligned skill(s).
- Example: Wiccan conjures Force 4 spirit in Hag Bolette ring (Rating 3, Wiccan-aligned): Conjuring limit **7** (4 + 3).
- Switching tradition/practice to match a domain is possible but a long process.
- Some domains have no alignable aspect: acclimate only.

### Astral phenomena

#### Mana ebbs

- Reduced mana; Rating typically **-1 to -12**.
- Astral/dual forms uncomfortable but can endure (unlike voids).

**Sample:** Crater Lake, Tír Tairngire: ebb **-7**, energy drawn toward island Tesetelinsstea.

#### Mana voids

- Mana loses cohesion; Rating typically **-13 to -20**.
- Size ~500 m2 to ~4 km diameter (sometimes smaller).
- Outer space is a void; Earth voids from pollution/magic abuse (SOX ~1% of area).

**Sample:** Cattenom Void (SOX): ~6 km dia (border fluctuates; can narrow and stretch to ~10 km); Rating **-20**; centered on nuclear facility; permanent; local foveae orbit it.

#### Foveae (moving voids)

- Invisible tornadoes, meters to ~2 km diameter; astral dark vortex; physical air distortion.
- Center lacks mana: lethal to dual/astral beings.
- BG typically **-7 to -12**; height ~ Rating in km.
- Causes: contamination or concerted mana manipulation (Aztlan, Chicago UCAS, SOX).

**Game information:**

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Reagents after pass | Treat BG Rating as **weeks to recover** area (SR5 p. 317) |
| Preparations inside | Potency -1 every **1/2 hour** (not hour); if fixated, every **6 hours** (not day) |
| Duration | <= 24 hours |
| Decay | Force -1 every **2 hours**; below Force 4: stops, becomes temporary mana ebb; ebb recovers at same rate |

#### Mana warps

- Chaotic mana flares; **cannot** be aspected, aligned, or acclimated.
- Can spawn mana storms.
- Common at gaiasphere cusp into void; rare lasting Earth warps.
- Rating band cited **-16 to -20** (and positive >=16 as warp).
- Astral exposure damages as high BG; casting triggers **mana surge**.

**Sample:** Blackstone Prison (Sioux): warp ~1 km dia, Rating 16; Circles of Cleansing for staff; long-term psych effects even on mundanes.

### Blood Magic

- Blood mage in play terms: has learned select metamagics/rituals; generally once **Sacrifice** is known.
- Cannot self-teach Sacrifice: only same-tradition teacher who knows it, or a spirit that knows it.
- Aura shows blood magic (strongly negative to most assensing).
- Illegal nearly everywhere; Aztlan exception (religious).

#### Metamagic: Sacrifice

1. Inflict Physical damage with a **melee weapon that draws blood**.
2. Melee attack; if victim restrained/unable to defend: unopposed + **+4 dice**.
3. **Blood Magic Points:**
   - Non-sapient or **willing** victim (incl. self): **1 point per 3 boxes** Physical
   - Sapient critter or **unwilling** metahuman: **1 point per 1 box** Physical
   - Spirits never valid victims (even possessing a body)
4. Unused points lost after the following Combat Turn.
5. Spend points (any mix):
   - +1 Force of spell per point (may exceed 2x Magic)
   - +1 effective Magic for Drain Stun vs Physical threshold per point
   - -1 Drain per point (min 0)
   - Store in athame
   - Summon blood spirit

#### Metamagic: Cannibalize (adepts / mystic adepts)

- Generate Blood Magic Points as Sacrifice.
- Spend **3 points** -> +1 to a Physical Attribute (up to augmented max); mix attributes; **one Simple Action**.
- Duration: (Magic x initiate grade) Combat Turns; spend Karma = total attribute points raised -> duration becomes (Magic x initiate grade) **days**.

#### Metamagic: Power Bleed (adepts; prereq Cannibalize)

- As Cannibalize, but instead of 3 points -> siphon **one** critter or adept power from victim; use as own.
- Duration (Magic x initiate grade) CT; spend **3 Karma** -> days.

#### Focus: Athame

- Blood-mage exclusive; must know Sacrifice to bond.
- Stores Blood Magic Points from **Blood Rite** ritual; Free Action to spend as Sacrifice uses when casting.
- Capacity = Force.
- Decay of stored points (from initial Blood Rite): after **1 week**, **1 month**, and **3 months**, **half** of current unused points are lost each interval.

#### Blood spirits

- Summon with Sacrifice; Blood Magic Points generated = **max Force**.
- Bindable normally.
- Need Essence Drain to remain; Essence 0 -> depart native metaplane.
