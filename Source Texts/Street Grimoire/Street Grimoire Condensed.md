### Ritual magic (general)

- Tailor performance to tradition/background.
- Some rituals need initiation into an art; keywords apply.
- **Adepts:** substitute relevant skill/attribute for Ritual Spellcasting on Seal (e.g. Animal Handling for Attune Animal/Empower; Armorer/Artisan/Automotive Mechanic for Imbue Item). GM final say.
- **Learn cost:** rituals 5 Karma (SR5 p. 299) unless noted.

**Keywords:** Anchored (p. 296 SR5); Adept (adept/mystic adept only; usually no group assist); Blood (sapient death; normally not PCs); Contractual (2+ willing; forms bond); Mana (must be on mana line/domain); Material Link; Minion; Organic Link (necromancy remains; no sympathetic/symbolic sub); Spell; Spotter.

**Line of Influence (LOI):** cylinder around mana line; radius = 20 m × mana line Force.

**Fooling wards:** Flux or Astral Doppelganger: Intuition + Magic + initiate grade vs Force×2. Material link/spotter doppelganger: ward pool -4; sympathetic: -2. Fail = no cross, no alert (unlike attack).

**Combining ward traits:** +2 Force for Seal Opposed Test dice per extra ward ability; actual Force for other purposes unchanged. Combinable: Alarm, Charged, Circle of Protection*, Cleansing Circle*, Door Ward*, Masking, Obfuscating, Polarized, Trap. (*GM permission.)

**Tattoo Magic:** 5 Karma, 2 weeks. Artificing → qi/other foci as tattoos; with contractual rituals reinforce bonds on Awakened/mundane; with Quickening/Anchoring can tattoo spells. Adepts with Tattoo Magic may use Artisan for Group Bond.
