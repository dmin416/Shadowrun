## 12 - Physical Magic

**Story/hooks:** Adept public perception, sports bans, mystic-adept "backup mage" stigma, and Paths/Ways as character-concept tools for runners.

### Ways (general rules)

- An adept cannot follow two Ways at once.
- For every 2 points of Magic Rating, discount **one level** of **one** power from that Way's list by half (1 PP → 0.5; 0.5 → 0.25). Powers costing 0.25 PP cannot discount further.
- Discount applies to only one level of a multi-level power (may apply to different Increased Attribute instances). Saved PP buy other powers/levels at full cost.
- On Magic increase via Initiation, may use discount again each time Magic rises by 2.
- Retroactive Way purchase: GM discretion (*SR5* p. 103).

## 13 - The Immaterial Touch

**Story/hooks:** Summoner reputation, spirit ontology debates, free-spirit taxonomy (wardens, shadows, vanguards, animae/animi, mimics, observers), wild spirits/ghosts, and a Cheyenne lore-shop heist fiction lead-in to alchemy.

### Core spirit rules

**Astral movement:** Complex Action to native metaplane (instant). May bypass dual-natured mana barriers via native plane if conjurer is beyond barrier **or** spirit knows what lies beyond.

**Disruption:** Full Physical or Stun CM → return to native metaplane. Possession: only if vessel takes lethal overflow (*SR5* p. 170). Out for 28 days - Force (min 24 hours). Early return: metaplanar quest (p. 140); possession needs prepared vessel waiting. Disrupted still counts vs spirit limit; may release anytime.

**Vessels:** Possession (*SG* p. 197) or Inhabitation (*SG* p. 195). Dual-natured (*SR5* p. 395); one spirit per vessel. Magician's body always prepared for own spirits; projecting empty body is available vessel. Own summoned possession: magician retains awareness/commands; Channeling (*SG* p. 148) for more control.

**Spotting in vessels:** Perception + Intuition [Mental] (6 - Force). Success also clues true appearance. Active powers may act as shamanic mask: +2 Perception (GM).

### Ally spirits

**Formula:** Arcana + Logic [Mental] (Force x 5, 1 day) Ext., or metaplanar quest. Physical copy required; vessel allies: carve into prepared vessel.

**Build Karma:**

1. Force: 8 Karma x Force
2. Form: Inhabitation **or** Materialization **or** Possession. Extra Materialization forms: 2 Karma each (Complex to switch). Inhabitation needs vessel; multi-form only if true form merge.
3. Powers: start Astral Form, Banishing Resistance, Realistic Form, Sapience, Sense Link + 1 power per Force from tradition spirits. Extra tradition-appropriate: 5 Karma each. Elemental Attack / Energy Aura / Engulf: specify form.
4. Skills: start Assensing, Astral Combat, Perception, Unarmed Combat. Extra known/needed: 5 Karma. No Conjuring group; Flight only if form fits.
5. Spells: known 3 Karma; unknown 5 Karma (formulae still needed).

**Conjure:** Lodge Force ≥ ally. Summon then bind (*SR5* p. 300); Drain as summon+bind of that Force; pay formula Karma or fail. Inhabitation: immediately inhabit; conjurer may bias test; days = Force, continuous attendance.

**Abilities:** Aid Sorcery/Study for all categories. Attrs = Force (Edge = conjurer's); Inhabitation Physical per merge. Loyalty starts 6. Full Magician skills except Conjuring; no initiate; inhabited cannot project. Open-ended services; unlimited bound/unbound services incl. Resist Drain; does not count vs bound limit. Resist Drain: use ally Magic for Physical-or-not and ally resists, **or** buy 1 Drain hit for 1 unresisted Physical box on ally (abusable → hatred). Sense Link: no spell targeting through it.

**Enhance (Ritual of Change):** Modify formula (Arcana Ext. or quest); lodge ≥ Force; binding reagents; pay Karma for changes. Force increase: **16** Karma per point above initial. May new vessel without new Inhabitation Test.

**Lose ally:** Break attempt on Ritual of Change or master KO by Drain/Physical, if wronged. Force x 2 [Force] vs Binding + Magic [Mental]. Net hits: free (may become free spirit). During Ritual of Change success: ritual fails (reagents/time spent, Karma unspent); magician resists Drain = 2 x spirit hits; if KO by Drain, ally frees immediately.

**Avatars:** Powerful free spirit guides; combat vs them usually futile (metaplanes, magic loss, etc.). Plot tools, not PC assets.

### Free spirits

**Born free:** Uncontrolled spirit: Edge [Force] (3). Success: free, Edge 1 +1 per net hit. Fail: depart. Unbound -4; memorable/frequent metahuman contact +2. Bound Force 6+ often free.

**New powers:** Banishing Resistance + full Magician (no Conjuring; no project from inhabited vessel without Astral Projection). Gain Edge new powers at freedom, then +1 power per Edge increase, from: original optional powers; Astral Gateway / Aura Masking / Divining / Energy Drain (Essence) / Materialization / Possession / Realistic Form; any metamagic except Spiritual Arts (Edge as grade); or unique Free Spirit Powers.

**Services:** May perform any bound service voluntarily. Bound via formula: compelled services; does not leave when services/master die.

**True name / formula:**

| Method | Rules |
|--------|-------|
| Physical imprint | Assensing + Intuition [Astral] (3) to ID; Arcana + Logic [Mental] (Force x 5, 1 day) to translate. Aid Study OK any type. |
| Metaplanar quest | Assense plane: (2) direct / (5) signature. Quest etches name; Arcana Ext. to translate. |
| Design from scratch | Assensing + Intuition [Astral] (5) while observing; Arcana + Logic [Mental] (Force x 10, 1 day). |

Spirit always knows location of all formula copies.

**Karma:** Must be freely given (pact or formula ritual: 1 hour per Karma) unless Energy Drain (Karma). Spend as advancement; Force +1 costs new Force x 10 Karma (raises Force-based stats/powers). "Initiate" grades grant a new spirit power each, not metamagic.

**Summon free spirit:** Own formula + concentrate: spirit appears (no roll, no Drain, no services). Magic cannot stop. Possession abandons vessel; inhabited must travel fastest physical means (if summoner moves first, pull ends).
