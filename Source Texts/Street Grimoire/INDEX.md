# Street Grimoire

Core magic expansion beyond the rulebook.

## Sections
- **The Awakened World**: Magic culture and how Awakened live
- **Traditions**: Expanded magical traditions and mentor spirits
- **Spells**: Large spell lists (combat, detection, health, illusion, manipulation)
- **Rituals & Enchanting**: More rituals, alchemy, and foci options
- **Spirits**: Spirit types, services, and advanced conjuring
- **Adepts**: Extra adept powers and ways
- **Initiation & Metamagic**: Advanced Awakened development
