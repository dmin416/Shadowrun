- **Setup in Denver / Aurora Warrens**: Dark secrets among people helping the Warrens
- **Investigation & double-crosses**: Digging into abuse, cover-ups, and rival agendas
- **Run structure**: Scenes and objectives for the first Denver adventure
- **Leads into False Flag / Ripping Reality**: Hooks for the rest of the trilogy
