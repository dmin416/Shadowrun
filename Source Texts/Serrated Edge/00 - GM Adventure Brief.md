## Premise

FRFZ / Aurora Warrens. Sons of Sauron hire runners (via elf Mr. Johnson front) to steal Paladin Health Warrens Clinic (PHWC) medical records. Records expose a Human Nation soft-genocide program (sterilization / biased care / underground research) run through Paladin Health System clinics. Runners dig deeper, then must stop a bombing by SoS hardliners while Parker James offers parallel wetwork on eleven HN clinic staff.

## Scenes

### 1. The Meet
- **Src:** `03 - Scene 1 - The Meet.md`
- **Goal:** Survive Humanis ambush (SoS loyalty test); meet Mr. Johnson at Borealis, Smoky Hill; accept PHWC records job.
- **Pay:** 2,000¥ each (+250¥/net Neg hit). 500¥ each upfront (not negotiable). Soft/let HP flee: -25% pay. Execute all HP: +25% in Neg for "zeal."
- **NPCs:** Mr. Johnson (elf, blue eyes, black hair, two red rings **left** ear); fake twin (rings **right** ear); Cortex (fixer); SoS ork watchers.
- **Twist:** HP told runners meet ORC; SoS watches the fight.
- **Debug:** Lose to HP -> orks help; pay drops to 1,000¥ each, no upfront.
- **Next:** Scene 2.

### 2. The Clinic
- **Src:** `04 - Scene 2 - The Clinic.md`
- **Goal:** Steal all PHWC medical records (portable node provided).
- **Pay:** Scene 1 contract.
- **NPCs / staff web:** Drs Chanson, Montalban, Namo Bavar, Astolpho, Fierambras, Turpin, Malagig, Friend; Nurses Salomon, Mart; Dane Ogiér; Frank Ganelon (possible ally); Ramdallah Bavar (Flaming Sword); Hard Corps Security Officers (all HP).
- **Twist:** Soft-looking clinic; underground HN labs; Archer Heights approach is hateburg.

### 3. Delivery and Attack
- **Src:** `05 - Scene 3 - Delivery and Attack.md`
- **Goal:** Deliver data, get paid, meet Parker James; survive Flaming Sword / HP hit on the meet.
- **Pay:** Collect Scene 1 pay; Parker "bonus" (amount not quantified in text).

- **Next:** Scene 4.

### 4. The Doctor is In (Trouble)

### 7. Thwarting the Bombers
- **Src:** `09 - Scene 7 - Thwarting the Bombers.md`

- **Pay:** 2,000¥ each (+250¥/net Neg, cap +1,000 -> up to 3,000¥ each). Keep recovered explosives (don't get caught). Optional courier / one-way ticket out.
- **NPCs:** Ms. Johnson (ORC); Shadow Reich roster; Humanis delivery goons; original Mr. Johnson (second thoughts; can be convinced).
- **Detonator:** Frag 18P(f), AP+5, -1/m.
- **Next:** Concurrent Scene 8; then Aftermath.

### 8. Cutting Out the Cancer (optional parallel)
- **Src:** `10 - Scene 8 - Cutting Out the Cancer.md`

- **Pay:** 7,000¥ per confirmed kill; all 11 doubles total (77k -> 154k). -10,000¥ per innocent civilian killed. Cannot claim credit if bombing kills them.
- **Targets (11):** Chanson, Salomon, Montalban, Namo Bavar, Astolpho, Fierambras, Turpin, Ogiér, Mart, Malagig, Friend.
- **Next:** Aftermath.

## Aftermath pay / Karma / rep / contacts

**Src:** `11 - Aftermath.md`

### Money
- 2,000¥ apiece (+250/net Neg) Parker: PHWC break-in
- 5,000¥ group Parker: Hippocrates pickup
- 700¥ Parker per Warrens interview conducted
- 2,500¥ apiece IOND: secret lab
- 2,000¥ apiece (+250/net Neg, up to 3,000¥ total) ORC: stop bombing
- 7,000¥ Parker per confirmed dead PHWC staff; doubled if all eleven

### Karma
- +1 records from PHWC
- +1 HN info for IOND
- +1 Brackhaven info for Blake
- +1 stop bombing
- +1 kill 1-4 HN staff / +2 for 5-10 / +3 for 11
- +1 save Hippocrates
- -1 kill Humanis in Scene 1
- +1 negotiate past Nocturna
- +1 overcome barghests
- +1 interview 3+ patients
- -2 innocents killed any scene

### Reputation
- Innocent deaths: >=1 Notoriety
- Any Karma for killing HN staff: +1 Street Cred
- Fail to stop bombing: +1 Notoriety
- Useful Brackhaven info for Blake: +1 Public Awareness

### Contacts
- Successful Parker job: Parker Loyalty 1, Connection 4
- Hippocrates pickup: Hippocrates Loyalty 1, Connection 3

### Faction aftermath
- All PCs earn Human Nation as enemy (intensity scales with how hard they hit).
- SoS unhappy if bombing stopped; may stay warm if Parker's hits done.
- ORC happy if bomb stopped (esp. if Humanis looks guilty); disapproves assassinations.
- IOND / Blake: future work only if intel delivered.
