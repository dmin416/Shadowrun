# Run & Gun

Combat core book: weapons, tactics, martial arts.

## Sections
- **Fight for Your Life / What You Don't Know**: Combat advice and mindset
- **Arsenal**: Expanded melee, firearms, ammo, explosives, armor, mods
- **Tactics & Tools**: Team tactics, overwatch, PITN networks
- **Killshots and More**: Optional combat rules, called shots, qualities
- **Martial Arts**: Styles, techniques, and how they hook into fights
