# Hooder Runs

**Source:** Better Than Bad | `Source/PDF/better-than-bad-pdf.pdf` | PDF page index 165-168

HOODING RUNS FOR THE DISCERNING GAMEMASTER These pages duplicate the tables for generating a hooding run found on p. 65-67 of this book. Use them to quickly build a run from the ground up. You can always feel free to change the details to suit your table, re-roll, hand choose, or just throw out what you don't like. After all, you're the gamemaster! To use the tables, roll once for each of the tables, using the indicated number of D6s for each, adding the totals together. THE TEAM IS CONTACTED BY...


## The Pay Offered Is...

2D6 RESULT Alternative payment (gear: free or discounted restricted/forbidden ok) 3-4 No physical payment offered, extra Karma given for run 5-6 Lower than normal pay Standard pay Higher than normal pay 9-10 Alternative payment (information) Alternative payment (gear: free or discounted, not restricted/forbidden) Very unusual in type: a rare item, magical gear, etc. THE PERSON OFFERING THE JOB IS... 1D6 RESULT Honest; they are working for the people or genuinely needs help. They give the runners as much help as they can. 2-3 Mostly benign but may be hiding facts from the team due to embarrassment or minor selfish reasons. 4-5 Is not being honest; they lie or withhold information crucial to the run. They are either being coerced or have incorrect information themselves. Is actively betraying/misleading the team.

If successful, the run isn't a hooding run, but the team ends up "working for the man" on accident THE DIFFICULTY RATING OF THE JOB IS... 2D6 RESULT 2-4 Low: easy for the team to perform 5-9 Medium: moderate security; neither a challenge nor easy 10-11 High: increased security, heightened alert, or unusual security measures (run is a trap, there are paracritters, requires gear not possessed by team) Extreme: It is not likely the team will be able to complete the run. Retreat/admitting defeat/not taking run suggested THE ACTUAL JOB IS LOCATED...

2D6 RESULT 2-3 In another city in the runners' home nation 4-8 In the runners' home city 8-9 In another city not in the runners' home nation In a benign wilderness area (forest, mountain, refuge, park, etc.) In a hostile location (space, moving vehicle, tundra, underwater, underground, etc.) In an exotic location (Seelie Court, the Matrix, dragon's lair, metaplane, etc.) THIS UNFORTUNATE THING HAPPENS ON THE RUN... 2D6 RESULT The planets align and the run actually goes smoothly. 3-6 The details of the run are much different than described. 7-8 An unexpected combat happens (random security, gang intervention, rival runner team) 9-10 The person who gave the run betrays the team. Either s/he or agents actively thwart the runners or try to kill them. A personal enemy or undermined organization (gang, corp exec, gov. official) chooses now to take revenge/interfere.

A bizarre and unpredictable event occurs to a team member (runner becomes awakened, runner SURGEs, a mentor spirit claims the runner, HMHVV infection, dependent is in crisis, loss of limb)* *Use judgment when using this result. If you and your player can tell an interesting story together using an extreme event, great! You'll have another great tale to tell! If it would lead to the player feeling angry or hurt, perhaps another choice would do better. WHEN THE RUN IS FINISHED, THE PERSON WHO GAVE THE RUN...

2D6 RESULT Attempts to kill the runners (may be coerced or mind-controlled, etc.) 3-6 Gives the runners the agreed-on payment, but suggests it is a hardship (give runners extra karma if they don't take the money, or take less) 7-9 Gives the runners the agreed-on payment, and spreads the word of their success (give runners extra street cred) 10-11 Attempts to deny the runners the full payment (give runners extra karma if they don't force the issue, or settle for less) Does not answer calls or show up to complete the deal (may be coerced or in distress)
