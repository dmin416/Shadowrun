# Shadowrun Cover Scenes (SR1 through SR5)

Art-only foldout (core rulebook p.504). No rules text. Four core covers left-to-right, each with its period Shadowrun logo overlaid.

## SR1
Larry Elmore (1989). Alley firefight at dusk: elf decker jacked into a street terminal; blonde mage with shotgun and glowing spell-hand; street sam dual-wielding SMGs behind cover; corp security returning fire from behind a car; sprawl skyline beyond.

## SR2
Industrial high structure: runners in mid-action on gantries/cables over a deep shaft; heavy weapons and climbing figures; cold industrial lighting.

## SR3
Daytime alley under red laser sights: trench-coated shooter with suppressed pistol; horned troll; masked elf; dwarf with handheld deck at a wall terminal; biohazard cargo/vehicle; Japanese neon シャドウラン (Shadowrun).

## SR4
Night neon sprawl battle vs a giant glowing cyan insect spirit: ork with energy blade and pistol; elf mage casting pink/white mana; other runners and drones in the mix; megacorp neon (e.g. Shiawase, Horizon).

## SR5
This book's cover (Michael Komarck; see Credits). Not printed as a fifth panel on this foldout; logo family continues into the current edition branding.

## Adjacent endmatter (p.505, "Null sweat, chummers.")
Not a separate INDEX chapter. Extra splash strip: street firefight (dwarf shotgun, troll rotary cannon, runners behind wrecked car); Matrix/avatar melee; industrial extraction/rigger scene (Saeder-Krupp Wasseraufbereitung Aachen 22); street samurai from behind (anarchy-A vest, katana, gun, cyberarm) in neon alley.
