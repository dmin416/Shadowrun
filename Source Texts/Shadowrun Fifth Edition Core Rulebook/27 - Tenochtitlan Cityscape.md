# Tenochtitlan Cityscape

Art-only foldout (core rulebook p.503). No rules text.

Wide dusk/night panorama labeled "TENOCHTITLAN" in the sky. Dense Aztlan mega-sprawl mixing skyscrapers with glowing stepped pyramids (large lit pyramid with apex light beam mid-right; smaller pyramid farther left). Heavy air traffic: airships/blimps and many small VTOL craft. Dark mountains on the horizon. Corporate and street neon on towers (Mesoamerican sun-stone style emblem on a central building).

Setting context (elsewhere in book): Aztlan as Aztechnology's nation; Aztechnology world HQ Tenochtitlán; see Life in the Sixth World, Central America / Aztechnology.
