# Character Sheet

Official SR5 character sheet field schema (2 pages). Photocopyable form; blank rows omitted here.

Header (both pages): Character | Player | Notes

## Page 1

### Personal Data
- Name/Primary Alias
- Metatype
- Ethnicity
- Age | Sex | Height | Weight
- Street Cred | Notoriety | Public Awareness
- Karma | Total Karma | Misc

### Attributes
Primary: Body | Agility | Reaction | Strength | Willpower | Logic | Intuition | Charisma | Edge | Edge Points | Essence | Magic/Resonance
Derived: Initiative | Matrix Initiative | Astral Initiative | Composure | Judge Intentions | Memory | Lift/Carry | Movement
Limits: Physical Limit | Mental Limit | Social Limit

### Core Combat Info
- Primary Armor + Rating
- Primary Ranged Weapon: Dam | Acc | AP | Mode | RC | Ammo
- Primary Melee Weapon: Reach | Dam | Acc | AP

### Condition Monitor
Physical Damage Track: boxes = 8 + (Body / 2, round up); black out extras. Wound markers -1 through -6. Overflow.
Stun Damage Track: boxes = 8 + (Will / 2, round up); black out extras. Wound markers -1 through -4.
Wound rule: for every 3 boxes of damage on any one damage track, -1 dice pool modifier on tests; cumulative within and across damage tracks. See Wound Modifiers, p.169.

### Qualities
Columns: Quality | Notes | Type (P/N = Positive/Negative)

### Skills
Columns: Skill | RTG | Type (A/K = Active/Knowledge). Two columns of skill rows.

### IDs / Lifestyles / Currency
- Primary Lifestyle
- Nuyen
- Licenses
- Fake IDs / Related Lifestyles / Funds / Licenses

### Contacts
Columns: Name | Loyalty | Connection | Favor

## Page 2

### Melee Weapons

### Armor

- Model | Attack | Sleaze
