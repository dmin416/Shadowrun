# Berlin Cityscape

Art-only foldout (core rulebook p.502). No rules text.

Wide night panorama of Berlin sprawl. Left: rooftop ledge with a back-facing runner silhouette (long weapon/staff, blue gear accents) and a black cat; pale moon above. Mid/far: dense lit city, vertical blue light beams, aircraft silhouettes. Right: Fernsehturm (TV tower) and megacorp neon on towers. "BERLIN" appears in the sky.

Setting context (elsewhere in book): AGS / Berlin as neo-anarchist hotbed vs megacorp power; see Life in the Sixth World, Europe.
