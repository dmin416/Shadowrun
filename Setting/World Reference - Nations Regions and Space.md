# Shadowrun World Reference - Nations, Regions, and Space

**Scope:** Major and notable nations/powers worldwide, plus off-world presence (orbit, Moon, Mars). Setting year ~2075–2078.

**Power frame:** Governments still exist, but megacorporations (via extraterritoriality and the Corporate Court) are the real top tier of power everywhere. Nations remain useful for SINs, borders, local law outside corp walls, and identity.

**Primary anchors:** SR5 Core (*Life in the Sixth World*), Market Panic (corp HQs / Zurich-Orbital / space assets), Run & Gun (space asset brief), Hard Targets / Dark Terrors / Stolen Souls (Monad–CFD–DSECI), Street Lethal (grav craft), Better Than Bad (Kilimanjaro / Azania).

## NORTH AMERICA

**UCAS (United Canadian and American States):** Heir to the old USA; capital DeeCee (D.C.). Formed after Native American secession movements broke apart the old US/Canada. Hosts 2 of the Big Ten: Ares (Detroit) and NeoNET (Boston). Also home to **Seattle**, the shadowrunning capital of the world (carved out of Salish-Shidhe land, a UCAS "island" in NAN territory). President: Nadja Daviar survived a 2064 coup attempt by radical group New Revolution (killed the president + Sec. of Defense); current president is General Angela Colloton, dogged by rumors of New Revolution ties herself.

- **Boston / NEMAQZ:** Major ongoing crisis. Boston has been under total megacorporate-enforced quarantine (NEMAQZ) for over a year, publicly blamed on a "viral encephalitis epidemic" but actually caused by CFD (Cognitive Fragmentation Disorder): a nanite-based condition that reshapes cognition and (per Monad claims) may connect to AI/technomancer Resonance-realm phenomena. Deus (the AI that caused the Renraku Arcology disaster) is rumored trapped inside. Various fringe/eco/anarchist groups (New Revolution, Warpath, TerraFirst!, Alamos 20K, and others) regularly attack the quarantine wall and are wiped out. A black market exists in smuggling people in/out; getting in is easier than getting out.
- **Chicago:** Still defined by the Cermak Blast / Containment Zone history (bug spirits, Universal Brotherhood fallout). Not a nation, but a recurring North American shadow hub and hazard zone.
- **St. Louis (ARCHology):** Notable extraterritorial arcology whose ownership is disputed and pending Corporate Court decision; currently guarded by Minuteman Security.

**CAS (Confederation of American States):** South of UCAS; vibrant diversified economy despite no homegrown AAA corp. Home to **Lone Star**, the world's largest independent security corporation (still used as police-for-hire in many sprawls even after Knight Errant displaced it in Seattle). Includes major shadow cities such as Atlanta, Dallas/Fort Worth, and New Orleans (vodou / Caribbean League cultural overlap).

**Native American Nations (NAN):** A patchwork of sovereign nations that reclaimed land from the US/Canada under the Treaty of Denver; coordinated (loosely) via the Sovereign Tribal Council.

- **Pueblo Corporate Council (PCC):** Southwest, includes Los Angeles (home of Horizon). Absorbed the former **Ute Nation** (dissolved ~2067).
- **Sioux Nation:** Central plains; Cheyenne and related centers; strong military/talismongering reputation.
- **Salish-Shidhe Council (SSC):** Pacific Northwest, surrounds Seattle; holds Council Island as a diplomatic outpost inside the Metroplex. Effectively treats the Tsimshian territory as a protectorate after MCT's withdrawal left it hollowed out.
- **Tsimshian Protectorate:** Northern Pacific coast; notorious for toxic industrial scarring (reagent hunters avoid it).
- **Algonkian-Manitou Council (AMC):** Northern / Great Lakes–adjacent NAN territories.
- **Athabaskan Council:** Far north / Alaska-Yukon belt; sparse, cold, survival-focused; common source of cold-weather gear and frontier runs.
- **Trans-polar Aleut Nation (T-PA):** Arctic / Aleutian span; straddles North America and the Russian Far North in geopolitical listings.

**Tír Tairngire:** Elven nation carved from the former US Pacific Northwest / California border region (Portland as a key city); isolationist, closely tied to the Telestrian family/corporation. Not offering Monad/AI citizenship (unlike most of North America) as of the mid-2070s.

**California Free State (CFS):** Shrunken remnant of California, having lost territory to Tír Tairngire (north) and been fragmented in the south. Politically progressive by comparison: offers open citizenship to AIs, Monads, and all metasapients. Rumored to be edging toward merging with UCAS as the Seattle / Treaty of Denver situation destabilizes. San Francisco remains a named CFS hub.

**Québec:** Recovering corporate haven, French-speaking remnant of Canada.

**Kingdom of Hawaiʻi:** Independent Pacific kingdom (often filed under North America or Oceania). Metahuman-friendly relative to many Asian powers; home culture for menehune metavariants. Separate from UCAS/CAS.

**Denver (Treaty City / Front Range Free Zone):** A sprawl divided among five different nations/powers and ruled over by the great dragon **Ghostwalker**; constant intrigue, treaty renegotiations. The Treaty of Denver also governs Seattle's status, and that framework is currently unstable/collapsing. Aztechnology has been re-inserting into Denver after earlier expulsion: a standing shadow-job magnet.

**Caribbean League (Carib League):** Loose confederation of Caribbean islands plus South Florida / Miami and Borinquen (former Puerto Rico). Founded to resist full corporate takeover; in practice a pirate, smuggler, and Ghost Cartel playground. Vodou magic is a major cultural force (Cracking the Bones, Voice of Ogoun, etc.). Most megas present **except Aztechnology**. Gunderson (Miami) is a notable local AA. Bermuda/Barbados remain UK-linked and outside the League.

## CENTRAL AMERICA

**Aztlan:** Effectively synonymous with **Aztechnology**: the nation functions as a corporate subsidiary-state. Stretches from the Texas border down to Amazonia. Capital/HQ: Tenochtitlán. Recently fought and won a war against Amazonia (Az-Am War). Blood magic / Tezcatlipoca cult reputation, heavy internal security, and a longstanding feud with Amazonia and many runners.

- **Panama Canal:** Controlled directly by the Corporate Court (not any single nation).
- **Yucatán:** Politically "complicated": disputed / unclear control, recurrent guerrilla and magical unrest.

---

## SOUTH AMERICA

**Amazonia:** The only South American nation with real global weight; ruled by the ecologically-minded great dragon **Hualpa**. One of the few places corporate power takes a back seat to national/draconic authority. Recently lost a war to Aztlan, weakening Hualpa's position and inviting renewed corporate interest. Pristine rainforest remains a premier (dangerous) reagent harvest zone away from Azzie-occupied northern edges.

**Other South American states (lower global profile, still on the map):** Argentina, Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Venezuela, Paraguay, Uruguay, Bolivia (status often "unofficial"/contested in references), French Guiana. These exist as national entities and shadow markets but lack Amazonia's megaplot weight. Caracas and similar cities appear in magical-society and smuggler circuits.

**Sea Dragon:** Not a nation, but a major underwater/power player along Pacific coasts; Proteus and other aquacorps clash with merfolk and the Sea Dragon's lairs. Recurring deep-ocean adventure hook.

## ASIA

### East Asia

**Japanese Imperial State:** Highest concentration of megacorp HQs on Earth: **Mitsuhama** (Kyoto), **Renraku** (Chiba), and **Shiawase** (Osaka), recently cooperating more closely (worrying the rest of the Big Ten). Strong nationalist pride; historically low metahuman tolerance, slowly improving.

- **Neo-Tokyo:** Recurring named sprawl/enclave associated with corporate intrigue and Yakuza activity; major Asian hub alongside the Imperial cities.

**Hong Kong (Free Enterprise Enclave / Zone):** Independent business-friendly city-state, home to **Wuxing**. Extremely competitive and unforgiving: great to be winning, brutal if you lose. Kowloon Walled City remains a security nightmare.

**Fragmented China (former PRC broken into multiple states):** Not a single nation. Notable pieces include:

- **Canton Confederation**
- **Manchuria**
- **Sichuan**, **Henan**, **Kingdom of Shaanxi**, **Beijing**, **Coastal Provinces**, **Guangxi**, **Shandong**, **Yunnan**, **Gansu**, **Ningxia**, **Hainan**
- **Taiwan**, **Republic of China** (labels vary by source era)

Corporate and Triad contests define most of these; Wuxing, MCT, Renraku, and Evo all fish here.

**Republic of Korea:** Separate from Japan; tech/industrial competitor; Yakuza–Korean underworld history in Seattle and Neo-Tokyo stories.

**Mongolia:** Independent; buffer between Chinese fragments, Russia, and Yakut.

### Siberia / Russia

**Russia (fragmented):** Broken into multiple nations, but retains a corridor connecting western Russia to the Pacific coast. **Vladivostok** hosts **Evo**'s HQ, making it a top global tech center (site of Evo/Roskosmos **DSECI** deep-space mission launch framing, Dec 31 2077/2078).

**Yakut (Awakened Siberia):** Magic-heavy Siberian polity; Evo has been "sewing" influence there (Market Panic). Distinct from the Russian Republic corridor.

### South / Southeast Asia

**Indian Union:** Large parliamentary democracy (New Delhi); major population and market; Renraku and other megas maintain substantial India divisions. Naga-friendly reputation in some regions.

**Pakistan, Bangla Commonwealth, Khalistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Tibet:** Present as distinct or contested polities; Tibet especially as an Awakened / spiritual frontier.

**Singapore, Inc.:** Corporate city-state; finance and Matrix/black-market crossover (BTL studios, data work).

**Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia, Burma / Shan State / Kashin State, Malaysia, Pattani Islamic Republic:** Patchwork of nations, Islamic republics, and warlord states. Pirate and Triad routes thick through the South China Sea and Strait of Malacca.

**Philippines:** Maritime / pirate-adjacent; Wuxing shipping disasters and Renraku Oceania ops appear in corp fiction.

**Indonesian successor states (examples):** Javanese Republic, Sumatran Alliance, Dayak Council of Borneo and Sulawesi, Enlightened Kingdom of Bali and Lombok. Not one Indonesia.

## MIDDLE EAST / WEST ASIA

Entirely thin in Core's "Where to Run" snapshot; still geopolitically dense:

- **Arabian Caliphate** (Arabia): Major Islamic political bloc on the Peninsula.
- **Islamic Republic of Iran**
- **Federal Republic of Iraq**, **Syria**, **Jordan**, **Lebanon**, **Kurdish Autonomous Zone**
- **Israel** and **Palestine** (plus contested Jerusalem framing in setting materials)
- **Secular Republic of Turkey**, **East Anatolia**, **Cyprus**
- **Free City of Constantinople:** Independent city-state straddling Europe/Asia; featured heavily in *Cutting Aces* (cons, alibis, underworld tourism).
- **Transcaucasian Federation**, **Commonwealth of Turkestan**, Afghan city-states

Shadow work here skews corp resource wars, Alliance-for-Allah historical scars, and magical/religious intrigue (e.g. Riyadh formula markets).

## EUROPE

**Allied German States (AGS / ADL):** Home to **Saeder-Krupp** (Essen), the largest megacorporation in the world for much of the timeline, run personally by the dragon **Lofwyr**. Site of severe ecological disasters from corporate pollution; **Berlin** in particular is a hotbed of neo-anarchist resistance and anti-corporate activism.

- **Black Forest Troll Republic** (formerly Kingdom): Awakened meta nation carved from Bavaria/Black Forest; capital Freiburg; now a republic under Hugo von Hasslach after King Berthold's disappearance. Source of lingering German/S-K cultural resentment and meta politics.
- **SOX (Saar-Lorraine-Luxembourg Special Administrative Zone):** Irradiated / polluted quarantine zone; toxic shamans, Hard Corps contracts, classic "don't go there" adventure site (Core run hooks).
- Other AGS pieces and neighbors (Westrhine duchies, etc.) exist as local color.

**Tír na nÓg:** Elven nation covering the island of Ireland (incl. former Northern Ireland). Parallel to Tír Tairngire but Atlantic/European. Dominated behind the scenes by the **Danaan** families (Danaan mór); Path of the Wheel magical tradition; Seelie Court influence. The Veil and related astral phenomena (Aetherology) make it magically distinct. Major miss if a "world" list only has Tír Tairngire.

**United Kingdom:** Stable enough on paper; London politics include neo-anarchist groups (Panopticans / Lambeth Martyrs style Opposition). Fomori metavariant cultural revival on the British Isles creates tension with Tír na nÓg.

**France:** Functions "as normal" at the nation level, plus:

- **Brocéliande Forest:** Autonomous nation/enclave for pixies and fae-type paranormals within/adjacent to France (Mist / Brittany phenomena).
- Corsica and other local autonomies appear in European gazetteers.

**Scandinavian Union:** Denmark, Finland, Norway, Sweden under a union framework; giant metavariant politics; Maersk shipping power; many giants emigrated toward the Black Forest Troll Republic after PR misfires.

**Italian Confederation, United Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria, Spain, Portugal, Poland, Czech Republic, Slovak Republic, Hungary, Romania, Ukraine, Belarus, Baltic states, Hellas (Greece), Vatican City:** Present as functioning European states. Switzerland remains a banking/corp-law node; Brussels is a named European political hub.

**Euskal Herria:** Basque autonomy (Spain/France border region).

**The Balkans:** Ongoing seat of chaos and political confusion (Serbia, Croatia, Bosnia variants, Kosovo, Macedonia, Albania, etc.). Unstable shadow market.

**Kronstadt, Königsberg, and similar free/odd cities:** Recurring European "special status" enclaves in gazetteers.

## AFRICA

**Egypt:** Expanded significantly west and south, held in check by Algeria, Tunisia, Sheba, and the Ethiomalian Territories.

**Kingdoms of Nigeria:** Divided among competing tribal factions fighting over oil revenue (Igbo, Awori, and others appear in Street Lethal-era hooks).

**Asamando (West Africa):** The controversial "ghoul kingdom": a nation run by and for ghouls. Deeply divisive: milestone for ghoul rights to some; horrifying to others because metahumans are regularly fed to its population as policy. MCT Africa has grown ruthlessly into/around it.

**Nairobi (Kenya):** Home to the **Kilimanjaro Mass Driver**, a mass-driver launch facility for cheap orbital resupply. Locals and spirits protest continued use after promises it would shut down once the **Skyhook** space elevator was complete; Draco Foundation mediation is ineffective. Evo has extensive covert holdings and drone/grav-craft testing nearby (Tanga on the coast). MCT leans on KMD as an alternate to S-K elevator access.

**Azania:** Controls much of southern Africa; held together mostly by industrial wealth despite internal cultural fractures. Pretoria and related districts appear in Better Than Bad (corp farms, Monad-linked drone tech, Growers Union deals with Aztechnology / Evo / Universal Omnitech).

**Other African polities (named in setting maps):** Algeria, Tunisia, Libya, Morocco, Mauritania, Mali-Faso, Sudan, Ethiopia, Somalia, Liberia, Angola, Congo DMZ / Congo Tribal Lands, Gold and Ivory Coast tribal lands, Asante Nation, Baule Empire, Anyi Lands, Sekondi, Madagascar (chupacabra origin rumors), Wit Afrika (unofficial), Pan-African Federation (unofficial). Most matter locally or as corp extraction zones rather than global capitals.

## AUSTRALIA & OCEANIA

**Australian Republic:** The Outback is regularly swept by dangerous mana storms; a magnet for parazoologists hunting uncatalogued Awakened critters.

**Tasmania:** Reportedly become something like a single unified living organism, using its own plant/animal life to actively destroy metahuman construction efforts.

**New Guinea:** A flashpoint. Australia attempted to annex it in 2064; the resisting prime minister disappeared under mysterious circumstances. Functionally part of Australia since, but a growing anti-Republic resistance means ongoing politically charged shadow work.

**New Zealand:** Allied/coalition history with Australia (ANZAC framing in older timelines); still a distinct Oceania state.

**Other Pacific entities:** Corporate Republic of Fiji, Melanesian Coalition, Autonomous Realm of Kiribati, Federated States of Micronesia, Guam Freeport, Nauru Protectorate, Free Tahitian League of Polynesia, Tongan Allied Regime. Smuggler and corp logistics stepping-stones more than plot capitals. Kingdom of Hawaiʻi: see North America.

# SPACE, ORBIT, AND THE MOON

Space development is corporate-led (no unified "space agency" for humanity) and is an active frontier, primarily driven by **Saeder-Krupp** and **Evo**, with **Ares** and **Aztechnology** also heavily present. NeoNET, Horizon, Proteus, Trans-Orbital, and Starfield Botanical Engineering hold notable niches.

**Magic note:** Deep space is an astral void. Magic is heavily penalized off-Earth; spirits cannot be summoned in vacuum; projecting/assensing in empty space is soul-scarring. Large stations and the Moon are "merely" terrible voids rather than absolute nothing, which is why corps keep researching mana extension.

### Grav / logistics craft (Evo)

**Border Jockey**, **Star Ferry**, **Sky Whale**, **Hummingbird** (and related SkyGuide / GravJack tech): Evo gravity-drive craft meant to operate in atmosphere and vacuum without classical rocket fuel costs. Potentially revolutionary for cheap orbital/lunar logistics once perfected. Star Ferry is also the prototype framing for Monad transit / Project Exodus adventure hooks.

## Mars

- **Gagarin Base (Evo / Roskosmos):** Martian colony originally research-focused, later entirely populated and controlled by **Monads** (CFD-transformed humans identifying as a new collective). Aether (an AI) and the Monads ran **Project Exodus**: relocate willing Monads ("SBIL" framing in some dumps) to Gagarin, then leave the solar system via **DSECI** (Deep Space Exploration and Colonization I), a ~3,000-passenger one-way vessel funded/built covertly by Evo/Roskosmos, publicly framed with a Vladivostok New Year launch. Post-launch sources describe DSECI departure, returned hosts, and abandoned/weird Matrix presence at Gagarin.
  - Evo's diplomatic relationship with Monads/Gagarin is tense and opportunistic.
  - **Charon Station (Ares):** Mars-orbit / Deimos L1 surveillance-military post; effectively a loaded gun pointed at Gagarin during the Monad crisis; first-ever off-world corporate war risk ("Omega order" territory).
  - **Star Ferry:** Prototype craft associated with the Monad logistics story (standalone adventure seed: recovered Monads or kidnapped runners waking mid-transit).

## Overall space politics

- No single nation or corp fully "owns" space; it is an unclaimed frontier the wealthiest AAAs race to stake.
- The Corporate Court's **Omega order** (sanctioned collective corporate strike) is the restraint mechanism against any one corp gaining unchecked off-world advantage, mirroring CC conflict management on Earth.
- Off-world work is an emerging niche for shadowrunners ("spacerunners"), mostly corp-fronted/sanctioned: independent teams lack private launch capability.
- Space Rescue Service (SRS) is the named emergency/logistics actor other corps negotiate with (e.g. Nimbus debt deals; Monad launch standby in some accounts).
