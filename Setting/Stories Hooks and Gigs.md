# Shadowrun — Stories, Hooks & Gigs (Master Reference)

Every condensed sourcebook in this project was built around the same idea: strip out the fiction and JackPoint banter, keep the mechanics, and boil everything else down to **one-line pointers to story/hooks/gigs**. This file pulls every one of those pointers (plus every dedicated hook table, NPC-with-a-hook roster, and job generator) into a single place, organized book by book. Each line is a seed, not a full writeup — cross-reference the named chapter in the book's own condensed file (or the source PDF) for the full text.

**Legend:** *Story/hooks* and *Pointer* lines are one-liners describing a chapter's plot thread. Tables labeled *Hook*, *Gig*, or similar are dedicated GM-ready seeds meant to be used directly.

**Links** use paths from this file under `Setting/` (e.g. `../Source Texts/…`). Condensed masters live beside those chapters and in `Source Texts Condensed/`.

**Scope notes:** Pure rules chapters (e.g. Core Combat/Matrix/Magic) are omitted unless they carry fiction pointers. *Run Faster* has source text but no condensed file yet, so it is not listed. *Gun Heaven 3* pointers are mostly catalog marketing blurbs (kept for completeness).

## Universal Gig Generators

Two fully reusable random-job systems, good for any book, any campaign, any night you need a run on short notice.

### Core Rulebook — Random Run Generator

Roll on each table, then fill in the blanks: *"The runners go to a meet {Meet Location} for their next job. They are hired by {Employer} for {Job Type} job, targeting {MacGuffin}. The run gets complicated when {Twist}!"*

Source: [Core Rulebook Condensed — ch. 24](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/Core%20Rulebook%20Condensed.md)

### Meet Locations (1D6)
1. At a bar, club, or restaurant
2. At a warehouse, loading dock, or other underused location
3. In the barrens district or some other urban hell hole
4. In a moving vehicle
5. In a Matrix host
6. In Astral space

### Employers (2D6)
2. A secret society (Black Lodge, Human Nation)
3. A political or activist group (Humanis Policlub, Mothers of Metahumans)
4. A government or government agency
5-6. A minor corporation
7-8. A megacorporation
9. A criminal syndicate (Yakuza, Mafia)
10. A magical group (Illuminates of New Dawn)
11. A private individual
12. An exotic or mysterious being (free spirit, dragon, AI)

### Job Type (1D6)
1. A data steal
2. An assassination or demolition
3. An extraction or insertion
4. A misdirection
5. A protection
6. A delivery

### MacGuffins (1D6)
1. A key employee
2. A prototype product
3. Cutting edge technology research
4. A bioengineered life form
5. A Magical object
6. An urban building, rural location, or infrastructure object

### Twists (1D6)
1. Security is unexpectedly high
2. A third party is also interested
3. The target is not what it appears to be (group was lied to)
4. The job requires a rare piece of equipment
5. Target has been moved or is being moved
6. The employer decides to double-cross them

## Core Rulebook

### Story pointers (by chapter)

- **05 - Life in the Sixth World:** The Sixth World runs on what you pay (mind, self, soul, blood); shadowrunners need fixers, Mr. Johnson, and legwork to survive megacorps, crime, and each other. See [05 - Life in the Sixth World](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/05%20-%20Life%20in%20the%20Sixth%20World.md).
- **05 - Life in the Sixth World:** Magic returned on Dec 24, 2011 (UGE 2011, Goblinization 2021); megacorps rule via extraterritoriality and the Corporate Court; augmentations cost Essence (and Magic/Resonance for Awakened/Emerged). See [05 - Life in the Sixth World](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/05%20-%20Life%20in%20the%20Sixth%20World.md).
- **05 - Life in the Sixth World:** UCAS/Seattle is the shadowrunning capital; CAS hosts Lone Star; Aztlan is Aztechnology; Amazonia under Hualpa; Japan hosts MCT/Renraku/Shiawase; Hong Kong is Wuxing's cutthroat sprawl; S-K and Lofwyr rule Essen; Denver is five-nation treaty city under Ghostwalker. See [05 - Life in the Sixth World](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/05%20-%20Life%20in%20the%20Sixth%20World.md).
- **05 - Life in the Sixth World:** You need runner teammates, contacts (especially a fixer), and Mr. Johnson; runs follow meet, legwork, plan, action, wrap-up. See [05 - Life in the Sixth World](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/05%20-%20Life%20in%20the%20Sixth%20World.md).
- **05 - Life in the Sixth World:** Job types: datasteal, burglary, breaking things (incl. wetwork), extraction/insertion, delivery/smuggling, protection, hooding, misdirection. See [05 - Life in the Sixth World](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/05%20-%20Life%20in%20the%20Sixth%20World.md).
- **05 - Life in the Sixth World:** AAA corps (Ares #7, Aztechnology #4, Evo #6, Horizon #10, MCT #3, NeoNET #2, Renraku #5, S-K #1, Shiawase #8, Wuxing #9); organized crime (Mafia, Yakuza/MCT, Triads, Vory, Koshari); street/go-gangs; academics; mana chasers (Draco/Atlantean Foundations); politicos (Humanis, neo-anarchists); privatized law (Lone Star, Knight Errant, etc.); shadow networks (JackPoint, Denver Nexus, Black Lodge, New Revolution). See [05 - Life in the Sixth World](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/05%20-%20Life%20in%20the%20Sixth%20World.md).
- **05 - Life in the Sixth World:** Downtime covers nuyen/credsticks, Matrix entertainment, music/trideo/sports, soy-based food, commodified sex, VITAS/HMHVV/drugs/BTLs, DocWagon vs street docs, GridGuide vs manual driving, and tiered travel security (semiballistic tightest, regular air loosest). See [05 - Life in the Sixth World](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/05%20-%20Life%20in%20the%20Sixth%20World.md).
- **06 - Shadowrun Concepts:** Rules resolve actions and move the story; players and GM cooperate; characters are metatype + attributes + skills + qualities + gear. See [06 - Shadowrun Concepts](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/06%20-%20Shadowrun%20Concepts.md).
- **07 - Girls With Guns:** A dwarf runner recalls his first job (Predator from Hauser, van driver) and recruits a hungry street girl after they survive a ganger shootout at Allen's Diner. See [07 - Girls With Guns](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/07%20-%20Girls%20With%20Guns.md).
- **08 - Creating A Shadowrunner:** Build a unique runner in nine steps; default 25 starting Karma; archetypes (street samurai, covert ops, occult investigator, street shaman, combat mage, etc.) provided as samples. See [08 - Creating A Shadowrunner](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/08%20-%20Creating%20A%20Shadowrunner.md).
- **09 - Skills:** Skills turn information into action; specializations narrow expertise; Knowledge defines who you are beyond combat stats. See [09 - Skills](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/09%20-%20Skills.md).
- **10 - Where There's Smoke:** Charlie Broken Horse and Ladybug of the Smokers Club abduct Dr. Eric Leiden from a Bellevue cyber clinic to find their missing teammate Riser, fighting corp security and an Awakened troll bodyguard with a water elemental. See [10 - Where There's Smoke](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/10%20-%20Where%20There%27s%20Smoke.md).
- **12 - Rooftops and Rainbows:** Technoshaman decker Badger Four on overwatch spots enemy runners, traces their decker to a rigger-controlled Nissan Hound extract chopper, and crashes the rigger's controls to avenge fallen teammate Christopher Laughing Coyote. See [12 - Rooftops and Rainbows](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/12%20-%20Rooftops%20and%20Rainbows.md).
- **14 - The Dangers of Side Jobs:** Rigger Ellida smuggles a VITAS-recovered child in a medical pod past Disassembler go-gang ambushes for Trollface's crew, losing her Dragonfly drone but earning a bonus limo and a dinner date. See [14 - The Dangers of Side Jobs](../Source%20Texts/Shadowrun%20Fifth%20Edition%20Core%20Rulebook/14%20-%20The%20Dangers%20of%20Side%20Jobs.md).

### Experimental CFD Treatment (Phalanx / Xenosapient Strain)

**Scope:** GM discretion only. Not automatic for all head cases. Dominant PF must descend from a **xenosapient AI** (AI alienated from human form). Does not work on fully overwritten head cases. Does not work on non-xenosapient PFs (Coriolis gives false positive on non-xenosapients; dangerous).

**Select head case ability:** Some CFD nanomachines can form a Matrix device (implanted cyberdeck-like) usable by the PF when in control.

**Four-step treatment:**

1. **Convince PF to leave** (host must consent to torture)
2. **Coriolis complex form** (technomancer)
3. **Reverser nanomachine colony injection**
4. **Restore brain function** via cyberware/bioware augmentation; monitor recovery

#### Step 1: Convince the Fragment

Force the xenosapient PF to experience starvation, dehydration, intense emotions (drugs or torture). Host warned and must accept.

- Host resists stopping torture: **Willpower + Body** test; failure resets process.
- Convince PF: **Negotiation + Charisma vs. Willpower + Charisma**, once per day for **7 consecutive days**. Most daily wins decides outcome.
- Xenosapient PFs: **-2** to resist Negotiation; **-3** if physical-body exploits apply (tailored pheromones, Influence spell, Kinesics, etc.).

#### Step 2: Coriolis (New Complex Form)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Target** | Persona |
| **Duration** | E |
| **FV** | L + 3 |

**Software + Resonance [Level] (5, 24 hours)** Extended Test to eject PF. Minimum several days; advise life support for technomancer and patient. If threshold not met within **one week**, Reversers lose effect; must restart with new Reversers.

- **Glitch:** Target **-2** to all tests during treatment.
- **Critical glitch:** Interval +48 hours; hits reset to 0.
- If PF cedes control or disconnects from Matrix: process disrupted; **host and technomancer take full Fading as unresisted Stun**.

PF must access Matrix via hot-sim or nanite-formed Matrix device.

#### Step 3: Reverser Nanomachines

- Prototype only; AAA-affiliated biochem labs; high demand; cannot be replicated on standard nano-fabrication arrays (per Nephrine/KAM debate).
- Injection process: standard nanite treatment (injection, food/drink for material).
- **Only works if:** PF is genuine xenosapient, agreed to leave, and Coriolis succeeded.
- **If fragment refuses or Coriolis not used:** Reversers have no effect.
- **If treatment fails anyway:** Reversers corrupted like any nanoware injection; CFD spreads further.

#### Step 4: Recovery

Reversers evacuate AI; infected brain portions become latent (not dead). Restore function with augmentation/gene therapy (risky). One successful case: host cured of blackouts but required heavy implant load; near-fatal Essence loss if host had prior augmentation or unclean lifestyle.

**Murai Holdings:** Reverser creator; main office Boston (lockdown complicates access).
